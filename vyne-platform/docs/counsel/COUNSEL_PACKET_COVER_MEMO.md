## 1 · What VYNE is

**VYNE Strategies is a decision advisory for financial advisors making consequential business
decisions.** An advisor approaches VYNE while weighing whether to stay at their current firm, change
affiliation model, launch or join a firm, merge, sell, plan a succession, or wind down. VYNE's work is to
help them decide well. Recruiting is one outcome the methodology can support — it is not the premise.

**Three facts about the business model that shape everything in this packet:**

1. **VYNE is compensated by the hiring firm, on success.** Advisors pay nothing for traditional
   recruiting and transition-consulting services.
2. **Several legitimate outcomes pay VYNE nothing** — staying, waiting, remediating in place, or the
   advisor building their own firm. VYNE's documents state this affirmatively because the discipline is
   only credible if it is checkable.
3. **VYNE does not claim to be unbiased, neutral, conflict-free, or economically disinterested.** It
   states how it is paid and describes the controls it applies. **This is a deliberate positioning
   decision and we ask counsel to preserve it** rather than substituting conventional neutrality
   language.

## 2 · What we are asking counsel to do

**Clear a narrow set of advisor-facing instruments for production use**, so that VYNE can engage its
first real advisor. Today the governing document carries the banner *DRAFT — COUNSEL REVIEW REQUIRED
BEFORE PRODUCTION USE*, and no advisor may be onboarded until that is resolved.

**In scope:**

- the **Advisor Engagement Understanding** (the plain-English document the advisor accepts);
- the **compensation disclosure** language, used verbatim across all surfaces;
- **privacy and retention** — including setting retention periods, which VYNE has deliberately left
  blank;
- **document ownership** and the advisor's permitted use of VYNE's written work;
- **electronic acceptance** — validity, evidence, and version capture;
- **advisor withdrawal**, authorization revocation, and record retention on exit;
- a scan for **any language that could imply legal, tax, valuation, portability, or investment advice**,
  across the enclosed advisor-facing documents.

## 3 · What we are NOT asking counsel to do

**Please do not review the VYNE methodology.** The consulting method, assessment framework, decision
ontology, and internal quality controls are extensive, are not advisor-facing contracts, and are still
being tested. Reviewing them would multiply cost and delay without reducing legal risk.

Also out of scope for this packet: firm-side agreements, entity formation, trademark, state
recruiting-activity licensing, and the public website. Several of these are already registered as
separate matters (see §6) and will come to you as their own packets.

## 4 · The most useful thing counsel can tell us

**Instrument architecture.** VYNE currently has one plain-English document doing several jobs at once.
We expect the correct answer is a small family of instruments — an engagement agreement, a plain-English
companion, a privacy notice, an authorization form, and a retention policy — and we would rather be told
that now than discover it after launch.

**Specifically: does the Advisor Engagement Understanding stand alone, accompany a separate terms
document, or get incorporated by reference?** VYNE has deliberately not characterized its legal effect.

## 5 · Four business positions already settled — instructions, not questions

*Counsel is asked to implement these and confirm consistency with VYNE's firm-side agreements, not to
revisit them.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **BP-1 · Compensation on withdrawal** | The advisor owes VYNE nothing on withdrawal. VYNE's compensation rights arise **only** under separate agreements with hiring firms. **No advisor fee, no placement tail, no liquidated damages, no fee-protection obligation, and no restriction on the advisor dealing directly with a firm** may appear in any advisor-facing instrument. |
| **BP-2 · Authorization on withdrawal** | Unused firm-specific authorizations are automatically revoked on withdrawal unless the advisor confirms otherwise in writing. A completed introduction cannot be undone; no further disclosure occurs without renewed authorization. |
| **BP-3 · NDA sequencing** | No standalone NDA gates an ordinary engagement. Confidentiality controls apply from first contact. A mutual NDA is available on request or where circumstances warrant. |
| **BP-4 · Electronic acceptance** | Acceptance must record advisor identity · document version · date and time · method · delivery of a copy · acknowledgment of material later amendments. **The advisor must be demonstrably shown to have accepted that specific version.** |

**Standing constraints counsel is asked to preserve:**

- **No claim of neutrality, independence, conflict-freedom, or market completeness** anywhere.
- **No fee percentages, amounts, step-ups, payment timing, clawbacks, or firm-specific commercial terms**
  in any advisor-facing or public document. These are founder-restricted and are excluded by design.
- **No client personally identifying information** is requested, retained, or transmitted — ever, and not
  waivable by the advisor.
