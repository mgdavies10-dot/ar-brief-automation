## C · The neutrality refusal — the clause most likely to be "corrected"

| Ref | Current language | Why | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **§5** | *"We do not describe ourselves as unbiased, neutral, or free of conflicts. We are compensated in a particular way, we tell you how, and we manage it — which is a different and more honest claim."* | **This is a deliberate competitive and ethical position, not an oversight.** Competitors in this category do claim to be unbiased. VYNE has ruled it will not. | **HOLD — do not substitute conventional neutrality language.** C-32 |

> **Note to counsel.** A reviewer encountering this for the first time often reads it as a drafting error
> and proposes softening it to *"we act in your best interests"* or *"we provide objective advice."*
> **Please do not.** If it creates legal exposure, tell us what and we will solve it another way.

   vendor list yet because it has no vendors yet. **C-16.**
