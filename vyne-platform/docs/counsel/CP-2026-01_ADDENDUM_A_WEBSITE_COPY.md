## Why this addendum exists

The standing constraint recorded in `AEU_COUNSEL_REVIEW_SCHEDULE.md` cross-cutting question 4 is that
**the website must not exceed the claims made in the Advisor Engagement Understanding.** Counsel is asked
to confirm that it does not.

**This is the exact rendered copy**, extracted from the built HTML — not the source code, and not a
summary. What appears below is what a visitor would read.

## What counsel is asked to review

1. **Does any statement exceed the Advisor Engagement Understanding?** Particularly the confidentiality
   commitment, the authorization commitment, and the description of what VYNE delivers.
2. **The compensation section.** It states the arrangement, names the potential conflict, and describes
   controls. It deliberately does **not** claim that compensation "plays no part" in VYNE's judgment, and
   does **not** claim VYNE is unbiased, neutral, or conflict-free. **Is this disclosure adequate, and is
   the conflict acknowledgment correctly framed?** *(Relates to C-28 to C-32.)*
3. **The footer disclosure.** Does it adequately disclaim legal, tax, accounting, valuation, investment,
   and compliance advice, and adequately state what VYNE is not? *(Relates to C-9 and C-11.)*
4. **The advice-implication scan (C-11) applied to public copy.** Does anything here read as legal, tax,
   valuation, portability, or investment advice?
5. **"Legitimate outcomes may include…"** — deliberately framed as possibility rather than a claim that
   VYNE has already reached and recommended each outcome. **Is that framing sufficient?**
6. **Entity naming.** The footer reads `© 2026 VYNE Strategies. All rights reserved.` with **no entity
   type stated**, pending formation (`LR-2026-001`). Confirm this is the correct interim treatment.

## Specific language notes for counsel

| Item | Note |
|---|---|
| **Confidentiality** | States what VYNE controls — *"VYNE will not contact a firm about you, disclose your identity, or share your information without your explicit written authorization naming that specific firm"* — rather than an absolute guarantee against every route of disclosure. **Deliberate.** |
| **Introductory call** | *"nothing shared with a firm or outside party without your permission"* — narrowed from an earlier absolute, because the advisor is by definition sharing information with VYNE. **Deliberate.** |
| **Client data** | *"client-identifying information"*, not *"client information"* — VYNE does need aggregate information about the book, clients, products, and revenue. **Deliberate.** |
| **No neutrality claim** | The words unbiased, neutral, conflict-free, compensation-neutral, and objective appear **nowhere**. This is a standing constraint, not an oversight. **Please preserve it.** |
| **Contact address** | `hello@vynestrategies.com` is a **local placeholder**. The mailbox does not exist and will not be published. |
| **Founder section** | Built in code but **hidden**. No employer, title, tenure, credential, license, or biography fact has been inferred or published. |
| **Privacy and Terms** | Placeholder pages stating only that nothing is in effect. **No fabricated legal language.** Their real content is an output of C-18 and C-21. |

## Publication gate

**Nothing is published.** The site runs only on the founder's machine. Under EA-WEB-001 there is no
hosting, no domain, no analytics, no form submission, and no data collection. **Public launch is gated on
counsel's review of this copy, the Privacy Notice, the Terms, and the compensation language.**
