## 5 · Content and conduct constraints — carried forward unchanged

**Never:** unbiased · neutral · conflict-free · compensation-neutral · unqualified objective · the
founder-restricted fee range · calculator formulas presented as advice · controlled internal methodology
· competitor language, design, formulas or trade dress · exclamation marks · software-company positioning.

**Never requested at the top of the funnel:** CRD · exact T12 · exact AUM · documents · client
information.

**Absolute:** **no information submitted through the website triggers firm contact or submission.** Firm
contact requires separate, written, firm-specific authorization.

**Calculators:** transparent formulas · visible editable assumptions · sensitivity ranges · methodology
and effective date · directional-estimate language only · **no strategic recommendation, formal
valuation, firm ranking, portability conclusion or best-fit conclusion** · **"take-home pay" prohibited
unless taxes are actually modelled** · no hidden industry defaults. **Calculations are not publicly
activated until formulas and QA are founder-approved.**

**Proof:** **no invented numerical proof statements.** Substantiated process proof only.

**Photography:** private-advisory editorial art direction approved. **Nothing purchased, generated or
published before founder review.** Prohibited: handshakes · skyscrapers · chess pieces · compasses ·
mountains · artificial luxury offices · literal vines, leaves or foliage · generic smiling-advisor stock
· obvious AI-generated people.

**Founder section:** built into the page architecture and **not hidden**. Draft fields and a
non-public placeholder until approved biography and portrait exist. **No biography fact is inferred.**
