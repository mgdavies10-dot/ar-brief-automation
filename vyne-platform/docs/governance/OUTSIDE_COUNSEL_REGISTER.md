## Process

New escalations use the Legal Escalation Template
(`templates/LEGAL_ESCALATION_TEMPLATE.md`), receive the next `LR-YYYY-NNN` ID from the
restricted register (the founder assigns; this index mirrors ID + status only), and are
linked from any Decision Log entry they block or condition. A register row may block a
product decision (Charter Amendment 2: the council tracks exactly that one fact).
