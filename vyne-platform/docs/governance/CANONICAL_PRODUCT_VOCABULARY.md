# Canonical Product Vocabulary

**Status:** Binding for the Framework · **Provisional for The VYNE 9 question set**
**Established:** 2026-08-02 · **Owner:** Founder · **Parent:** `../00_VYNE_MASTER_INDEX.md`

> **Why this exists.** A prior session found no VYNE Framework and no VYNE 9 in the repository, and
> **invented a substitute ("Eight Questions") rather than flagging the gap.** This document fixes the
> vocabulary so that failure cannot repeat — and marks precisely which parts are settled and which are

## 1 · THE VYNE FRAMEWORK — **binding**

**Founder-approved 2026-08-02.** Use this wording. Do not paraphrase the four lines.

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **V** | **Vision** | Define the business, client experience and future the advisor wants to build. |
| **Y** | **Your Objectives** | Clarify what must improve, what must be protected and what cannot be compromised. |
| **N** | **Navigate** | Evaluate the firms, models, platforms and paths that deserve serious consideration. |
| **E** | **Enterprise Economics** | Understand compensation, ownership, enterprise value, succession and the long-term consequences of each path. |

**Public hierarchy — current working direction:**

```
VYNE
 └─ The VYNE Framework      how the decision is approached
     └─ The VYNE 9          the questions that make the decision clearer
```

**Constraints.** The acronym is the **memorable public expression** of the method — **not VYNE's entire
competitive moat**, and not the method itself. Evidence discipline, firm intelligence, economics and
human judgment sit behind it. The **six-step consulting process is a separate thing**; both may exist and
they serve different purposes. **Never substitute one for the other.**

**Implementation:** `apps/site/content/framework.ts` · `/how-we-help/framework`
