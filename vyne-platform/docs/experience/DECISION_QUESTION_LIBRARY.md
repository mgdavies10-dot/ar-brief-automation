# The Decision Question Library

**Status:** DRAFT / living inventory · **Date:** 2026-07-25 · **Phase:** 1 — Expression
**Derived from:** the Messaging Architecture, the Advisor Journey, and the foundation.

**What this is.** The **canonical inventory** of the questions an advisor actually asks
while deciding about the future of their business. If the **Messaging Architecture** is the
*structure* (how we answer), this is the *inventory* (what we answer). **Every** future
article, newsletter, AEO page, report, calculator, assessment module, consultation, AI
interaction, podcast, or webinar should **trace back to one or more questions here.** New
content that maps to no question in this library is, by definition, company-centric — and
shouldn't ship.

**What this is NOT.** Content, copy, pages, AEO keywords, or answers. It is the question set
only. *(Which question gets which answer format is decided in Content Architecture; the rule
there — content type follows the question's complexity, not the reverse — reads from this
library.)*

## How questions are tagged

Each question carries (at least) three tags today, with more to develop:

- **Stage** — where in the Advisor Journey it arises (Curiosity · Exploration · Perspective ·
  Evaluation · Decision · Implementation · Long-term · Cross-cutting).
- **Conversation** — *internal* (what am I trying to accomplish?), *external* (what is this
  firm offering me?), or *interpretive* (what actually matters?). See Messaging Architecture §2.
- **Complexity** — *Simple* (short/factual), *Moderate* (needs framing or comparison), or
  *Complex* (needs advisor-specific analysis or consultation). This is the primary driver of
  answer format in Content Architecture.

**To develop (do not invent scales yet):** advisor **type**, **channel** / business model
(employee, regional, IBD, supported independence, hybrid RIA, independent RIA, team, staying),
**concern** taxonomy, and a **confidence-relevance** dimension. Seed questions below are mostly
universal; channel-specific and persona-specific variants expand the library toward the
eventual hundreds.

*Notation below:* `(i)` internal · `(e)` external · `(int)` interpretive · complexity in
brackets.

## Curiosity

- `(i)` Is there really a better option out there for me? *[Moderate]*
- `(i)` Am I missing something by staying where I am? *[Moderate]*
- `(i)` Is now even worth exploring, or should I wait? *[Moderate]*
- `(i)` Am I being disloyal just by looking? *[Simple — emotional]*
- `(e)` What's actually changed in the market for advisors like me? *[Moderate]*
- `(e)` What are firms offering advisors right now? *[Moderate]*
- `(int)` Does my situation even warrant a serious look? *[Moderate]*

## Exploration

- `(i)` What am I really trying to accomplish — economics, ownership, autonomy, my clients,
  my team, my future? *[Complex]*
- `(i)` What kind of business do I actually want to be running in five years? *[Complex]*
- `(e)` What actually changes for my business if I move? *[Complex]*
- `(e)` What happens to my clients if I move? *[Complex]*
- `(e)` What happens to my team? *[Complex]*
- `(e)` What are my real options — employee, regional, IBD, supported independence, hybrid
  RIA, independent RIA, joining a team, or staying? *[Moderate–Complex]*
- `(e)` Am I even comparing the right things? *[Moderate]*
- `(int)` Which of my goals are actually in tension with one another? *[Complex]*

## Perspective

- `(i)` What actually applies to *my* situation — not advisors in general? *[Complex]*
- `(i)` Am I overthinking this, or underthinking it? *[Moderate]*
- `(e)` Which of these firm claims actually matter for me? *[Complex]*
- `(e)` How much of my book would realistically move? *[Complex]*
- `(e)` What am I not seeing? *[Complex]*
- `(int)` Given everything, what are my realistic options — and which deserve real work? *[Complex]*

## Evaluation / due diligence

- `(i)` Can I trust this analysis — and my own judgment here? *[Moderate]*
- `(e)` Which risks actually matter for me? *[Complex]*
- `(e)` Which of these promises should I believe? *[Complex]*
- `(e)` What should I verify before I trust an offer? *[Moderate–Complex]*
- `(e)` What don't I know yet? *[Moderate]*
- `(e)` What are the real economics after client attrition, repricing, and transition costs —
  not the headline check? *[Complex]*
- `(e)` How do transition loans and forgivable notes actually work? *[Moderate]*
- `(e)` What's the tax picture at a high level? *[Moderate — boundary: high-level only, not
  tax advice]*
- `(int)` After verification, how do my options really compare under consistent standards? *[Complex]*

- `(i)` Should I wait? *[Complex]*
- `(i)` Should I stay? *[Complex]*
- `(i)` How confident can I actually be? *[Complex]*
- `(i)` Will I regret this? *[Complex — emotional]*
- `(e)` Does the recommendation hold up under scrutiny? *[Complex]*

## Implementation / transition

- `(i)` Did I make the right call? *[Moderate — reassurance]*
- `(i)` Will my clients be okay? *[Moderate]*
- `(i)` Will my team be okay? *[Moderate]*
- `(e)` What happens next? *[Simple–Moderate]*
- `(e)` What surprises should I expect? *[Moderate]*
- `(e)` What will my clients actually experience? *[Moderate]*
- `(e)` How long until things feel normal again? *[Moderate]*

## Long-term

- `(i)` Was this the right partner? *[Simple]*
- `(i)` Do they still understand my business? *[Moderate]*

## Cross-cutting (about the process itself)

- `(i)` Can I explore this confidentially, without it getting back to my firm? *[Simple — trust]*
- `(e)` How does a consultant like this get paid — and does that bias the advice? *[Moderate — disclosure]*
- `(e)` Do I even need a consultant, or can I do this myself? *[Moderate — self-directed]*
- `(int)` When is this decision too complex or consequential to make alone? *[Moderate]*

## Research-surfaced additions (2026-07-25 — evidence-grounded)

Found in real marketplace sources (see `../market/ADVISOR_DECISION_INTELLIGENCE_RESEARCH.md`,
§4). `[E]` = evidenced with a cited source.

- `(e)` What happens to my book if I leave the *new* firm? *(exit options — most-forgotten
  question)* `[E]` *[Moderate]*
- `(e)` Can I legally take my clients — what do Reg S-P, Broker Protocol, and my non-solicit
  actually allow? Do I need affirmative consent? `[E]` *[Complex — legal boundary]*
- `(e)` How does a forgivable-loan / transition deal actually work, and how is it taxed?
  `[E]` *[Moderate]*
- `(e)` Should I take the counteroffer if my firm makes one? `[E]` *[Complex — emotional]*
- `(e)` Is the extra payout actually worth it after I run my own infrastructure? `[E]` *[Complex]*
- `(i)` Do I actually want independence — or do I just want better economics? `[E]` *[Complex]*
- `(e)` How long will this really take, and when does revenue dip? `[E]` *[Moderate]*
- `(e)` Who can I talk to who recently made this exact move? `[E]` *[Simple]*
- `(i)` Do I want to *run a business*, or just serve clients? *(the supported-independence
  fork)* `[E]` *[Complex]*

**Three tags to add to every question** (research findings — the sharpest predictors of VYNE
value): **primary decision supported** (stay · move · independence · join · partner · sell ·
succession · acquire · retire · wait — see the Wave-2 taxonomy), **AI-sufficiency** (AI alone
suffices · AI helps but can't personalize · only confidential consulting can answer), and
**personalization** (generic · advisor-specific · book-specific).

## Wave-2 additions — the broader decision set (2026-07-25, evidence-grounded)

The advisor decision is not only "change firms." Questions across adjacent primary decisions
(see `../market/ADVISOR_DECISION_INTELLIGENCE_RESEARCH_WAVE2.md`, §4):

- **Sell / valuation** `[E]`: What is my practice actually worth? · Revenue multiple or EBITDA
  multiple? · Do favorable terms beat a higher headline multiple? · What makes my book more
  valuable? *[Complex]*
- **Succession** `[E]`: Internal successor or external sale? · Does my candidate have the
  capital/skill/trust? · How long a runway (5–10 yrs)? · A minority-stake/hybrid to keep control?
  *[Complex]*
- **Acquire** `[E]`: Can I finance this (SBA/conventional/seller note)? · Do I have the cash flow?
  · How do I value the target and structure contingencies? *[Complex]*
- **Independence / supported** `[E]`: Do I want to *run a business* or *serve clients*? · What's my
  real net after infrastructure? · Fully independent vs. a platform? · Does it preserve ownership
  and enterprise value? *[Complex]*
- **Changing broker-dealer** `[E]`: How do payout, technology, PM flexibility, compliance, and
  transition resources compare? *[Moderate]*

## Governance (how the library stays canonical)

- **One inventory, everywhere.** Every content, AEO, report, consultation, and AI artifact
  maps to ≥1 question here; if it maps to none, reconsider it.
- **Grow it deliberately.** New advisor questions get *added*, not answered ad hoc; the library
  is expected to reach hundreds, organized by the tags above (stage · conversation · complexity,
  then advisor type · channel · concern · confidence).
- **Advisor-voiced.** Questions are written in the advisor's words and **do not mention VYNE** —
  that is what keeps the whole system advisor-first.
- **Feeds Content Architecture.** Content Architecture decides *which question deserves which
  answer format* (short article, long-form guide, research piece, interactive framework,
  assessment module, or consultation) — driven by each question's **complexity**, not by what
  VYNE feels like publishing.
