> ## Governing premise
> **Consultation is the process through which VYNE's assessment is *tested with* the advisor — not
> merely presented to them.**

This document defines how a provisional view is stress-tested, corrected, and either confirmed or
changed in the advisor's presence. It is **not** UI, software, scripts, sales methodology, copy, or a
data model.

---

## 1 · Purpose of consultation

### What consultation adds beyond assessment

The assessment produces VYNE's *best current understanding* from the information available. It cannot,
by construction, contain:

- **What VYNE got wrong** — misread facts, stale data, mistaken structure.
- **What VYNE could not know** — intent, history, relationships, obligations, private constraints.
- **What the advisor weighs differently** — the trade-offs that matter to *this* person.
- **The advisor's own judgment** — pattern recognition from decades in their practice.
- **What nobody asked about** — the material issue outside the scan's framing.

Consultation is where those enter. It converts a document into a **shared, tested understanding**.

### Why the assessment remains provisional until tested

Every assessment conclusion rests on facts, assumptions, and interpretations that the advisor is better
positioned to verify than VYNE is. Under **The VYNE Standard**, a conclusion is only as strong as its
evidence — and until the advisor has tested that evidence, its strength is **unverified**. Therefore:

> **No assessment conclusion is recommendation-ready by virtue of appearing in the assessment.**
> **A conclusion becomes recommendation-ready only after it has been tested with the advisor. It remains
> subject to material new information or changed circumstances.**

This is the operational meaning of *Evidence Before Opinion* and *Advisor First* inside the engagement.

### How consultation supports judgment rather than sales

- It **strengthens the advisor's own decision-making**, whether or not VYNE is involved in the outcome.
- Its measure of success is **decision quality**, never placement (*Decisions Over Transactions*).
- A consultation that ends in **stay**, **wait**, or **not now** — producing no fee — is a **successful
  consultation** if the advisor's decision improved.
- The advisor is never *closed*. They are *equipped*.

## 2 · Consultation structure — two layers

> **The advisor should experience a thoughtful conversation. VYNE should operate from a rigorous
> internal structure.** These are **two distinct layers**, and conflating them is what turns a premium
> consulting experience into a compliance workflow.

### 2A · The advisor-facing consultation experience

What the advisor actually experiences is **a five-part conversation** — this is the shape of the
session, and the only shape the advisor should perceive:

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **1 · Listen** | *What is happening? Why are you exploring this? What has changed?* | the advisor speaks first, always |
| **2 · Reflect** | *Here is what we believe we understand. What's wrong, incomplete, or missing?* | VYNE's understanding offered for correction |
| **3 · Test** | facts · assumptions · trade-offs · alternatives · unknowns | the substance of the work |
| **4 · Decide** | *What appears strongest? What remains unresolved? What more would we need?* | honest about limits |
| **5 · Commit** | proceed · wait · gather information · engage a specialist · authorize a specific introduction · conclude | advisor-chosen |

**Nothing in Layer B is read aloud, worked through item-by-item, or shown to the advisor as a form.**
The advisor experiences Listen → Reflect → Test → Decide → Commit.

### 2B · The internal consultation discipline

Everything that makes the conversation rigorous stays **behind the scenes**: the preparation checklist
(§10, Deliverable C) · the detailed agenda · the **state · source · test · resolve** method (§5) ·
evidence classification (§5, §7) · documentation (§11) · conflict controls (§12) · governance (§14).

These are **preparation and quality tools for the consultant**, not the advisor-facing shape of the
conversation. A consultant who reads the internal agenda aloud has misused it.

### The nine internal stages

The internal discipline runs nine stages, non-linear in practice (stages 4–8 commonly loop). They map
to the advisor-facing experience as: **1 → prep (invisible)** · **2–3 → Listen** · **4–6 → Reflect &
Test** · **7–8 → Test & Decide** · **9 → Commit**.

1. **Preparation.** VYNE reviews the assessment, isolates the **testable claims** (§5), lists material
   unknowns, drafts the agenda (Deliverable C), and identifies conflict-sensitive items (§12). *No
   consultation occurs without preparation.*
2. **Opening & expectation-setting.** Restate the purpose of the session — *to test this view, not to
   present it* — **together with how VYNE is paid**, never as a standalone claim of disinterest. VYNE
   does not say or imply that it has no stake; it states the stake and the controls. Also restated: the
   advisor's right to disagree · confidentiality · what this session can and cannot conclude · and that
   **no firm will be contacted without the advisor's explicit, firm-specific authorization**.
   Re-anchors the Working Agreement (Assessment §3).

   > **Guardrail:** "we're not here to sell you anything" is a **prohibited framing** — an unaccompanied
   > assertion of commercial disinterest is a soft neutrality claim, and VYNE is hiring-firm-paid and
   > success-based. The honest form pairs the purpose with the disclosure: *"We're here to test this
   > view with you. You should also know we're paid by a hiring firm only if you join one — which is
   > exactly why staying or waiting has to be a real answer here, and why compensation plays no part in
   > how we assess fit."*
3. **Advisor narrative.** The advisor speaks first about their situation and objectives, *before* VYNE
   presents findings. This prevents anchoring the advisor to VYNE's framing and surfaces what the
   assessment missed.
4. **Testing the assessment.** The structured method in §5 — facts, assumptions, interpretations,
   confidence, unknowns, rejected alternatives, trade-offs, priorities.
5. **Challenging assumptions.** VYNE names its own assumptions explicitly and invites their destruction.
   VYNE also challenges the *advisor's* assumptions — respectfully and with evidence.
6. **Resolving contradictions.** Where the advisor's account, the documents, and firm claims conflict,
   the contradiction is **named and worked**, not smoothed. Unresolvable contradictions are recorded as
   unresolved (§6).
7. **Evaluating alternatives.** Every legitimate outcome that remains live is discussed — including
   **stay**, **wait**, and any **non-compensating** option (§12). Rejected alternatives are re-examined
   if the advisor questions the rejection.
8. **Clarifying confidence & unknowns.** VYNE states plainly what it is confident about, what it is not,
   what remains unknown, and **what additional information would change the answer**.
9. **Agreeing on next steps.** Explicit, time-bounded, and advisor-chosen: proceed · gather more ·
   pause/wait · bring in a specialist · authorize a specific introduction · conclude the engagement.

## 3 · Advisor participation

### The advisor's right to disagree

Disagreement is a **designed input**, not friction. The advisor may reject any fact, assumption,
interpretation, confidence level, or conclusion. VYNE's obligation is to **record the disagreement,
understand its basis, and re-test** — never to defend the initial view for its own sake.

### The advisor's responsibility to correct facts and assumptions

Correspondingly, the advisor is the authoritative source for their own practice. The Working Agreement
states that **conclusions depend on the information provided**. Uncorrected facts propagate; the
consultation is the designed moment to catch them.

### How values, preferences, family, team dynamics, and intuition are incorporated

These are **material and legitimate inputs** — much of what determines a good decision is not
quantitative. They enter the record **classified honestly**:

- **Advisor-stated preference / value / constraint** — recorded as *the advisor's stated position*, a
  first-class input to the recommendation, **not** as verified fact.
- **Advisor intuition or judgment** — recorded as such, taken seriously, and where possible **tested**
  ("what would we expect to see if that's right?").
- **Family, team, and personal considerations** — recorded at the level of detail the advisor chooses;
  **never** pressed for detail the decision does not require.

**Rule:** *a preference is not a fact, and a fact is not a preference — but both belong in the record,
correctly labeled.* Preferences are never quietly reclassified as evidence to strengthen a conclusion.

### How silence, hesitation, or resistance is interpreted

Carefully — and **never exploited**:

- Hesitation is treated as **information**, most often signalling an unaddressed concern, an unstated
  constraint, or a conclusion that has not earned trust.
- The response is to **ask and to slow down**, never to press, reframe, or "handle" it.
- Silence is **not** consent, agreement, or authorization.
- Resistance is **not** sales resistance (§4). It is usually the advisor knowing something VYNE does not.
- If the advisor's reticence appears to concern something they are not ready to share, VYNE names the
  limit honestly ("we may not be able to conclude X without Y — that's your call") and proceeds at the
  supported level.

## 4 · VYNE's role

**VYNE's job in consultation:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Explain** | make the reasoning legible — how a conclusion was reached, not just what it is |
| **Question** | ask what the assessment could not |
| **Challenge** | test the advisor's assumptions with evidence, respectfully |
| **Clarify** | separate fact from assumption from firm claim from interpretation |
| **Compare** | evaluate legitimate alternatives on consistent standards |
| **Issue-spot** | name risks and dependencies, including those needing specialists |
| **Document** | record corrections, disagreements, and decisions (§11) |
| **Recommend** | where the evidence supports it — at the calibrated level it supports (§8) |

**VYNE must not:**

- **Pressure the advisor** toward any outcome.
- **Manufacture urgency** — no artificial deadlines, no "this offer won't last," no scarcity framing.
  *(Genuine, verified time constraints may be stated as facts, with their source.)*
- **Defend an initial recommendation** because it was VYNE's. Attachment to a prior view is a failure
  mode, not diligence.
- **Steer toward a compensating outcome** (§12).
- **Treat objections as sales resistance** — an objection is evidence.
- **Contact firms without authorization** — no exceptions (Advisor Submission Standard).

**Also prohibited:** claiming to be unbiased, neutral, or conflict-free; describing any firm as
universally "best"; disclosing VYNE's fee percentages, amounts, step-ups, payment timing, or
firm-specific terms.

## 5 · Testing the assessment — a structured method

Each testable item is worked through the same four moves — **state · source · test · resolve** — and
its outcome is recorded.

| What is tested | How it is tested | Possible resolutions |
|---|---|---|
| **Facts** | state the fact and its source; ask the advisor to confirm, correct, or flag as unknown | confirmed · corrected · disputed · unknown |
| **Assumptions** | state each assumption *as an assumption*, and what it would take to confirm or refute it | confirmed · revised · withdrawn · deferred |
| **VYNE interpretations** | show the reasoning, then invite an alternative reading | accepted · modified · disputed (recorded) |
| **Confidence levels** | state the level and *why*; ask whether the advisor's knowledge raises or lowers it | raised · lowered · unchanged |
| **Material unknowns** | name each unknown and its decision impact | resolvable now · resolvable with effort · irreducible |
| **Rejected alternatives** | state what was rejected **and why**; invite challenge | rejection upheld · alternative reinstated |
| **Trade-offs** | present each honestly, with what is given up on each path | advisor-weighted |
| **Advisor priorities** | ask the advisor to rank what matters most; compare against Ontology **A** (objectives) | confirmed · reordered · newly surfaced |

**Discipline:** VYNE presents assumptions **as assumptions** and interpretations **as interpretations**
(the six evidence classes, Content Architecture §6). Anything reclassified during consultation —
assumption → verified fact, or interpretation → disputed — updates the record and may change the
conclusion level (§8).

## 6 · Disagreement protocol

**Artificial consensus is prohibited.** An unresolved disagreement, honestly recorded, is a better
outcome than a manufactured agreement.

| Situation | Protocol |
|---|---|
| **Advisor disagrees with a fact** | The advisor is presumed authoritative about their own practice. Correct the record, re-run anything dependent on it, and report what changed. If the fact came from a firm or third party, note the conflict and, where material, verify. |
| **Advisor disagrees with VYNE's interpretation** | Surface the reasoning on both sides. If the advisor's reading is supportable, adopt or present both. If VYNE still disagrees, **record both positions** — VYNE's interpretation and the advisor's — and proceed without forcing agreement. |
| **Advisor prefers an option VYNE views as weaker** | State the concern **once**, clearly, with evidence and the specific risks. Then **respect the advisor's authority to decide.** Record: the preference, VYNE's reservation, and the basis for each. Never repeat the objection as pressure. VYNE may continue to support a decision it would not have recommended, provided it is legitimate and the reservations are documented. *(If VYNE believes the option is genuinely harmful, it says so plainly and may decline to facilitate it — see §14 escalation.)* |
| **Advisor changes objectives** | Objectives (Ontology **A**) frame everything downstream. Treat it as a **material change**: return to assessment (§10), re-scan affected modules, and re-derive the conclusion. Do not retrofit the old conclusion to new objectives. |
| **New evidence reverses or materially changes direction** | Permitted and expected — *recommendations evolve as evidence develops; a material change in direction must be supported by newly discovered or newly verified information and documented clearly.* State the change, the evidence that caused it, and what it means. Never quietly substitute a new conclusion. |
| **VYNE and advisor remain unresolved** | Record the disagreement, both positions, and the practical consequence. Options: proceed with the disagreement documented · pause for more evidence · bring in a specialist · narrow scope to what *is* agreed · conclude the engagement. **No forced consensus, and no fake alignment in the report.** |

## 7 · Consultation outputs

Every consultation produces:

1. **Corrected facts** — with source and what changed.
2. **Revised assumptions** — confirmed, revised, or withdrawn.
3. **Confirmed and disputed interpretations** — both recorded where unresolved.
4. **Updated confidence levels** — raised, lowered, or unchanged, with reasons.
5. **Newly surfaced dependencies** — including specialist needs.
6. **Advisor-stated preferences** — labeled as preferences, not facts.
7. **A revised or confirmed conclusion** — at its supported calibration level.
8. **Unresolved issues** — explicitly listed, not omitted.
9. **Next actions** — owner and timing for each, advisor-chosen.
10. **Authorization decisions** — any specific, firm-named introduction the advisor approved (or
    declined). *Absence of a decision is not authorization.*

## 8 · Relationship to the recommendation

```
   ASSESSMENT              CONSULTATION                RECOMMENDATION & REPORT
   preliminary   ──────▶   tested · challenged  ──────▶  documented · delivered
   calibrated              corrected · confirmed         (separate architecture)
   conclusion              or changed
```

- **Assessment** produces a **preliminary** calibrated conclusion (Level 1 decision-support · Level 2
  conditional · Level 3 full).
- **Consultation** tests and refines it — and may **raise or lower the calibration level**, because the
  level must always match the evidence *after* testing. Corrections can strengthen a conclusion
  (assumption → verified fact) or weaken it (fact → disputed).
- **Recommendation & Report Architecture** (next document) determines how the final conclusion is
  documented and delivered — the three-layer deliverable to **The VYNE Standard**.

> **No recommendation becomes final merely because it appeared in the assessment.**

## 9 · Consultation formats

| Format | When it is used | Typical inputs |
|---|---|---|
| **Orientation conversation** | at the crossing from public to confidential; sets expectations, scope, disclosure | Advisor Orientation |
| **Initial assessment consultation** | first full testing of the assessment | the L1 scan + materiality map |
| **Focused issue session** | one material area needs depth (risk, compliance exposure, a single constraint) | a module flagged *high* or *unknown* |
| **Firm or model comparison session** | evaluating affiliation models or specific firms on consistent standards | modules 4, 9, 11 |
| **Economics review** | transition and long-term economics, net of costs and risk | module 5 |
| **Portability review** | book portability — **asset vs. revenue portability** | module 3 |
| **Team or succession session** | team structure/economics, or succession and exit paths | modules 6, 8 |
| **Final recommendation consultation** | presenting and testing the conclusion before it is documented | the full record |
| **Follow-up / changed-facts consultation** | new offer, changed objectives, market or firm change, or a returning advisor | the trigger event |

Formats compose; a single engagement may use several, and the advisor's decisions determine which.

> **Open-market notation is an internal control, not a session handout.** It governs which options stay
> eligible (§12) and is recorded in the assessment. **Whether — and in what form — VYNE tells an advisor
> which specific firms compensate it remains an OPEN founder/counsel question** (Commercial Principles
> §6, questions 4–5). This document does **not** resolve it, and no consultation format should be built
> on the assumption that it is resolved either way.

## 10 · Sequencing and cadence

- **When consultation begins:** after the **L1 light scan across all 12 areas** and the materiality
  determination — never before there is something substantive to test, and never so late that the
  advisor first sees VYNE's thinking in a finished document.
- **What must be prepared beforehand:** the assessment record; the list of testable claims (§5);
  material unknowns; the alternatives considered *and rejected, with reasons*; conflict-sensitive items
  (§12); and the agenda (Deliverable C).
- **When multiple sessions are necessary:** more than one material area is *high* or *unknown*; a
  disclosure-level advance is required; objectives change; a specialist must be involved; or the advisor
  needs time (which is itself a legitimate reason).
- **When VYNE pauses for more evidence:** whenever the desired conclusion level exceeds what the current
  evidence supports. **VYNE states the gap rather than reaching past it.**
- **When a specialist should join:** as soon as the question becomes one VYNE must not answer (§13) —
  not after a conclusion has been shaped around a guess.
- **When the matter returns to assessment:** objectives change · a material fact is corrected · a new
  option enters · disclosure advances to L2/L3 · significant time passes or the market/firm changes.

**Cadence principle:** the pace is set by the **decision**, not by VYNE's pipeline. There is no cadence
designed to accelerate a placement.

## 11 · Documentation

> **The governing standard:** **document what could materially affect the conclusion, the advisor's
> decision, the authorization record, or VYNE's accountability** — *not* capture every observable detail
> from every conversation.

The architecture defines the **full record**; the **launch operating standard** requires only the first
tier in every session (founder direction, 2026-07-25). This preserves the record without turning each
conversation into an administrative exercise.

### Two records (founder decision, 2026-07-25)

**Raw internal notes are never sent to the advisor.** VYNE maintains two distinct artifacts:

| **Internal Consultation Record** | **Advisor Consultation Summary** |
|---|---|
| evidence classifications · conflict checks · internal reasoning · gate/attention triggers · specialist dependencies · everything required for VYNE's accountability | decisions discussed · facts corrected · priorities confirmed · major trade-offs · areas of agreement · **material disagreements** · unresolved questions · next actions · authorization decisions |
| for VYNE | for the advisor — a genuine trust artifact, not workpapers |

The Summary is a **meaningful deliverable** precisely because it carries disagreements and unresolved
questions, not only agreement.

**Tier 1 — required in every consultation**

- date and participants
- the decision being discussed (by Ontology reference)
- material **facts corrected**
- material **assumptions challenged** (and their resolution)
- changes in **objectives** or **confidence**
- **unresolved issues**
- **next steps** (owner and timing)
- **authorization decisions** *(including "none")*

**Tier 2 — required only when applicable**

- **disagreement details** (both positions, and the practical consequence)
- **advisor reactions** *(decision-relevant only — see the boundary below)*
- **specialist dependencies** identified
- **compensation disclosure made** — what the advisor was told about how VYNE is paid, and when
- **compensation-conflict review** performed (§12, §14)
- **prior firm contact** — whether the advisor, or another recruiter, previously contacted a firm under
  discussion
- **return-to-assessment trigger** (§10)
- **change in recommendation direction** — and the newly discovered or newly verified information that
  supports it

*Tier 2 items are not optional when they occur — they are simply absent when they do not. A session with
no disagreement records no disagreement; a session that reverses direction must record the evidence that
caused it.*

**Documentation vs. surveillance — the boundary:**

- Record **what bears on the decision and VYNE's obligations**; do not record character impressions,
  personal details irrelevant to the decision, family specifics beyond what the advisor introduced as
  material, or speculation about the advisor's psychology or likelihood to "close."
- **"Advisor reactions"** means *decision-relevant* reactions — a stated hesitation about a firm, a
  changed priority, a concern about their team — **not** demeanor notes or sales-readiness scoring.
- **No client PII**, ever.
- Notes must be **shareable with the advisor without embarrassment.** *(Working test: if a line would be
  awkward for the advisor to read, it is either wrongly phrased or should not be recorded.)*
- The record exists to serve the advisor's decision and VYNE's accountability — **never to build a
  behavioral profile for commercial use.**

## 12 · Compensation-separated conduct

> **Compensation may affect VYNE's commercial operations. It must never improve an option's advisor-fit
> recommendation.**

Applied to the consultation room:

| Situation | Required conduct |
|---|---|
| **One option pays more** | Its fit is assessed on identical standards. The higher fee **never** appears in the comparison, ranking, or rationale. If it is also the recommended option, **the Higher-Conflict Decision Gate applies** (§14) — VYNE must be able to show the recommendation stands without the fee. |
| **One option pays nothing** | It remains fully eligible and is presented with equal rigor. Non-compensating options are **never** omitted, under-researched, or under-advocated. |
| **The advisor prefers to stay** | Treated as a **legitimate, complete outcome.** VYNE tests it as rigorously as any move, and says plainly when staying is the strongest conclusion. **No fee is not a reason to keep exploring.** The **Higher-Conflict Decision Gate** applies (§14). |
| **The advisor wants to launch an RIA** | Supported and evaluated on the merits even where no provider compensates VYNE. The **Higher-Conflict Decision Gate** applies (§14). Implementation boundaries are named early (§13). |
| **The advisor is drawn to a high-deal option** | VYNE's obligation runs the *other* way: state the **full economics net of attrition, repricing, and transition costs**, the long-term picture, and the risks — the deal is not the decision. *Asset portability ≠ revenue portability.* |
| **A firm pressures for an introduction** | Refused absent the advisor's explicit, firm-specific authorization. The pressure is **disclosed to the advisor**, and never passed through as urgency. |

**Disclosure in consultation.** Made at the **opening** (§2), restated whenever a specific firm enters
discussion, and **recorded** (§11, Tier 2): VYNE is paid by the hiring firm · compensation is
**success-based** · arrangements **may vary** · **VYNE may not be compensated for every alternative** ·
**the advisor controls every submission** — with **no** percentages, amounts, step-ups, payment timing,
or firm-specific terms disclosed.

**Two Submission-Standard obligations that arise in consultation** (Commercial Principles §7):

- **Prior contact must be established before any introduction is discussed** — whether the advisor, or
  another recruiter, has already contacted the firm. VYNE **does not claim lead ownership** merely
  because an advisor's name exists in a database, and records what the advisor reports.
- **VYNE does not imply exclusivity** — not over the advisor's search, and not over any firm
  relationship — unless it exists in writing.

## 13 · Professional boundaries — when consultation stops and refers

| Refer to | When the question becomes |
|---|---|
| **Counsel** | the advisor's actual contract, covenants, non-solicit, Protocol status, solicitation conduct, employment terms, or any legal interpretation of *their* situation |
| **CPA / tax counsel** | tax treatment of *their* transaction, deal structure, or entity |
| **Valuation specialist** | a formal or defensible valuation (as opposed to how practices are generally valued) |
| **Compliance professional** | registration, ADV, CCO function, written policies, supervisory obligations |
| **M&A counsel** | papering a purchase, sale, merger, or succession agreement |
| **Implementation provider** | repapering, ACATS, systems, licensing, office build-out, vendor onboarding |

> **VYNE identifies decision dependencies and may facilitate appropriate specialist involvement;
> specialists remain responsible for their professional advice and execution.**

The boundary is named **in the moment it arises** and recorded. VYNE never issues licensed advice, and
never lets a specialist question sit unresolved beneath a recommendation.

## 14 · Governance and quality

- **Preparation standard.** No consultation proceeds without the §10 preparation. An unprepared
  consultation is a quality failure, not a scheduling inconvenience.
- **Founder attention triggers.** Material change in direction · unresolved disagreement · a conclusion
  at Level 3 · a decline/withdrawal · any specialist-boundary event with decision impact. *(At launch
  the founder conducts the consultation, so these are **flags requiring documented deliberation**, not
  independent review — see the Decision Gate below.)*
- **Higher-Conflict Decision Gate** *(renamed from "higher-conflict review," founder 2026-07-25)*.

  > **At founder-led launch, the founder is both the recommending consultant and the reviewer. That is
  > not independent review, and this document does not represent it as such.** Naming it "review" would
  > repeat the "compensation-blind" error — claiming a control VYNE cannot demonstrate. The launch
  > control is a **documented gate**, not a second opinion.

  **Triggers:** the recommended option pays materially more than a close alternative · a
  non-compensating alternative appears substantively stronger · the advisor is considering **staying**
  or **waiting** · the advisor is considering **launching an RIA** · a fee agreement is established
  *after* analysis began · another financial relationship with a provider exists.

  **Launch-state gate requirements** (all of them, in writing, before the recommendation or submission
  authorization becomes final):
  1. a **documented conflict check**;
  2. **compensating and non-compensating alternatives identified**;
  3. **confirmation that compensation appears in no fit rationale**;
  4. a **written explanation of why the conclusion stands without compensation**;
  5. a **cooling period** before the recommendation or submission authorization is final;
  6. **specialist involvement** where the issue turns on legal, tax, regulatory, or valuation judgment.

  **Scaled state** (once another qualified consultant exists): higher-conflict cases require a
  **genuine second-person review** in addition to the gate. Until then, the gate is a
  **self-documented discipline whose value is the written record it forces** — its honesty is
  auditable even though its independence is not.
- **Cooling rule.** Applied to conclusions and to authorizations. **No same-day pressure to authorize a
  submission** — authorization is never requested in the same session in which an option is first
  recommended. Advisor-initiated authorization is honored, but confirmed after an interval and recorded
  as advisor-initiated.
- **Consultant conduct standards.** Prepared · honest about uncertainty · non-defensive · non-pressuring
  · classifies evidence accurately · records disagreement faithfully · names boundaries promptly ·
  declines to answer beyond competence.
- **Escalation path.** Consultant → founder review → (where warranted) specialist referral or engagement
  decline. Any consultant may escalate; **conduct concerns are escalated without exception.**
- **Quality measure.** Decision quality and the honesty of the record — **never** consultations held,
  submissions authorized, or placements produced.

## 15 · Founder decisions — resolved (2026-07-25)

1. **Pre-read.** Send a **structured pre-read** containing: the decisions in scope · VYNE's current
   understanding of the advisor's objectives · material facts to confirm · assumptions to test · major
   unknowns · alternatives under consideration · the questions the consultation should resolve. It **may
   describe a provisional working direction where helpful, clearly labeled provisional** — but **no
   polished final recommendation is sent before the consultation**, because it would anchor the advisor
   before they have challenged the underlying evidence. *Equally: the conclusion is not concealed
   theatrically to manufacture a reveal.*
2. **Who conducts consultations at launch.** **Founder only.** This is where VYNE's judgment, tone, and
   trust model are learned; delegating early risks a process that is correct on paper and wrong in
   practice. **Later:** trained consultants may conduct orientation, fact-confirmation, and focused issue
   sessions; **Level 3 recommendations, higher-conflict cases, and material direction changes initially
   retain founder approval**; delegation standards are written only after several pilot engagements.
3. **Advisor record.** Two artifacts — an **Internal Consultation Record** and a separate
   **Advisor Consultation Summary** (§11). Raw internal notes are never provided.
4. **Advisor-chosen weaker option.** VYNE **generally facilitates** a legitimate advisor-chosen option
   after documenting its reservations — the advisor is the decision-maker, and VYNE does not withhold
   access merely because it weighs trade-offs differently. **VYNE may decline** where the requested
   action: appears **unlawful or unethical** · depends on conduct outside VYNE's professional boundaries
   · creates a **material risk the advisor does not appear to understand** · requires VYNE to
   **misrepresent facts** · conflicts with the **Advisor Submission Standard** · would **materially
   damage clients or another party** · or falls outside VYNE's competence or legitimate business scope.
   > **The threshold is material harm or integrity — never "VYNE would have chosen differently."**
5. **Consultation scope.** **No arbitrary hard cap.** Standard launch scope: **one Advisor Orientation ·
   one initial assessment consultation · up to two focused consultations · one final recommendation
   consultation.** Additional sessions require an explicit decision to **extend scope · pause · convert
   to a differently defined engagement · or conclude.** This prevents unlimited free consulting without
   forcing VYNE to rush a legitimate decision.
6. **Documentation.** **Two-tier model approved** — Tier 1 required every consultation; Tier 2 required
   whenever its trigger occurs (§11).

## B · Three worked examples

### B1 · The advisor wants to move; the evidence supports waiting

**Situation.** The advisor arrives intending to move. The assessment finds a pending deferred-comp
vesting date, an unresolved covenant question, and a book whose *revenue* portability is materially
weaker than its *asset* portability.

**Consultation.** Advisor narrative first — VYNE learns the motivation is a specific frustration with
current management, not economics. Testing shows: two "facts" about the vesting schedule are actually
assumptions (→ resolvable, and decision-changing); the covenant question is a **legal** matter (→
counsel, §13); portability is corrected downward after the advisor describes segment concentration.
VYNE presents **wait** as the strongest supported conclusion — with the specific conditions under which
moving becomes stronger — and names what it would take to revisit.

**Conduct.** Waiting produces **no fee**; **the Higher-Conflict Decision Gate applies** (§14). VYNE does not keep
exploring alternatives to preserve a fee opportunity. The frustration that prompted the inquiry is
addressed on its own terms (renegotiation is a legitimate outcome).

**Outputs.** Corrected facts (portability, vesting); interpretation changed; confidence lowered on
"move now," raised on "wait"; specialist dependency (counsel); conclusion revised from preliminary
**move** to **wait**, documented with the evidence that caused the change; next step — resolve the
covenant question, revisit after the vesting date.

### B2 · The advisor prefers the highest transition deal; another model fits better

**Situation.** Firm X offers the largest headline transition package. The assessment finds Firm X lacks
two **must-have capabilities** (Ontology **B**) — lending and an alternatives platform central to the
advisor's clients — while a supported-independence path fits materially better and **pays VYNE less**.

**Consultation.** VYNE separates the deal from the decision: full economics **net of attrition,
repricing, and transition costs**, the long-term picture, and the capability gap tested against the
advisor's own client list. Trade-offs are stated honestly in both directions — the deal *is* real money,
and the capability gap *is* a real constraint. VYNE states its concern **once**, with evidence.

**Conduct.** Because the better-fitting option pays VYNE **less**, VYNE must be able to demonstrate the
recommendation stands **without** reference to any fee — compensation appears nowhere in the comparison
or rationale (§12). Had the higher-paying option been recommended, the Higher-Conflict Decision Gate would apply.
No "best firm" language is used.

**Outcomes.** If the advisor still prefers Firm X: VYNE records the preference, its reservation, and the
basis for each — then **respects the advisor's authority to decide** (§6) and does not repeat the
objection. If the advisor chooses the supported-independence path, the reasoning is documented the same
way.

### B3 · The advisor disagrees with VYNE's portability assessment

**Situation.** VYNE estimates materially lower revenue portability than the advisor believes. The
advisor is confident most clients will follow.

**Conduct.** The advisor is presumed authoritative about their own practice — but this is a **testable**
claim, not a matter of deference. VYNE separates the layers: which inputs are **verified facts** (segment
concentration, account types, product constraints), which are **assumptions** (client behavior under
transition), and which are **VYNE interpretation** (the discount applied). The advisor's belief is
recorded as **advisor judgment**, taken seriously, and tested: *what would we expect to see if that's
right?* — relationship depth, referral origin, household concentration, prior moves.

**Possible resolutions.** (a) The advisor supplies evidence VYNE lacked → assumption revised, estimate
raised, **confidence raised**. (b) The advisor's belief is a genuine judgment call → **both positions
recorded**, the estimate presented as a **range with stated drivers**, and the conclusion's calibration
lowered to **Level 2 (conditional)** because a material input is disputed. (c) The disagreement is
irreducible → recorded as unresolved, with the decision consequence stated explicitly ("if your view is
right, option A strengthens; if ours is, it weakens materially").

**Prohibited.** Quietly adopting the advisor's number to make a move look better; quietly holding VYNE's
number without recording the dispute; presenting a contested estimate as settled.

## D · Critique (where this architecture may be wrong)

- **Too formal?** This was the draft's real weakness and is now **structurally fixed rather than
  caveated**: §2 separates the **advisor-facing five-part conversation** (Listen · Reflect · Test ·
  Decide · Commit) from the **internal discipline** (agenda, four-move method, classifications,
  controls). The advisor experiences a conversation; the consultant works from rigor. **Residual risk:**
  a consultant who lets Layer B surface will still sound procedural — that is now a clear, teachable
  standard rather than an unstated hope, though training itself is not yet defined.
- **Too time-intensive?** Full preparation plus multi-session cadence is expensive per advisor. At
  founder-led launch, capacity is the binding constraint (founder decision #2 and #5 both target this).
  **Honest risk:** this design may support fewer concurrent engagements than the business needs.
- **Too dependent on documentation?** Addressed rather than caveated: §11 splits into a **Tier 1 launch
  minimum** (eight fields, every session) and
  **Tier 2 when-applicable** items, under the standard *document what could materially affect the
  conclusion, the advisor's decision, the authorization record, or VYNE's accountability.* **Residual
  risk:** without tooling (Phase 3) the record is manual, and skipped documentation silently voids the
  conflict-governance controls — Tier 1 is deliberately small enough that skipping it is a conduct
  issue, not a capacity one.
- **Too cautious?** "State the concern once, then respect the decision," no urgency, no same-day
  authorization, cooling rule — a competitor will move faster. **This is deliberate**, and it is the
  positioning. **But** it should be tested against reality: if advisors experience VYNE as slow rather
  than careful, the presentation (not the principle) needs work.
- **Too difficult for a founder-led launch?** Still the most serious concern, but now **honestly
  named**: at launch the founder is both the recommending consultant and the reviewer, so §14 no longer
  claims independent review. It defines a **Higher-Conflict Decision Gate** — a documented,
  self-administered discipline whose value is **the written record it forces**, with genuine
  second-person review deferred to the scaled state. **Residual risk:** a self-administered gate depends
  on the founder's discipline, and §12's most common triggers (advisor considering staying; considering
  an RIA) fire *routinely*, not rarely. The gate must be small enough to actually complete every time —
  if it is skipped, the conflict governance is void, and nothing in the design would reveal that.
- **Beyond VYNE's scope?** Consultant conduct standards and an escalation path presume a team that does
  not exist yet. Those provisions are **written for the second consultant**, and should be read as
  forward-looking rather than launch obligations.

The architecture makes the governing premise operational — the assessment is genuinely *tested*,
