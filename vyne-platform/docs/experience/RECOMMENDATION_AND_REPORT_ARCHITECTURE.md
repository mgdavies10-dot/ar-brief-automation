> ## Governing premise
> **The deliverable is not merely a report. It is an advisor-facing decision document and a governed
> record of VYNE's professional judgment at a defined point in time.**

The advisor receives a **polished, premium report**. The architecture exists to keep it from becoming a
static marketing document or a decorative presentation — and to keep VYNE's internal workpapers *behind*
it rather than inside it.

**Two distinct layers, as with Consultation:**

| **The advisor-facing deliverable** | **The internal supporting record** |
|---|---|
| clear · concise · premium · decision-oriented · understandable without methodology training | evidence classification · provenance · conflict controls · gate completion · detailed assumptions · governance history · material internal reasoning |

> **Raw internal workpapers are never exposed merely because they support the recommendation.**

## 1 · Purpose of the deliverable

**What the advisor receives:** a decision document that states what VYNE concludes (or cannot yet
conclude), why, at what confidence, what would change it, and what the advisor's realistic options are —
readable in one sitting, with depth available beneath.

**Why it exists:** consultation produces a tested view held in conversation. The deliverable makes that
view **durable, reviewable, and usable** — something the advisor can re-read, sit with, show a spouse or
partner, and return to in six months.

**How it differs from adjacent artifacts:**

| Not a… | Because |
|---|---|
| **Assessment** | the assessment is VYNE's *preliminary, untested* analysis; this is the tested conclusion |
| **Consultation Summary** | the Summary records *what happened in a session*; this records *what VYNE concludes* |
| **Pitch book** | it is **not promotional and is not designed to sell a firm, model, or transaction. It may recommend a direction when the evidence supports one** — including **stay** or **wait**, and frequently will |
| **Firm comparison** | comparison is one *layer*, not the document; the decision is the document |
| **Legal opinion** | it identifies legal dependencies and routes them to counsel; it never resolves them |

**Why it is judgment at a defined moment, not permanent truth:** every conclusion rests on information
current through a stated date and on conditions that can change. The document says so on its face —
*"Our perspective as of [Month Year], based on the information identified in this document."*

## 2 · The deliverable family

Deliberately small. Six artifact *types*, but only **one core document**, which flexes by conclusion
level rather than multiplying into separate products.

| Artifact | What it is | Required at launch? |
|---|---|---|
| **Advisor Consultation Summary** | per-session: decisions discussed, facts corrected, priorities confirmed, trade-offs, agreements, **material disagreements**, unresolved questions, next actions, authorizations (Consultation §11) | **Yes** |
| **Recommendation & Decision Record** | **the core document** — one modular artifact that renders at **Level 1 (decision-support)**, **Level 2 (conditional)**, or **Level 3 (full)** | **Yes** |
| **Changed-facts update** | an amendment or reissue of the Record when material information changes (§15) | **Yes** (as a mode of the Record, not a separate product) |
| **Firm/model comparison appendix** | Layer 2 detail when specific firms or models are in scope | **Only when applicable** |
| **Firm-specific authorization artifact** | the advisor's explicit, firm-named authorization — **separate from the Record** (§13) | **Yes** |
| **Decision Status and Closure Record** | concise; issued when an engagement ends **without** a decision (§19D) | **Yes** (a mode of the Record) |
| **Internal Higher-Conflict Decision Gate record** | internal only; never advisor-facing (§6) | **Yes**, internal |

**"Preliminary decision-support," "conditional recommendation," and "full recommendation" are not three
documents** — they are the **three calibration states of one Record** (§4). A **final engagement
record** is likewise not a new artifact: it is the last issued Record plus its Summaries, marked
*engagement concluded*.

## 3 · The three-layer structure (The VYNE Standard)

### Layer 1 — Executive Decision Narrative *(advisor-facing; page one)*

The decision being evaluated · VYNE's **recommendation or supported direction** (or the explicit absence
of one) · **conclusion level** · **confidence** · principal reasons · **material drawbacks** · **what
could change the conclusion** · the immediate next step.

Written as professional correspondence — plain, direct, and in the second person. No jargon, no scores,
no apparatus.

### Layer 2 — Decision Comparison *(advisor-facing)*

Legitimate alternatives considered — **including stay and wait** — evaluated on **consistent dimensions**
applied identically to every option: capabilities · affiliation and operating model · economics ·
portability · client impact · team impact · enterprise value · risks · implementation implications ·
unresolved issues.

**Consistency is the integrity control:** an option is never assessed on dimensions the others escaped.

### Layer 3 — Decision Basis and Supporting Evidence *(advisor-facing)*

What the conclusion rests on, **in plain language**: what is verified · what a firm claims (attributed
and dated) · what the market shows · what VYNE assumed · what VYNE judged · unknowns ·
**alternatives rejected and why** · specialist dependencies · **material advisor/VYNE disagreements**.

> **Naming discipline (founder, 2026-07-25).** "**Decision Basis and Supporting Evidence**" is the
> advisor-facing third layer. "**Internal Supporting Record**" is reserved **exclusively** for VYNE's
> internal workpapers — evidence classifications, conflict controls, provenance chains, governance
> history, gate record, and internal reasoning. **The two are never called by the same name, and Layer 3
> is never mistaken for the internal record.**

### What belongs where

| Element | Advisor-facing | Internal only |
|---|---|---|
| Conclusion, confidence, reasons, drawbacks, next step | ✓ | |
| Comparison on consistent dimensions | ✓ | |
| Assumptions the conclusion depends on | ✓ *(stated plainly)* | detailed derivation |
| Unknowns and what would change the conclusion | ✓ | |
| Alternatives rejected **and why** | ✓ *(summary)* | full reasoning |
| Material disagreements (advisor vs. VYNE) | ✓ **always** | full basis |
| Specialist dependencies | ✓ | |
| Provenance / sourcing | ✓ *(for firm and market claims)* | complete chain |
| Evidence-class labels as taxonomy | *(meaning conveyed in plain language)* | ✓ formal classification |
| Gate record, conflict checks, internal reasoning | | ✓ |

**Rule:** the advisor-facing document conveys **the substance of every evidence class in plain
language** — what is verified, what a firm claims, what VYNE assumes, what VYNE judges — **without
exposing the taxonomy as apparatus.**

## 4 · Calibration by conclusion level

The Record must **read** differently at each level. Formatting and confident prose must never make a
Level 1 look like a Level 3.

| | **Level 1 — Decision-Support** | **Level 2 — Conditional** | **Level 3 — Full** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **States** | issues, options, information gaps | a **directional** conclusion | an **advisor-specific recommendation** |
| **Contains** | no directional recommendation | explicit **conditions to confirm**, and **consequences if they fail** | rationale · drawbacks · alternatives · unknowns · confidence · changed-facts triggers |
| **Page-one line** | *"We are not yet in a position to recommend a direction. Here is what would put us there."* | *"Subject to the conditions below, the stronger direction appears to be X."* | *"Our recommendation is X, for these reasons, with these drawbacks."* |
| **Prohibited** | any language implying a direction | stating the conclusion without its conditions | implied certainty; omitted drawbacks |

**Anti-overstatement controls:**

- The **conclusion level appears on page one**, in words, not as a badge or score.
- **Conditions are never relegated to an appendix** — a Level 2 conclusion that hides its conditions has
  become a Level 3 by presentation.
- **Confidence is expressed in language**, tied to evidence and disclosure level — never as a number or
  percentage.
- A **higher disclosure level does not raise the conclusion level by itself**; evidence does.

## 5 · The recommendation-ready standard

> A conclusion becomes **recommendation-ready** only after **all** of the following:
>
> 1. **consultation testing is complete**;
> 2. **material corrections are incorporated**;
> 3. **unresolved disagreement is represented** (not resolved away);
> 4. **required specialist dependencies are identified**;
> 5. **the supported calibration level is confirmed**;
> 6. **applicable Higher-Conflict Decision Gate requirements are completed** (§6).
>
> **It remains subject to material new information or changed circumstances.**

"Recommendation-ready" is a **status of the Record**, not a quality judgment — and it is the
precondition for issuing the Record as final and for supporting any submission authorization.

## 6 · Higher-Conflict Decision Gate — lifecycle control

*This is the downstream control that makes the Consultation Architecture's self-administered gate
**visible**. Without it, a skipped gate would leave no trace.*

> ### All gate fields are INTERNAL (founder, 2026-07-25)
> The Higher-Conflict Decision Gate is an **internal governance control**, not an advisor-facing
> feature. The advisor-facing Record carries **the approved compensation disclosure and nothing more**
> — it never displays trigger status, gate status, completion date, basis-without-compensation,
> cooling-period status, or second-person-review status. **The completed internal gate permits or blocks
> issuance; the Record does not describe the control.**

**Every Recommendation & Decision Record carries these fields in its internal record**, as a status that
governs the Record's lifecycle:

| Field | Values |
|---|---|
| **Conflict-trigger status** | Not triggered / **Triggered** |
| **Gate status** | Not required / **Pending** / **Completed** |
| **Gate completion date** | date, or blank |
| **Basis for conclusion without compensation** | written explanation (required when triggered) |
| **Compensation absent from fit rationale** | confirmed / not confirmed |
| **Required cooling period completed** | Yes / No |
| **Second-person review** | Not available (launch) / Pending / Completed — *once a qualified second reviewer exists* |

> ### Binding rule
> **A recommendation cannot become recommendation-ready, be issued to the advisor as a final
> recommendation, or support a submission authorization while a required Higher-Conflict Decision Gate
> remains pending.**

**Honesty constraint:** at founder-led launch the founder is both the recommending consultant and the
gate administrator. **This is auditable discipline, not independent review, and the record must never
describe it as independent.** The `Second-person review` field reads **"Not available (launch)"** —
which is itself the honest disclosure. When a qualified second reviewer exists, that field becomes a
real control and the scaled state applies.

**Why the fields matter more than the intention:** a gate with no status field is a good intention; a
gate whose *pending* status blocks issuance is a control. These fields later become CRM status
requirements — **but they exist first as methodology**, and they bind whether or not software exists.

## 7 · Representing advisor disagreement

**The deliverable must not manufacture consensus.** Four things are named separately and never blended:

| | |
|---|---|
| **VYNE's recommendation** | what the evidence supports, in VYNE's professional judgment |
| **The advisor's decision** | what the advisor has decided (or has not yet decided) |
| **The advisor's stated preference** | what the advisor wants — a legitimate input, **not** evidence |
| **Unresolved disputes** | factual or judgment disagreements that remain open |

**How each is recorded:**

- **Corrected facts** — stated, with what changed and what it affected. Corrections are visible, not
  silently absorbed.
- **Disputed interpretations** — **both readings**, each with its basis, and the decision consequence of
  each.
- **Advisor prefers a different path** — recorded as the advisor's preference *and* VYNE's reservation,
  each attributed. VYNE's concern is stated **once**, in full, without repetition.
- **Unresolved portability assumptions** — presented as a **range with stated drivers**, and the
  conclusion's calibration lowered accordingly (a disputed material input caps the level).
- **Material disagreement over trade-offs** — the advisor's weighting and VYNE's weighting shown side by
  side; weighting is a legitimate difference, not an error.
- **A choice VYNE would not have recommended** — recorded plainly: *"The advisor has decided X. Our
  recommendation was Y, for the reasons stated. Our reservations are recorded here."* No editorializing,
  no repetition, no implied disapproval.

> **A Record that shows no disagreement in an engagement that had one is defective.**

## 8 · Readability and advisor usability

The Record must be readable by an advisor **without methodology training**, usable in an actual
decision, concise at the executive level, deep where the advisor wants depth, visually hierarchical, and
free of internal terminology.

**Page one contains, and only contains:**
- the decision being evaluated;
- the conclusion (or the explicit absence of one) and its **level**, in words;
- confidence, in language;
- the principal reasons (few, and substantive);
- the **material drawbacks**;
- what could change the conclusion;
- the immediate next step.

**Moves to later sections or appendices:** the full comparison, detailed assumptions, sourcing and
dates, rejected alternatives in full, specialist-dependency detail, methodology explanation.

**Never shown as a score without context:** portability, confidence, fit, readiness, enterprise value —
none appear as a bare number, index, gauge, or grade. Where a quantity is genuinely useful it is a
**range with its drivers and its uncertainty stated beside it**.

**Caveats stay visible without overwhelming:** conditions and drawbacks appear **on page one with the
conclusion**, in the same voice and type hierarchy — not in a footnote, not in smaller text, not at the
back. A caveat the advisor can miss is a caveat VYNE did not really give.

**Internal terminology that does not appear advisor-facing:** "Layer 1/2/3," "Level 1/2/3" as bare
labels, "evidence class," "materiality determination," "L1/L2/L3 disclosure," "gate," "PTA module."
*(The concepts appear; the vocabulary does not.)*

*(Visual template and final copy are deliberately out of scope here.)*

## 9 · Firm comparisons

- **Specific firms may appear** when the advisor's decision has narrowed to real, identified options and
  the advisor is evaluating them. **Only affiliation models or paths appear** when the decision is still
  at the model level — naming firms early anchors the advisor prematurely.
- **Firm claims are attributed and dated**, never presented as verified fact: *"Firm X states…
  (source, date)."* Verified items are distinguished from claims.
- **Consistent comparison dimensions** apply to every option in the set, including **stay** and any
  **non-compensating** option.
- **Missing information is shown as missing** — an empty cell reads *"not established"* or *"not
  disclosed by the firm,"* never as a neutral or favorable value, and never quietly omitted.
- **No "best firm" language.** VYNE may identify the **strongest fit the evidence supports for this
  advisor**; it never describes a firm as universally best.
- **Firms not considered:** the Record states plainly which options were evaluated and that **others
  exist that were not evaluated**, with the reason (out of scope, advisor's exclusion, no information).
  **VYNE never implies it reviewed the entire market**, and never implies exclusivity.

## 10 · Economics presentation

- **Transition economics and long-term economics are presented together.** A transition package shown
  without its long-term counterpart is a misrepresentation by omission.
- **Retention/attrition assumptions, repricing, and expenses are stated explicitly** as assumptions, with
  what they are based on — never buried inside a net figure.
- **Ownership implications and enterprise-value effects** are shown where the option affects them.
- **Ranges, not point estimates**, wherever the underlying evidence is a range. A point estimate is used
  only where the input is verified.
- **Scenarios** (rather than a single projection) where outcomes hinge on a small number of uncertain
  drivers; each scenario names its driver.
- **Uncertainty is stated beside the number**, not in a general disclaimer.
- **The load-bearing principle appears wherever it applies:** *asset portability ≠ revenue portability.*

> **VYNE's fee percentages, amounts, step-ups, payment timing, clawback economics, and firm-specific
> commercial terms never appear in any advisor-facing material** (Commercial Principles §10).

## 11 · Compensation disclosure

**Only the approved advisor-facing disclosure is used.** VYNE is compensated by the hiring firm upon a
successful placement; advisors do not pay VYNE for traditional recruiting and transition-consulting
services; arrangements may vary; **VYNE may not be compensated for every alternative**; **the advisor
controls every submission and introduction.**

- **Where it appears — exactly three places:**
  1. **once** in a clear standing disclosure section of the Record;
  2. **during consultation**, when a new specific firm enters discussion;
  3. **again** in the separate firm-specific authorization artifact.
- **Not repeated beside each firm in a comparison** — that clutters the report and, worse, could imply
  VYNE is disclosing *firm-specific* compensation, which remains unresolved.
- **Internal gate record vs. advisor-facing disclosure:** the **gate record** (§6) contains the conflict
  analysis, alternatives notation, and the basis-without-compensation explanation — **internal only**.
  The **advisor-facing disclosure** contains none of that analysis; it states how VYNE is paid and that
  the advisor controls introductions.
- **Never appears:** percentages, amounts, step-ups, payment timing, clawback terms, firm-specific
  commercial terms — or any claim of being unbiased, neutral, conflict-free, or independently reviewed.

> **Deliberately unresolved:** whether — and in what form — VYNE identifies which specific firms
> compensate it remains an **open founder/counsel question** (Commercial Principles §6, questions 4–5).
> **This document does not resolve it**, and no template should presume either answer.

## 12 · Professional boundaries

The Record **identifies issues and their decision consequences**; it **never replaces specialist
advice**.

| Dependency | The Record may | The Record may not |
|---|---|---|
| **Legal** (covenants, solicitation, Protocol, employment terms) | identify the issue and its decision consequence | interpret the advisor's contract or opine on enforceability |
| **Tax** | note that tax treatment materially affects the outcome | state the advisor's tax result |
| **Regulatory / compliance** (registration, ADV, CCO) | identify what will be required | prepare filings or act as compliance |
| **Formal valuation** | present valuation *methods and ranges* | issue a formal or defensible valuation |
| **M&A** | describe structures and their implications | paper a transaction |
| **Implementation** | describe the choices and their consequences | perform repapering, ACATS, systems, or build-out |

Each dependency appears in the Record with **what it affects** and **why it must be resolved by a
specialist** — never as boilerplate.

> **VYNE identifies decision dependencies and may facilitate appropriate specialist involvement;
> specialists remain responsible for their professional advice and execution.**

## 13 · Recommendation versus authorization

**The Record may recommend a direction. It authorizes nothing.**

It does **not** authorize: contacting a firm · submitting the advisor · sharing the advisor's identity ·
sharing confidential information · beginning implementation.

**Firm-specific authorization is a separate, explicit, advisor-controlled artifact** naming the specific
firm(s), the information to be shared, and the date — consistent with the Advisor Submission Standard
(Commercial Principles §7). **Accepting, acknowledging, or agreeing with a recommendation is not
authorization.** Silence is not authorization. A prior authorization does not extend to a new firm.

**No same-day pressure:** authorization is never requested in the same session in which an option is
first recommended (Consultation §14).

## 14 · Authorship, approval, and lifecycle

Every Record carries:

**prepared by** · **consultation completed with** *(and when)* · **approved by** · **date** ·
**information current through** · **disclosure level** · **conclusion level** · **version** ·
**superseded status** · **changed-facts trigger** · archive/replacement pointer.

> **On "consultation completed with"** *(renamed from "tested with," founder 2026-07-25 — the old
> wording could imply the advisor **agreed**)*: **the advisor's participation confirms that the
> recommendation was discussed and tested. It does not mean the advisor agreed with VYNE's
> conclusion.** This sentence appears in the Record itself, not only in this architecture.

**The standing framing on every Record:**

> *"Our perspective as of [Month Year], based on the information identified in this document."*

Never a version number in the advisor's view; never language implying permanent certainty.

**Lifecycle:** Draft → In review → Approved → Issued → (Amended / Reissued / Superseded / Withdrawn).
The **cooling rule** applies before approval (no same-day approval; an override requires a logged
reason), **founder approval** is required at launch, and the **gate status must be Completed or Not
required** before issuance (§6).

**Archive and replacement:** superseded Records are **archived with a pointer to their replacement**,
never deleted or silently overwritten. The history of VYNE's thinking is part of the record.

## 15 · Changes after issuance

| Change type | What it means | How it is handled |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor correction** | typographical or non-substantive | corrected in place; noted |
| **Refinement** | added detail; conclusion and direction unchanged | amendment note; no reissue |
| **Material amendment** | a material input changes; direction holds | amendment stating what changed and its effect |
| **Reissued recommendation** | **the direction changes** | a new Record that **identifies the newly discovered, newly verified, or materially changed information that caused the change**, and supersedes the prior one |
| **Withdrawal** | the conclusion can no longer be supported | Record withdrawn with a stated reason; nothing is left standing that VYNE no longer supports |
| **Return to assessment** | objectives changed · a material fact corrected · a new option entered · disclosure advanced · time passed | re-scan affected modules; re-derive |
| **Return to consultation** | the change needs testing with the advisor before it can be concluded | new session; new Summary |

> **A material direction change must identify the newly discovered, newly verified, or materially changed
> information that caused it.** A reversal is permitted; an **unexplained** reversal is not.

## 16 · Handoffs — the paths after the Record

**stay · wait · negotiate internally · gather more evidence · specialist review · explore affiliation
models · evaluate specific firms · authorize a firm-specific introduction · launch · join · merge ·
acquire · sell · succession · retire or wind down · conclude the engagement.**

Every path is legitimate and equally supported by the Record's structure. Several produce **no fee** for
VYNE, and the Record treats them identically to those that do.

**How VYNE stays a decision consultant and does not become the transition vendor:** VYNE's role after
the Record is to help the advisor **decide and evaluate** — including evaluating implementation options
and specialists. **Execution belongs to the advisor and their chosen providers.** VYNE does not perform
repapering, systems setup, registration filing, or build-out, and does not represent itself as
accountable for their delivery. *(Whether VYNE ever offers hands-on implementation remains an open
operating-model question — not resolved here.)*

## 17 · Launch operating standard

Lean, and no more:

1. **Advisor Consultation Summary** (per session).
2. **One modular Recommendation & Decision Record**, capable of Level 1, Level 2, or Level 3
   conclusions.
3. **Firm/model comparison appendix** — only when applicable.
4. **Specialist-dependency section** — only when applicable.
5. **Separate firm-specific authorization artifact.**
6. **Internal Higher-Conflict Decision Gate record.**

**Not built at launch:** separate document types per conclusion level · a visual template system ·
scored dashboards or indices · a client portal · automated generation · a firm database or directory ·
any advisor-facing document containing methodology explanation as a feature.

## 18 · Governance and quality

- **No client PII**, ever.
- **No undisclosed assumptions** — an assumption the conclusion depends on is stated where the advisor
  will see it.
- **No unsupported precision** — no figure more exact than its evidence.
- **No "best firm" language.**
- **No compensation-driven rationale** — compensation appears in no fit assessment, comparison, ranking,
  or reason.
- **No recommendation-ready status while a required gate is pending** (§6).
- **No same-day pressure for submission authorization.**
- **The advisor's decision remains distinct from VYNE's recommendation** — always separately labeled.
- **Factual corrections and superseded versions remain traceable.**
- **No claim of neutrality, unbiasedness, conflict-freedom, or independent review.**

## 19 · Founder decisions — resolved (2026-07-25)

**A · A written Record in every substantive engagement — scaled to the supported conclusion level.**
Every advisor who completes substantive assessment work receives a Record, **including when VYNE cannot
recommend a direction.** The document **scales**:
- **Decision-Support Record (L1)** — concise: the decision, unknowns, options, and the evidence required
  next. *May be only a few pages.*
- **Conditional Record (L2)** — direction plus explicit conditions.
- **Full Recommendation Record (L3)** — complete narrative, comparison, decision basis, drawbacks, and
  handoffs.
> **The requirement is a durable conclusion — not maximum length.**

**B · Ownership and sharing — VYNE retains the methodology; the advisor receives a confidential-use
right.** *(Provisional business rule — counsel must finalize the language in the Working Agreement and
document terms. This is not the final legal policy.)*
- **VYNE retains ownership** of its methodology, templates, frameworks, analysis structure, and internal
  records.
- **The advisor receives a copy of the advisor-facing Record** for personal decision-making.
- **May share confidentially** with: spouse or partner · attorney · CPA or tax professional · relevant
  team members · another named professional adviser.
- **May not** be publicly posted, reproduced commercially, distributed broadly, or **sent to hiring
  firms** without VYNE's written consent.
- **When information must reach a firm**, VYNE prepares an **approved excerpt or separate firm-facing
  summary** — never automatic sharing of the full Record.

**C · Delivery — live first, then left with the advisor.** Delivered during a **final recommendation
consultation**, with the advisor's copy provided immediately afterward. **The advisor should never first
encounter VYNE's recommendation alone in an email.** In that session: walk through page one · explain the
principal reasoning · **confirm the disagreements are represented correctly** · answer questions ·
explain the next step · **remind the advisor that the Record authorizes nothing.** Because the
recommendation was already tested in consultation, this is a clear delivery and decision discussion —
**not a theatrical reveal.**

**D · No-decision engagements — issue a concise Decision Status and Closure Record.** It states: the
decision evaluated · what VYNE could and could not conclude · material unknowns · unresolved
disagreements · why the engagement is ending or pausing · **what circumstances should cause the advisor
to revisit the decision** · whether any authorization remains active (**normally none**).
> **"No decision" is a legitimate status and does not erase the work completed.**

**E · Retention and destruction — no number is set here.**
> **Records will be retained and destroyed under a written retention policy approved before production
> use. Legal holds, active disputes, contractual obligations, and applicable law override ordinary
> destruction schedules.**

Counsel determines: separate periods for advisor-facing Records vs. internal workpapers · email and CRM
retention · authorization records · declined engagements · deletion requests · backups · legal holds.
**No arbitrary period is frozen into the methodology.**

## B · Three worked examples

### B1 · Level 1 — insufficient information for a directional recommendation

**Situation.** The advisor engaged at L1 disclosure, exploring whether to make a change. Two material
areas came back **unknown**: the enforceability posture of a non-solicit, and the revenue concentration
of the top of the book. The advisor is not yet willing to share segment detail.

**The Record.** Page one states plainly: *"We are not in a position to recommend a direction, and we do
not think it would be responsible to imply one."* It then gives what the work **did** produce — the
decision actually in play, the options genuinely available, the two questions that dominate the outcome,
what answering each would require, and what VYNE could conclude at each level of additional information.

**Calibration discipline.** No directional language anywhere; no ranking; no "leaning." The absence of a
recommendation is the honest headline, not an apology buried at the end.

**Gate.** Not triggered (no option recommended). Field reads *Not required.*

**Value.** The advisor leaves knowing exactly which two unknowns govern their decision — which is more
than a confident-sounding recommendation built on air would have given them.

### B2 · Level 2 — wait now; move later if stated conditions are met

**Situation.** Consultation established that moving is plausible but a deferred-compensation vesting
date and an unresolved covenant question dominate near-term economics.

**The Record.** Page one: *"Subject to the conditions below, the stronger direction appears to be to wait
rather than move now."* Immediately beneath — **not in an appendix** — the conditions: (1) counsel
confirms the covenant position; (2) the vesting date passes or its forfeiture is quantified and accepted;
(3) revenue portability holds within the stated range once segment detail is available. Each condition
carries **the consequence if it fails**.

**Layer 2** compares **stay · wait · move now · move after vesting** on identical dimensions.
**Layer 3** carries the portability range with its drivers, the assumptions, the covenant question as a
**legal dependency routed to counsel**, and the unknowns.

**Gate.** *Triggered* — the advisor is considering **waiting**, a no-fee outcome. Gate completed before
issuance: conflict check documented; compensating and non-compensating alternatives identified;
compensation confirmed absent from every fit rationale; written basis recorded — *the conclusion rests on
vesting economics and an unresolved legal question, neither of which involves VYNE's compensation*;
cooling period completed. `Second-person review: Not available (launch).`

### B3 · Level 3 — supported independence recommended; the advisor chooses differently

**Situation.** VYNE recommends a supported-independence path on capability and enterprise-value grounds.
The advisor chooses a wirehouse offer with a larger transition package.

**The Record.** VYNE's recommendation is stated in full, once, with rationale, drawbacks, and the
alternatives considered. Then, **separately and without editorializing**: *"The advisor has decided to
proceed with Firm X. Our recommendation was the supported-independence path, for the reasons stated
above. Our reservations — the capability gap in lending and alternatives, and the enterprise-value
implications of the employee model — are recorded here."*

**Four things stay distinct** (§7): VYNE's recommendation · the advisor's decision · the advisor's stated
preference (near-term liquidity, explicitly weighted higher by the advisor) · the unresolved
disagreement (whether the capability gap is material to *this* client base — VYNE says yes, the advisor
says no, both positions recorded).

**Gate.** **Not required unless another defined trigger applies.** The internal record may note that the
recommendation was made against VYNE's apparent financial interest, **but that fact does not enter or
strengthen the advisor-fit rationale.**

> **Why not a trigger:** the gate exists for situations where VYNE's economics could reasonably
> *distort* a recommendation. Recommending an option that pays VYNE **less** is the opposite case — it
> is evidence the separation held, not a conflict needing review. **Expanding the gate to every
> compensation difference would dilute the trigger that matters.** *(If a separate trigger applies here
> — e.g. the advisor were also weighing **staying** — the gate applies on that basis, not this one.)*

**Authorization.** The Record does **not** authorize contacting Firm X. A **separate firm-specific
authorization** is executed, not same-day.

## C · Report skeleton — section order and page one

```
════════ PAGE ONE — the whole decision, readable in one sitting ════════
  Header:  Advisor · the decision being evaluated
           "Our perspective as of [Month Year], based on the information
            identified in this document."
  1. What we conclude          ── or: what we cannot yet conclude, and why
                                  (conclusion LEVEL stated in words)
  2. How confident we are      ── in language, tied to evidence; never a number
  3. Why                       ── principal reasons, few and substantive
  4. What gives us pause       ── MATERIAL DRAWBACKS, same voice and type size
  5. Conditions                ── Level 2 only; on page one, never an appendix
  6. What would change this
  7. What we suggest next      ── the immediate next step (never an authorization)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
  8. The options we compared           (Layer 2 — incl. stay & wait, consistent
                                        dimensions, missing data shown as missing)
  9. What we assumed                   (plainly stated; not buried)
 10. What we don't know                (unknowns and their decision impact)
 11. What we considered and set aside  (rejected alternatives, and why)
 12. Where we disagree                 (advisor vs. VYNE — both positions)
 13. What needs a specialist           (legal · tax · compliance · valuation · M&A ·
                                        implementation — with what each affects)
 14. How VYNE is paid                  (approved disclosure only)
 15. Record details                    (prepared by · consultation completed with ·
                                        approved by ·
                                        information current through · disclosure &
                                        conclusion level · version · supersedes)
 ── Appendices ── firm/model comparison detail · sourcing and dates · methodology
                  note (brief, optional, never the document's center of gravity)
```

## D · Artifact comparison

| | **Internal supporting record** | **Advisor-facing Record** | **Advisor Consultation Summary** | **Firm-specific authorization** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Audience** | VYNE only | the advisor | the advisor | the advisor (+ the named firm) |
| **Answers** | how VYNE got here and whether governance held | *what should I do, and why* | *what happened in that session* | *may VYNE contact this firm* |
| **Scope** | full engagement | the decision | one session | one or more **named** firms |
| **Contains** | evidence classes, provenance, gate record, internal reasoning, governance history | conclusion, comparison, assumptions, unknowns, disagreements, dependencies, disclosure | decisions discussed, facts corrected, priorities, trade-offs, agreements, **disagreements**, unresolved questions, next actions, authorizations | firm(s) named, information to be shared, date, advisor's explicit approval |
| **Cadence** | continuous | at recommendation-ready | per session | per authorization event |
| **Gate fields** | **full record (§6) — internal only** | **none** *(the gate is never advisor-facing; it governs issuance from behind)* | — | gate must be Completed/Not required **before** authorization is sought |
| **Never contains** | — | fee terms, internal workpapers, "best firm," neutrality claims | internal reasoning | implied or blanket authority |

## E · Critique (where this architecture may be wrong)

- **Excessive length?** A real risk: nineteen sections of architecture could imply a long deliverable.
  **Mitigation:** the page-one rule (§8, Deliverable C) is the hard constraint — the *decision* is one
  page, everything else is optional depth. **Residual risk:** without a page budget, Layer 2 and Layer 3
  will drift longer over time; a length norm should be set from the first pilot engagements, not
  theorized now.
- **Overly legal or defensive tone?** Conditions, drawbacks, dependencies, disclaimers, and disagreements
  could read as a firm protecting itself rather than advising. **Mitigation:** §8 requires caveats in the
  *same voice and type hierarchy* as the conclusion — integrated, not defensive. **Honest risk:** this is
  a writing-craft problem the architecture can constrain but not solve; the first Records will reveal
  whether it reads as candor or as liability management.
- **Too much methodology exposed?** §3 and §8 explicitly keep the taxonomy internal and convey its
  *substance* in plain language. **Residual risk:** a consultant proud of the method will over-explain
  it; the methodology appendix is optional for a reason and should stay small.
- **False precision?** Directly addressed — ranges over point estimates, no bare scores, uncertainty
  beside the number, no figure more exact than its evidence. **Residual risk:** ranges can themselves
  imply rigor they lack if their drivers aren't stated; §10 requires the drivers.
- **Documentation burden?** The gate fields (§6) and lifecycle metadata (§14) add real work per Record.
  **This is deliberate** — the gate fields are the only thing that makes a skipped gate visible, which
  was the acknowledged hole in Consultation Architecture. They are **seven short fields**, sized to be
  completable every time.
- **Founder-led production capacity?** The most serious practical concern. One person conducting
  consultations *and* producing a multi-layer Record per engagement is the binding constraint on how many
  advisors VYNE can serve at launch — and founder decision #1 (a Record in every engagement, including
  Level 1) makes it heavier. **This is a capacity question, not a design question**, and it should be
  measured in pilots rather than estimated here.
- **Premium or bureaucratic?** Honestly, it could go either way, and the architecture alone cannot settle
  it. The structural safeguards are real (page one carries the whole decision; internal vocabulary is
  banned; workpapers stay behind). But a document with conditions, drawbacks, disagreements, dependencies,
  and disclosures **will** feel heavier than a recruiter's two-page comparison. **The bet is that
  advisors making a consequential decision want the weight** — that is a positioning bet, and pilots
  should test it rather than assume it.

## F · Recommendation — resolved

**Approved with targeted revisions (founder, 2026-07-25); revisions applied; document FROZEN.**

The architecture holds the frozen strategy intact and closes the acknowledged hole from Consultation
Architecture: the **gate lifecycle fields (§6)** convert a self-administered control into a visible
workflow status that blocks issuance and authorization — **as methodology first**, with no software
dependency, and **entirely internal**. Recommendation stays strictly separate from authorization (§13);
disagreement is structurally required (§7); calibration cannot be overstated by formatting (§4); no fee
terms, no neutrality claims, no "best firm," no implied market completeness.

## Change log — revisions applied at freeze (2026-07-25)

1. **"It advocates nothing" removed (§1)** → *"It is not promotional and is not designed to sell a firm,
   model, or transaction. It may recommend a direction when the evidence supports one."* (A Level 2/3
   Record **does** advocate a supported direction.)
2. **Layer 3 renamed "Decision Basis and Supporting Evidence" (§3)**; **"Internal Supporting Record"** is
   now reserved **exclusively** for VYNE's internal workpapers. The two are never called the same thing.
3. **All gate fields are internal (§6, §D, map).** The advisor-facing Record carries the approved
   compensation disclosure and **nothing** about trigger, status, completion date,
   basis-without-compensation, cooling, or second-person review. The completed internal gate permits or
   blocks issuance; the Record does not describe the control.
4. **Worked example B3 corrected.** A recommendation that pays VYNE **less** than the advisor's chosen
   option **does not by itself trigger the gate** — that is evidence the separation held, not a conflict.
   Gate now reads *"Not required unless another defined trigger applies,"* with the internal note that
   the recommendation ran against VYNE's apparent financial interest **and that this does not enter or
   strengthen the advisor-fit rationale.** The gate stays focused on situations where VYNE's economics
   could reasonably *distort* the recommendation.
5. **Compensation disclosure placement fixed (§11)** — exactly three places (standing section of the
   Record · consultation when a new specific firm enters · the authorization artifact). **Not repeated
   beside each firm** in a comparison, which would clutter and could imply firm-specific compensation
   disclosure — still unresolved.
6. **Five founder decisions resolved (§19)** — Record in every substantive engagement scaled to level ·
   VYNE retains methodology / advisor gets confidential-use copy (counsel to finalize) · delivered live
   then left · **Decision Status and Closure Record** for no-decision engagements · **no numerical
   retention period**, counsel-approved policy required before production use.
7. **"Tested with" → "consultation completed with" (§14)**, with the clarifying sentence carried into
   the Record itself: *the advisor's participation confirms the recommendation was discussed and tested;
   it does not mean the advisor agreed.*
8. **§2 deliverable family** gains the **Decision Status and Closure Record**; map updated.
