# Content Architecture

**Status:** **PERMANENTLY FROZEN — Phase 2 (founder-approved with revisions 2026-07-25).** Factual
corrections only; **no further refinement** — additional changes are unlikely to produce materially
better outcomes (founder ruling). · **Date:** 2026-07-25
**Phase:** 2 — Architecture · **System:** *Knowledge & Demand* (not *Advisor Decision Experience*)
**Reads from (frozen):** the **[Advisor Decision Ontology](./ADVISOR_DECISION_ONTOLOGY.md)** (spine) and
the **[Decision Question Library](./DECISION_QUESTION_LIBRARY.md)** (inventory). **Governed by** the
foundation, Messaging Architecture, and Commercial Principles.

**What this answers:** *which advisor question, under which decision, deserves which answer **format**,
on which **channel**, and where it hands off to consultation?* It decides **form and flow**, not words.
It is **not** copywriting, page/nav/UI design, AEO keyword strategy, or software.

## 0 · Where this sits — the two Phase-2 systems

Phase 2 has two architectures that must not be conflated:

- **Advisor Decision Experience** (how VYNE *delivers* the relationship): Assessment Architecture ·
  Consultation Architecture · Recommendation & Report Architecture · Advisor-Journey conversion
  handoffs.
- **Knowledge & Demand** (how advisors *discover and learn*): **Content Architecture** *(this doc)* ·
  AEO Architecture · Editorial & Research Architecture · Public-to-Confidential Conversion Architecture.

**AI and CRM consume both systems later — they do not lead them.** Content Architecture defines the
*Knowledge & Demand* format/flow system and the seam where a reader becomes an advisor in a
confidential engagement.

**The two systems develop in parallel** once this document is frozen — Knowledge & Demand need **not**
be completed before Advisor Decision Experience begins. The public conversion handoff cannot be
designed properly without knowing what actually happens inside the assessment and consultation, so
**Assessment Architecture is the immediate next deliverable** (ahead of AEO).

## 1 · The Ontology is the organizing spine (req 1)

Content is filed under the Ontology's **13 decision families + the Strategic Outcome**, never under
"blog topics." Every asset traces: **question → decision → family**. If an asset supports no named
decision, it is company-centric and does not ship. *VYNE supports decisions rather than publishing
content.*

## 2 · Five layers, kept distinct (req 2)

These are different things and must never be collapsed:

| Layer | Definition | Source of truth |
|---|---|---|
| **Decision family** | one of the 13 Ontology families (+ ★ Outcome) | Advisor Decision Ontology |
| **Advisor question** | the advisor's own words for something they need to decide | Decision Question Library |
| **Content purpose** | what the asset is *for* — orient · educate · compare · qualify · convert · deliver | this doc |
| **Content format** | the *form* — answer · guide · comparison · framework · FAQ · checklist · assessment · report | this doc (§11) |
| **Distribution channel** | *where* it renders — web · FAQ · newsletter · AI response · consultation aid · advisor report | this doc (§9) |

**The purpose rule:** *Each content **asset** has **one primary purpose** and may have limited
**secondary** purposes. One advisor **question** may support **multiple assets** with different
purposes.* A question is never forced into a single purpose — "Which affiliation model fits my
practice?" legitimately educates, compares, **and** qualifies, across several assets.

A single decision generates many questions; a question may generate several assets; each asset has one
primary purpose; a purpose is served by a format; a format renders on channels. Confusing "topic" with
"decision," or "format" with "channel," is how content systems rot.

## 3 · Delivery mode per decision (req 3)

Which decisions are appropriate for which delivery — driven by the Ontology's delivery ladder
(Education → Consulting → Specialist) and each question's complexity:

| Delivery | Fits decisions that are… | Ontology examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Public education** | general-form, non-confidential, AI-sufficient | B4 capabilities (general), C6 models, E13/E14 valuation methods, F19 deal mechanics, K30 covenants (general) |
| **Downloadable resource** | education worth keeping — checklists, question banks | portability factors, due-diligence checklist, RIA-launch readiness list |
| **Interactive orientation** *(public — NOT automated assessment)* | helps the advisor organize their own thinking | portability considerations, capability-gap reflection, "questions to weigh about how I want to work" |
| **Consultation** | advisor-specific, confidential, interpretive | ★ stay/move/wait, C-fit-to-me, F my-net, G my-book, J succession-for-me |
| **Confidential advisor report** | the synthesized conclusion (The VYNE Standard) | the integrated recommendation + supporting record |
| **Specialist referral only** | regulated/executional advice | J30 my covenants (legal), tax specifics, ADV/registration filing, hands-on implementation |

**Rule:** the *general* form of almost every decision has a public-education asset; the
*advisor-specific* form is consulting; regulated specifics are specialist. No decision is education-only
if its personal form is consequential.

### Interactive orientation, not automated assessment (launch constraint)

Public interactive tools **may**: ask reflection questions · identify considerations · show readiness
*factors* · organize issues · suggest topics for a consultation.

They **must not** (at launch) provide: a definitive firm recommendation · a stay-or-move score · an
estimated portability percentage · an automated best-fit affiliation model · a firm match · or any
advisor-specific conclusion.

**Why:** an automated recommendation would outrun the methodology, the confidence model, and the
disclaimer system. **Advisor-specific assessments remain confidential** inside the advisor relationship
and are governed by the forthcoming **Assessment Architecture** — not by this document.

## 4 · The full legitimate outcome set gets fair representation (req 4)

VYNE is not move-biased. Each legitimate outcome carries real content, so a reader sees that **staying
and waiting are first-class conclusions**:

| Outcome | Representative content (education → consulting) |
|---|---|
| **Stay** | "When staying is the right call" · counteroffer analysis → stay-vs-move consultation |
| **Wait** | "When timing says wait" · what would need to change → timing consultation |
| **Move** | affiliation-model education · deal mechanics → transition consultation |
| **Launch an RIA** | RIA-launch readiness · build/buy/outsource → launch-evaluation consultation |
| **Join an RIA** | joining vs. building · what to diligence → fit consultation |
| **Supported independence** | supported vs. full independence → net-economics consultation |
| **Succession** | internal vs. external · runway → succession consultation |
| **Sale** | how practices are valued · terms vs. headline → valuation/sale consultation |
| **Acquisition** | financing · valuing a target → acquisition consultation |
| **Merger** | when a merger beats a move → merger consultation |
| **Retirement / wind-down** | orderly wind-down options → exit consultation |

**Editorial quota discipline:** the education library must not over-index on "move" simply because move
questions are highest-volume (see §10). Stay/wait/succession/growth get deliberate coverage.

## 5 · Education → advisor-specific interpretation (req 5)

Every public asset answers the *general* question honestly and completely, then names the exact point
where only confidential, advisor-specific work can continue — it never pretends generic content can
resolve the individual's decision.

```
  Education asset (answers the general form)
        │  "…but the answer for YOUR book/economics/goals depends on X, Y, Z."
        ▼
  Advisor-specific interpretation  →  Assessment / Consultation  →  Confidential Report
```

The handoff is **honest, not a bait**: the education must have real standalone value; the seam is the
truthful boundary of what a general answer can do.

## 6 · Evidence classification inside content (req 6)

Every content asset distinguishes — visibly where it matters — six evidence classes (aligned to the
research's evidence discipline and The VYNE Standard):

1. **Verified fact** — checkable, sourced, dated.
2. **Public firm claim** — what a firm says about itself (attributed, not endorsed).
3. **Market observation** — pattern across the market (cited, dated).
4. **Assumption** — stated as an assumption, with what would confirm/refute it.
5. **VYNE interpretation** — VYNE's judgment, labeled as judgment.
6. **Advisor-specific conclusion** — only inside confidential deliverables; never in public content.

Public content may carry 1–5; **class 6 is confidential by definition.** No asset blends a firm's claim
into fact, or VYNE's interpretation into "the market says."

## 7 · Professional boundaries (req 7)

Content stays on the correct side of every licensed line and routes to specialists for the rest:

| Domain | Content may… | Content must NOT… | Routes to |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Legal** (covenants, Protocol, non-solicit, Reg S-P) | explain generally, issue-spot | advise on the advisor's contract | counsel |
| **Tax** | explain mechanics at a high level | give tax advice or numbers for a person | CPA/tax counsel |
| **Regulatory / RIA registration** | explain what's required generally | prepare/file ADV or act as CCO | compliance/counsel |
| **Valuation** | explain methods/ranges | issue a formal valuation | valuation specialist |
| **M&A** | explain structures | paper a deal | M&A/legal |
| **Operational transition / implementation** | explain the choices (Ontology M) | perform repapering/ACATS/setup | implementation partners |

The in-model legal role is **issue-spotting only** (Council Charter §1.5). Boundary language is a
first-class content element, not a disclaimer afterthought.

## 8 · Sourcing, dating, review, refresh (req 8)

**Editorial verification** — required for **every** firm-specific claim (this is the ordinary path, and
it does **not** involve counsel): authoritative sourcing · attribution · publication or retrieval date ·
**source owner** · refresh cadence · editorial approval.

- **Firm-specific content** (a firm's programs/claims): sourced to the firm's own public materials,
  **attributed**, **dated**, classified as *public firm claim* (class 2), and refreshed on a fixed
  cadence or on a known change (earnings, program change). Never presented as VYNE's endorsement.
  *An ordinary factual firm claim — a published platform feature, office location, custody relationship,
  or recruiting program — needs editorial verification, not legal review (§14).*
- **Market-wide content**: sourced to cited third parties, dated, classified as *market observation*
  (class 3) or *verified fact* (class 1).
- **Every asset carries:** source(s), publication date, last-reviewed date, next-review date, and an
  owner (§14). **Undated firm/market claims do not publish.**

## 9 · One canonical source record, many outputs (req 9)

The single most important anti-rot rule — and it is a **governance standard, not a technology
requirement**:

> **Every substantive decision answer has one designated canonical source record. All derived assets
> must reference that record and be reviewed for consistency when it changes.**

The canonical record (one per **decision × ladder-rung**) holds: the **general answer** (education), the
**evidence classification** (§6), the **boundary** (§7), the **advisor-specific handoff** (§5), and
**provenance** (§8).

Derived assets — web article, FAQ entry, newsletter section, AI response, consultation aid, assessment
explanation, advisor-report passage — **cite** that record. Channels differ in length and framing,
**never in substance**. When the record changes, its derived assets are **reconciled** (a review
obligation on the content owner, §14). This is what prevents the FAQ, the AI, and the consultant from
saying three different things.

**Launch constraint (explicit):** this requires **no database, CMS, headless publishing engine, or
synchronization software.** At launch it is a documented discipline — a source record plus a reconcile
step. Automated rendering/synchronization is a *future product* capability (Phase 3), and Phase 2 must
not become dependent on building it.

## 10 · AEO support without letting search demand redefine the method (req 10)

Content Architecture *enables* AEO but subordinates it to the methodology:

- **Search demand informs prioritization only within the Education rung** — it never adds, removes, or
  reshapes a *decision*. The Ontology defines what exists; demand only orders the education queue.
- **Consequential low-volume decisions are protected** by the outcome quota (§4) — succession, wait,
  stay, and launch get coverage regardless of search volume.
- **Every asset supports a named decision** — no content is created to chase a keyword that maps to no
  decision.
- **VYNE is not a media publisher** — the education library exists to *lead into interpretation*, not
  to accumulate traffic. Volume is a distribution input for AEO Strategy (next doc), not a content
  mandate here.

## 11 · Content hierarchy (req 11)

From broad to confidential — each level has a defined job. **The Strategic Outcome is not a family or a
pillar**; it is the *synthesis* produced from the 13 families, so publicly it appears as a **Gateway**
(a high-level entry point and consultation gateway), never as a decision-family pillar:

```
  ADVISOR DECISION SYSTEM
    ├─ STRATEGIC OUTCOME GATEWAY   the synthesis, as a public entry point —
    │    "Stay · Wait · Move · Launch · Join · Merge · Acquire · Sell · Succeed ·
    │     Retire · Wind down"   (entry point + consultation gateway, NOT a pillar)
    │
    ├─ DECISION FAMILIES           the 13 durable pillars (e.g., Affiliation & Operating Model)
    │    └─ DECISION HUB             one decision (e.g., "Which affiliation model fits my practice?")
    │         ├─ QUESTION PAGE       a single advisor question (Direct answer; AEO surface)
    │         ├─ GUIDE               long-form "how it works"
    │         ├─ COMPARISON          models/paths side by side (evidence-classified)
    │         ├─ FRAMEWORK           interactive **orientation** (the consulting bridge, §3)
    │         ├─ FAQ                 short, high-frequency answers (derived from the canonical record)
    │         └─ CHECKLIST           downloadable resource
    │
    │    ── public / education above · confidential / consulting below ──
    │
    └─ CONFIDENTIAL
         ├─ ASSESSMENT MODULE      advisor-specific (a PTA area)
         ├─ CONSULTATION RESOURCE  used inside the engagement
         └─ CONFIDENTIAL DELIVERABLE  the advisor report / the Record
```

Decision families and hubs are the durable spine; the Gateway is the entry point; question pages and
FAQs are the demand surface; assessment/consultation/deliverable are the confidential floor.

## 12 · Minimum viable launch content set (req 12) + priorities (deliverable C)

Deliberately lean — depth over breadth. Prove the model on a few high-consequence decisions before
scaling.

- **Launch-critical (five items — build first):**
  - **A. Strategic Outcome Gateway** — "Should I stay, wait, or explore a change?", explicitly
    including launch, succession, sale, merger, and wind-down where relevant. *(Gateway, not a pillar.)*
  - **B. Affiliation Model Decision Hub** + model **comparison** — employee · regional · independent
    broker-dealer · supported independence · join an RIA · build an RIA.
  - **C. Book Portability Decision Hub** — asset portability · revenue portability · client behavior ·
    product and account constraints; carries the principle **asset portability ≠ revenue portability**.
  - **D. How VYNE Works** — the confidential process · advisor-controlled introductions · the approved
    compensation disclosure (§15) · professional boundaries (§7).
  - **E. Advisor Orientation** — the consultation-entry *experience*, not merely a form. It includes:
    the **questionnaire** (advisor objectives · business profile · primary concerns · preferred
    disclosure level · **no client PII**), the **confidentiality explanation**, a **consulting-process
    overview**, **what to expect / advisor and VYNE responsibilities**, **disclosure options**, the
    **recommendation philosophy** (VYNE may conclude stay/wait/any legitimate outcome), and
    **introduction authorization** (no firm is contacted without the advisor's explicit approval).
    *(Orientation, not assessment — §3. The confidential engagement that follows is governed by the
    Assessment Architecture.)*
- **First 90 days:**
  - Decision hubs for **D Ownership**, **E Enterprise Value**, **F Economics**, **J Succession & Exit**.
  - **RIA-launch readiness** + **due-diligence** checklists.
  - "When staying/waiting is right" education (outcome-quota coverage, §4).
- **Later expansion:**
  - Full question-page coverage across all 13 families; firm-specific comparison content (with the
    §8 sourcing/refresh discipline); research/editorial cadence.

*That launch set is enough to communicate the business, establish expertise, and generate
consultations. Ownership, Enterprise Value, Economics, Succession, RIA readiness, and the broader
due-diligence libraries move to the first-90-day inventory unless a launch dependency requires
otherwise.*

## 13 · What NOT to build at launch (req 13)

- No firm-by-firm directory or "rankings."
- No generic "advisor transition" blog/news feed (media-publisher trap).
- No advisor-paid-consulting content (deferred — Future Business Model Expansion).
- No AI chat surface, no calculators promising personal numbers, no marketplace/matching UI.
- No content for decisions VYNE cannot yet responsibly support to The VYNE Standard.
- Nothing containing fee percentages/terms (§15).

## 14 · Governance (req 14)

Every canonical knowledge object (§9) carries:

- **Content owner** (accountable for the answer) · **Source owner** (accountable for provenance/§8).
- **Approval status**: Draft → In review → Approved → Published (mirrors the Record lifecycle).
- **Review cadence**: default periodic; firm-specific objects on a shorter clock.
- **Stale-content rule**: past next-review date → auto-flagged **Stale**, removed from AEO surfacing
  until refreshed.
- **Two distinct review paths** (do not conflate — conflating them bottlenecks the business):
  - **Editorial verification** — required for **every** firm-specific claim: authoritative sourcing ·
    attribution · publication/retrieval date · source ownership · refresh cadence · editorial approval.
    *No counsel required for ordinary factual firm claims.*
  - **Legal or compliance review** — triggered **only** when content materially addresses: contracts ·
    restrictive covenants · solicitation · registration · regulatory obligations · privacy ·
    compensation · employment terms · transition conduct · legal interpretation · comparative claims
    presenting material risk · or another issue identified by counsel.
- **Archive & replacement**: superseded objects are archived with a pointer to their replacement — never
  silently deleted; links resolve forward.

## 15 · Confidentiality confirmation (req 15)

VYNE's **fee percentages, amounts, step-ups, payment timing, and firm-specific commercial terms never
enter public or advisor-facing content** (commercial §10). The only compensation language permitted in
content is the approved disclosure: *"VYNE is generally compensated by the hiring firm following a
successful placement. Advisors do not pay VYNE for traditional recruiting and transition-consulting
services."* Never unbiased/neutral/conflict-free.

## B · Three fully worked examples

**Question → Decision → content purpose → format → channel → consultation handoff → confidential deliverable**

1. **"Should I stay or move?"**
   → **★ Strategic Outcome Gateway** *(the synthesis as a public entry point — not a pillar)* → primary
   purpose **orient** *(secondary: educate)* → format **Guide** ("How to think about staying vs.
   moving," with stay/wait as real answers) → channels **web + FAQ + newsletter + AI** (all derived from
   one canonical source record) → handoff: *"the answer for your book, economics, and goals is
   advisor-specific"* → **Consultation** (the whole PTA) → confidential deliverable: **the integrated
   recommendation + Record** (calibrated Level 1–3). *Evidence:* market-observation + VYNE-interpretation
   public; advisor-conclusion confidential.

2. **"Which affiliation model fits my practice?"**
   → Decision **C6** → purpose **educate + compare** → formats **Guide** (the models) + **Comparison**
   (models side-by-side, firm-claims attributed) + **Framework** *(interactive **orientation** —
   questions to weigh about how I want to work; **no** automated best-fit model, §3)* → channels **web +
   FAQ + AI + consultation aid** → handoff: the framework surfaces the fit
   questions only VYNE can weigh against capability requirements (B) and ownership (D) → **Assessment**
   (Capability compatibility · Operating-model) → confidential deliverable: **model-fit section of the
   report.** *Boundary:* no "best firm" language (§15).

3. **"How portable is my book?"**
   → Decision **G21** → purpose **educate + qualify** → formats **Guide** (what drives portability) +
   **Framework** *(interactive **orientation** — the considerations that drive portability, teaching
   **asset ≠ revenue**; **no** portability percentage, §3)* + **Checklist** (downloadable) → channels
   **web + FAQ + AI + assessment explanation** → handoff: *"a real estimate
   needs your book"* → **Assessment** (Book Portability, PTA Area 3) → confidential deliverable:
   **portability estimate + evidence/confidence** in the report. *Boundary:* Reg S-P / non-solicit are
   general only → counsel.

## D · Critique (where this draft may be wrong)

Honest self-assessment against the failure modes:

- **Too complex?** The canonical-knowledge-object model (§9) and six evidence classes (§6) are the
  right *ideas* but could over-engineer a pre-launch company. **Mitigation:** at launch, apply §9/§6
  rigor only to the launch-critical objects (§12); let the rest be simpler until proven.
- **Too broad?** Thirteen families × many formats implies a large library. The MVP (§12) is the guard —
  but the founder should confirm the launch scope is lean enough.
- **Too media-oriented?** The pillar/hub/question-page hierarchy resembles a content-marketing SEO
  stack. **Guardrail (§10):** every asset must lead into interpretation; if the education library ever
  reads like a traffic play, it has drifted.
- **Too transition-centric?** The worked examples lean on move/affiliation/portability. **Mitigation:**
  the outcome quota (§4) and the stay/wait/succession launch items (§12) must be enforced, or VYNE
  quietly becomes "a better recruiter" again.
- **Too dependent on AI?** AI is listed as a channel, not an author. If AI ever *generates* rather than
  *renders* canonical objects, honesty and boundaries erode. Keep AI downstream (§0).
- **Beyond VYNE's scope?** Editorial/research cadence and firm-specific refresh (§8) imply an editorial
  function VYNE doesn't staff yet. That belongs to the Editorial & Research system (a separate
  Knowledge-&-Demand doc), not to launch.
