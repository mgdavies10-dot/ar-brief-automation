# The VYNE Advisor Journey

**Status:** DRAFT for founder review · **Date:** 2026-07-25
**Phase:** 1 — Expression · **Derived from:** the Constitution, the Founder Alignment
Memo, the VYNE Story, the Digital Experience Strategy, and the **Practice Transition
Assessment** engagement model.

**What this is.** The advisor's path through the *actual VYNE consulting relationship*
— not a marketing or sales funnel. Where the Digital Experience Strategy maps the
*emotional* arc across surfaces, this maps the *engagement*: how an advisor moves
(non-linearly) from independent research to a long-term relationship, and where the
modular assessment, graduated disclosure, and calibrated recommendations enter.

**Not this document's job:** screens, pages, CRM stages for sales management, or a
conversion funnel. The journey preserves advisor autonomy — **staying, pausing, or
continuing independently are valid outcomes,** never leaks to be plugged.

## Levels of engagement (depth, not a required sequence)

An advisor may stop, rest, or exit at any level — each delivers real value:

**Public education → self-directed value → preliminary consulting → formal engagement
→ deeper analysis → due diligence → recommendation → implementation support.**

## The stages

For each: *context · questions/needs/concerns · VYNE's role & activity · evidence used
· how trust is earned · disclosure level · outcome · what advances · alternate paths.*

### 1 · Independent research  *(public education / self-directed · no disclosure)*
The advisor researches firms and channels via AI, search, publications, firm sites,
and direct recruiter conversations — often before contacting VYNE, and often already
leaning. **Needs:** useful, neutral education and frameworks to think clearly.
**Concern:** being sold to. **VYNE:** gives genuinely useful education, questions, and
comparison frameworks, no strings (evidence: public market intelligence). **Trust:**
neutrality, accuracy, generosity, no urgency. **Outcome:** the advisor is better off
regardless of whether they engage. **Advances when** the decision feels too complex,
confidential, or consequential for self-service. **Alternate:** remains self-directed —
a legitimate end.

### 2 · Early curiosity & confidential contact  *(preliminary · Level 1)*
The advisor reaches out, cautiously. **Questions:** "Is it safe to talk? Will my
information be used against me?" **Needs:** safety; clarity on how VYNE works and is
paid. **VYNE:** establishes confidentiality, discloses compensation and conflicts,
listens. **Trust:** confidentiality, transparency, listening, no pressure. **Outcome:**
it feels safe to share high-level information. **Advances when** the advisor agrees to
a high-level review. **Alternate:** pauses; stays self-directed.

### 3 · Initial diagnostic — the high-level scan  *(preliminary consulting · Level 1)*
A **comprehensive scan across all twelve assessment areas** using aggregate inputs —
so material issues are not missed. **Needs:** to be understood specifically; to know
what actually matters. **Concern:** a generic assessment. **VYNE:** runs the scan,
surfaces which areas are material for *this* advisor, begins drawing out objectives.
**Trust:** preparation, specificity, honesty about the unknown. **Outcome:** a first
map of what matters and which modules need depth. **Advances when** material modules
and objectives are identified. **Alternate:** the scan finds no compelling reason to
change → a decision-support "stay / monitor."

### 4 · Objectives & current-practice understanding  *(Level 1→2 as the advisor chooses)*
Define **advisor-defined objectives** and a **fair** current-practice profile
(including the current firm's advantages, not only its frustrations). **Concern:**
being pushed toward a move. **VYNE:** translates goals into decision criteria; profiles
the practice fairly. **Trust:** listening, fairness to the current firm, remembering
context. **Outcome:** objectives + current-practice profile. **Advances when** both are
clear enough to form a view.

### 5 · Preliminary perspective  *(preliminary consulting · Level 1/2 · conclusion Level 1)*
VYNE's early **independent read** — what matters, the realistic options, how the
tradeoffs apply — **before** the advisor is asked to decide. **Concern:** acting on
firm marketing that doesn't fit their book. **VYNE:** independent interpretation, tests
the advisor's existing research, offers a **decision-support conclusion**. **Trust:**
independent interpretation, testing claims, acknowledging uncertainty. **Outcome:** a
Level-1 conclusion (preliminary direction + open questions). **Advances when** the
advisor wants deeper analysis. **Alternate:** the preliminary view is enough → stay,
wait, or pause.

### 6 · Engagement scoping — the tailored engagement  *(Level 1→2/3)*
Agree which modules go deep, the disclosure level, and the analysis plan — *the
framework is comprehensive; the engagement is tailored.* **Concern:** the cost, effort,
or exposure of deeper disclosure. **VYNE:** right-sizes depth (the comprehensive scan
is already done); sets disclosure requirements and the plan. **Trust:** not over- or
under-serving; transparency. **Outcome:** a tailored analysis plan. **Advances when**
scope and disclosure are agreed. **Alternate:** the advisor limits disclosure →
analysis proceeds at achievable confidence.

### 7 · Modular analysis  *(deeper analysis · Level 2/3)*
Deep work only in the material areas: firm/channel exploration, portability &
**capability compatibility**, transition economics & **enterprise value**, and
client/team/operating-model impact. **Concern:** portability or economic surprises.
**VYNE:** the assessment modules, delivered to The VYNE Standard, provenance-tagged
(firm claim vs. verified vs. modeled vs. advisor-provided vs. VYNE judgment).
**Trust:** consistent standards, distinguishing claim from verified, honest confidence.
**Outcome:** module analyses with ranges, confidence, assumptions, and open questions.
**Advances when** the analysis can support comparison. **Alternate:** analysis shows no
alternative is compelling → decision-support "stay."

### 8 · Due diligence & iterative comparison  *(due diligence · Level 2/3)*
**Verify** firm claims and **compare** alternatives under consistent standards,
iterating. **Concern:** hidden terms; a preferred firm not holding up. **VYNE:** the
verification plan (what must be verified, by whom, disqualifiers) and cross-model
comparison including staying. **Trust:** rigor, willingness to eliminate a favored
option, transparency. **Outcome:** a verified comparison. **Advances when** verification
is sufficient for a calibrated conclusion. **Alternate:** diligence eliminates an
initially preferred firm; the advisor pauses; objectives change.

### 9 · Calibrated conclusion  *(recommendation)*
VYNE's conclusion at **the level the evidence supports** — decision-support,
conditional, or full — with why, evidence, assumptions, confidence, risks, alternatives
considered, what could change it, and next steps. Delivered in the three layers
(executive narrative · decision comparison · supporting record). **Trust:** calibration,
disclosure of conflicts/compensation, no house answer. **Outcome:** the recommendation.
**Advances when** the advisor is ready to decide. **Alternate:** a conditional
recommendation returns them to diligence; or "wait."

### 10 · Advisor decision
The advisor decides — any of the twelve outcomes, **including stay or wait.** **VYNE:**
full support for whatever they choose, with no pull toward a destination. **Trust:**
supporting "stay" as readily as "move." **Outcome:** a decision the advisor owns.
**Alternate:** decides to stay (a success); revisits after defined milestones.

### 11 · Transition or implementation support  *(if appropriate)*
Steady, competent support through execution; realistic expectations on portability and
operational burden. **Concern:** chaos; abandonment; losing clients or revenue.
**VYNE:** implementation support; the same understanding carried into execution.
**Trust:** presence, accuracy, honesty about the hard parts. **Outcome:** an executed
transition or implemented decision.

### 12 · Long-term relationship
Continuity beyond the decision. **Needs:** to be remembered; help ready for the next
decision. **VYNE:** persistent, deepening understanding and useful presence between
decisions. **Trust:** remembering context, continued neutrality. **Outcome:** a
relationship that compounds — the advisor becomes an advocate.

## The journey is not linear

Legitimate paths, all valid outcomes rather than funnel leaks: the advisor **remains
self-directed** · **arrives with a preferred firm** · **already has a proposal** ·
**discloses only high-level information** · **pauses** · **decides to stay** ·
**returns later** · **changes objectives mid-process** · **diligence eliminates the
initially preferred firm** · **analysis reveals no current alternative is sufficiently
compelling.** The journey supports re-entry at the appropriate stage without starting
over.

## How graduated disclosure shapes the journey

Depth of analysis follows what the advisor chooses to share — never the reverse.
**Level 1** (aggregate) enables Stages 2–5 (contact → preliminary perspective);
**Level 2** (redacted/aggregated) enables meaningful modular analysis (Stage 7);
**Level 3** (confidential) enables highest-precision analysis and due diligence. *More
information improves precision; it is never the price of admission.*

## Where the Practice Transition Assessment enters

The **high-level scan** (Stage 3) *is* the comprehensive twelve-area framework; the
**modular depth** (Stages 6–8) is the tailored engagement; the **calibrated conclusion**
(Stage 9) is Area 12. The journey is the human experience of that assessment —
`../method/PRACTICE_TRANSITION_ASSESSMENT.md`.

## Autonomy

The advisor retains the final decision at every stage. VYNE provides clear professional
judgment; it does not decide for the advisor, does not push toward a destination, and
treats staying, pausing, and continuing independently as fully legitimate.
