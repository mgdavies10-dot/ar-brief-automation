# VYNE Foundation Change Log

A record of intentional changes to VYNE's **philosophy** — not its products. Every
time a foundational principle is adopted or amended, capture not just *that* it
changed, but *why*, so future leaders understand the reasoning behind the company
they inherited.

Each entry records: **Date · What changed · Why · What prompted it · Expected
impact.** Newest first.

---

## 2026-07-23 — The VYNE Constitution ratified (v1.0)

- **What changed:** VYNE adopted its first Constitution — a worldview preamble, ten
  enduring principles (Mission · Advisor First · Decisions Over Transactions · What
  VYNE Is · The Hierarchy · Trust · Evidence Before Opinion · How We Reason · The
  Decision Standard · What VYNE Will Not Become), a decision standard, and a
  two-question governance gate — established as the highest authority at the
  company.
- **Why:** to preserve VYNE's identity, principles, and decision-making philosophy
  as it grows, and to give every future employee, consultant, contractor, partner,
  and AI agent one source of truth to evaluate decisions against.
- **What prompted it:** an alignment review after M4 closed surfaced that the
  early foundational documents were drifting toward describing a *software
  platform* — when VYNE is a **consulting firm with a proprietary technology
  edge.** The founder paused all building to unify the philosophy first.
- **Expected impact:** future products, hires, marketing, website copy, and AI
  agents can be judged against a fixed philosophy; drift toward "software company"
  or "recruiting business" is structurally resisted by the governance gate
  (*should VYNE build this?* → *does it strengthen the consulting relationship?*).
  The advisor-first mission and "Decisions Over Transactions" become the permanent
  test of every decision.
