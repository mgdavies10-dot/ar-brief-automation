# Founder Alignment Memo

**Status:** Adopted (living document) · **Date:** 2026-07-24
**Interprets:** The VYNE Constitution v1.0 · **Authority:** below the Constitution,
above the VYNE Story.

This memo is **how the founder interprets the Constitution today.**

This memo intentionally contains strategy, interpretation, and current direction.
**Unlike the Constitution, it is expected to evolve as VYNE grows.** When guidance
here no longer reflects the company's strategy, *the memo should be updated — not
the Constitution.* The Constitution changes only when VYNE's **enduring
principles** themselves evolve. Where this memo and the Constitution ever seem to
conflict, the Constitution wins.

### How to read this memo

It deliberately mixes three kinds of content, and each section is tagged so you
know how durable it is:

- **[Philosophy]** — enduring; it restates or traces to the Constitution. For the
  permanent version, read the Constitution.
- **[Strategy]** — how VYNE competes and builds *right now.* Expected to change.
- **[Interpretation]** — today's reading of the principles. The most temporary of
  all.

If something tagged *Strategy* or *Interpretation* stops fitting reality, update
this memo. Nothing here may override the Constitution.

---

## 1. Reading VYNE's identity today · *[Interpretation]*

The Constitution fixes the identity (Principle 4): three layers that must never
be confused —

- **The company** is a consulting firm — what we say, publicly, always.
- **The method** — the VYNE Method — is how we consistently deliver understanding,
  perspective, and guidance.
- **The internal platform** — proprietary technology and market intelligence — is
  the edge that makes our consultants more informed and valuable than anyone
  else's.

**The platform exists to strengthen the consulting experience, not to define the
company.** Internally, it is fine to call it *the operating system that powers our
consulting practice.* Externally, we never lead with it and never describe VYNE as
software.

## 2. Language rules, public vs. internal · *[Interpretation]*

- **"Operating system," "platform," "engine"** — internal only. Never in external
  copy, decks, or the website.
- **The CRM / AI comparison** explains our internal *capability*, never our
  category. Approved framing: *"Traditional CRMs help firms organize information.
  AI tools help generate answers. VYNE's internal platform helps our consultants
  develop and preserve a deeper understanding of each advisor's business."*
- **Public voice leads with the advisor and the consulting relationship.** The
  technology is the quiet advantage, never the headline.

## 3. Who we compete with · *[Strategy]*

VYNE competes, *as a consulting firm*, with the established advisor-transition
consultancies — **Diamond Consultants, Bridgemark Strategies, Spartan Advisory
Partners, and Successful Onboarding.** We do not compete with software platforms,
custodians, broker-dealers, or marketplaces.

Our differentiation is **not** better individual consultants — those firms have
excellent consultants too. It is that VYNE **institutionalizes** great consulting:
understanding, judgment, and market intelligence compound *within the firm*
instead of walking out the door with individuals.

> **To ground before we assert externally:** the supporting market analysis — the
> Winthrop *State of Advisor Movement* report and structured competitor reviews —
> should be brought into the repository. It has been referenced but is **not yet
> captured here**, and no external positioning should rest on analysis we can't
> point to.

## 4. What we build now vs. later · *[Strategy]*

- **Now (delivered in M4):** the transition-consulting core — a deep understanding
  of the advisor's practice, an honest professional perspective, and a premium
  consulting artifact. **Next:** advisor-facing delivery (M5), with its own
  approved plan.
- **Later — deepenings of the practice, not separate businesses:** succession,
  acquisition, partnership, continuity, and enterprise-value planning. They extend
  the same relationship and understanding; never spun off as separate products.
- **Internal-only capabilities:** the founder-level intelligence layer and any
  future opportunity network are consulting capabilities — not public products,
  not a marketplace.

## 5. Value at any depth · *[Philosophy]*

An advisor receives meaningful value **whether they share very little or engage in
a deep, confidential review.** The relationship gives before it asks. *(This
traces to the Constitution, Principle 10.)*

## 6. Information governance · *[Philosophy → Interpretation]*

The principle is constitutional: advisor-provided information is **governed and
verified**, and never automatically becomes firm-wide truth. Today's application:
information informs the specific engagement; it enters shared market intelligence
only through deliberate, governed verification. Confidentiality is a first-order
commitment, not a setting.

## 7. The mission, in the founder's words · *[Philosophy]*

Our competitive advantage is **not AI.** It is **institutionalizing great
consulting** — building a consulting organization where knowledge, judgment, and
market intelligence compound over time instead of walking out the door with
individual consultants. Achieve that, and VYNE is genuinely differentiated without
ever ceasing to be a consulting firm.

## 8. Decision Filters · *[Strategy — management's daily use]*

These are **not** constitutional principles; they are the management questions we
run every real decision through. Whenever we consider a feature, partnership,
hire, marketing initiative, pricing change, AI capability, acquisition, or
research initiative, ask:

1. Does this **strengthen the consulting relationship**?
2. Does this **improve advisor understanding**?
3. Does this **preserve neutrality**?
4. Does this **increase institutional knowledge**?
5. Does this **align with the Constitution**?

If the answer to any is *no*, stop and reconsider. These five questions are
management's daily operating system.
