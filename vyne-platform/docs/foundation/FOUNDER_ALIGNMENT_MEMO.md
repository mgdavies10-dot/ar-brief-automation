# Founder Alignment Memo

**Status:** DRAFT for founder review · **Date:** 2026-07-23
**Interprets:** The VYNE Constitution v1.0 · **Authority:** below the Constitution,
above the VYNE Story.

This memo is **how the founder interprets the Constitution today.** The
Constitution is timeless; this is its present-day reading — the strategy,
boundaries, and language rules that are true *now* and may evolve as the company
does. It never overrides the Constitution; where they seem to conflict, the
Constitution wins. This memo is expected to be revised over time; each revision is
dated.

## 1. How to read VYNE today — the three layers

- **The company is a consulting firm.** This is what we say, publicly, always.
- **The method** — the VYNE Method — is how we consistently deliver understanding,
  perspective, and guidance.
- **The internal platform** — our proprietary technology and market intelligence —
  is the edge that makes our consultants more informed, more current, and more
  valuable than anyone else's.

Internally, it is fine to call the platform *the operating system that powers our
consulting practice.* **Externally, we never lead with it, and we never describe
VYNE as software.**

## 2. Language rules (public vs. internal)

- **"Operating system," "platform," "engine"** — internal language only. Never in
  external copy, decks, or the website.
- **The CRM / AI comparison** is used only to explain our internal *capability*,
  never to place VYNE in a software category. The approved framing: *"Traditional
  CRMs help firms organize information. AI tools help generate answers. VYNE's
  internal platform helps our consultants develop and preserve a deeper
  understanding of each advisor's business."*
- **Public voice always leads with the advisor and the consulting relationship** —
  the technology is the quiet advantage, never the headline.

## 3. Who we compete with (today)

VYNE competes, *as a consulting firm*, with the established advisor-transition
consultancies — **Diamond Consultants, Bridgemark Strategies, Spartan Advisory
Partners, and Successful Onboarding.** We do not compete with software platforms,
custodians, broker-dealers, or marketplaces.

Our differentiation is **not** better individual consultants — those firms have
excellent consultants too. It is that VYNE **institutionalizes** great consulting:
understanding, judgment, and market intelligence compound *within the firm*
instead of walking out the door with individuals.

> **To ground before we assert externally:** the supporting market analysis — the
> Winthrop *State of Advisor Movement* report and structured competitor reviews —
> should be brought into the repository. It has been referenced but is **not yet
> captured here**, and no external positioning should rest on analysis we can't
> point to.

## 4. What we build now vs. later

- **Now (delivered in M4):** the transition-consulting core — capturing a deep
  understanding of the advisor's practice, forming an honest professional
  perspective, and delivering it as a premium consulting artifact. **Next:**
  advisor-facing delivery (M5) — an EA-001 milestone stop with its own approved
  plan.
- **Later — deepenings of the practice, not separate businesses:** succession,
  acquisition, partnership, continuity, and enterprise-value planning. They extend
  the same relationship and the same understanding; they are never spun off as
  separate products or business lines.
- **Internal-only capabilities:** the founder-level intelligence layer and any
  future opportunity network are consulting capabilities that make our consultants
  better — **not** public products, and **not** a marketplace.

## 5. Value at any depth

An advisor receives meaningful value **whether they share very little or engage in
a deep, confidential review.** The relationship gives before it asks.

## 6. Information governance (today's interpretation)

Advisor-provided information is **governed and verified.** It informs the specific
engagement; it never automatically becomes firm-wide truth or market intelligence
without deliberate, governed verification. Confidentiality is a first-order
commitment, not a setting.

## 7. The mission, in the founder's words

Our competitive advantage is **not AI.** It is **institutionalizing great
consulting** — building a consulting organization where knowledge, judgment, and
market intelligence compound over time instead of walking out the door with
individual consultants. Achieve that, and VYNE is genuinely differentiated without
ever ceasing to be a consulting firm.
