# The VYNE Constitution

**Status:** DRAFT for founder review · **Date:** 2026-07-23
**Authority:** The highest document at VYNE. Everything — the Founder Alignment
Memo, the VYNE Story, the roadmap, the website, the brand, and every product
decision — derives from and must remain consistent with what follows. It is
written for everyone who will ever contribute to VYNE: employees, consultants,
contractors, partners, investors, and AI agents. Read it before you build,
write, design, or advise anything.

*A constitution establishes enduring principles without prescribing
implementation. This document changes only by deliberate amendment.*

---

## Article I — Mission

VYNE exists to help financial advisors make one of the most important decisions
of their professional lives — whether and how to stay, move, go independent,
sell, acquire, partner, or plan succession — **with greater clarity, confidence,
and trust.**

We accomplish this by **institutionalizing great consulting**, so that
understanding, judgment, and market intelligence *compound within VYNE over time*
rather than disappearing when an individual consultant does. Institutionalizing
consulting is our method. The advisor's decision is our purpose.

## Article II — Advisor First

Every significant product, research, technology, marketing, and business decision
must improve the **advisor's** ability to make a better-informed decision.

If a choice primarily benefits VYNE while adding little value to the advisor, it
must be reconsidered. Every other article of this Constitution is downstream of
this one.

## Article III — What VYNE Is

**VYNE is a consulting firm.** That is our identity, and it does not change.

Three layers make VYNE work, and they must never be confused:

- **The company** is a consulting firm — this is who we are and what we say.
- **The methodology** — the VYNE Method — is how we deliver value consistently.
- **The internal platform** — our proprietary technology and market
  intelligence — is what makes our consultants more informed, more current, and
  more valuable than anyone else's.

The platform is our advantage. It is never our identity. We are not a software
company, and we are not a new category — we are the best advisor-transition
consulting firm, with an edge no one can copy.

## Article IV — The Hierarchy

Value at VYNE always flows in this order, and technology sits last for a reason:

1. The consulting relationship
2. Understanding the advisor
3. Marketplace intelligence
4. Professional judgment
5. Decision support
6. Transition-readiness support (where appropriate)
7. Technology that strengthens all of the above

**Technology exists to strengthen consulting. Consulting never exists to
demonstrate technology.**

## Article V — The Trust Principle

**Technology is not what makes VYNE trustworthy. It is what helps VYNE earn trust
more consistently.**

## Article VI — Product Principles

How we build, always in service of the advisor's decision:

- **Understanding before recommendation.** A view is earned from understanding;
  it is never a form to complete.
- **Conviction, not readiness.** We measure our own confidence in our
  understanding. We never score the advisor.
- **Judgment, not automation.** The platform makes the consultant better; it
  never replaces the consultant's accountability.
- **Recommendations evolve, not flip.** Our view matures as we learn; it never
  lurches from silence to certainty.
- **No preferred destination.** Every recommendation must be willing to conclude
  that the advisor should **stay, move, wait, partner, acquire, sell, or pursue
  succession.** VYNE has no house answer.
- **The deliverable is a milestone in the relationship** — remembered for its
  insight, not its formatting.
- **Understanding gets deeper, not merely larger.** Every addition must improve
  our understanding of the advisor, or it does not belong.

## Article VII — The Decision Standard

When this Constitution does not explicitly answer a question, choose the option
that:

- Deepens understanding;
- Strengthens trust;
- Preserves consultant judgment;
- Protects advisor confidentiality;
- Improves long-term decision quality over short-term efficiency.

## Article VIII — Boundaries

What VYNE is *not*, and what it will not quietly become:

- VYNE is a consulting firm — **not a broker-dealer, custodian, transition
  vendor, software platform, or marketplace.**
- Advisor-provided information is **governed and verified**; it never
  automatically becomes firm-wide truth.
- VYNE's internal intelligence layer and future opportunity network are
  **internal consulting capabilities, not public products.**
- Advisors receive **meaningful value whether they share very little or engage in
  a deep, confidential review.**
- Adjacent practices — succession, acquisition, partnership, continuity — are
  **deepenings of our consulting practice, not separate businesses.**

## Article IX — Supremacy, Governance, and Amendment

- This Constitution is the **highest authority** at VYNE. In any conflict, the
  Constitution prevails over the Founder Alignment Memo, which prevails over the
  VYNE Story.
- **No roadmap item, feature request, marketing initiative, or website copy may
  contradict this Constitution.**
- **The governance gate:** every proposed feature or idea is first evaluated for
  alignment with this Constitution — *"Should VYNE build this at all?"* — before
  any discussion of *how* to build it.
- This Constitution changes only by **deliberate, dated amendment**, recorded in
  the Decision Log. Interpretation for the present day belongs in the Founder
  Alignment Memo; the narrative belongs in the VYNE Story. Neither may alter what
  is written here.
