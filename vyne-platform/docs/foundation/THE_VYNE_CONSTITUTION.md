# The VYNE Constitution

**Status:** RATIFIED — founder, 2026-07-23 · **Version:** 1.0
**Authority:** The highest document at VYNE. Everything — the Founder Alignment
Memo, the VYNE Story, the roadmap, the website, the brand, hiring, and every
product decision — derives from and must remain consistent with what follows. It
is written for everyone who will ever contribute to VYNE: employees, consultants,
contractors, partners, investors, and AI agents. Read it before you build, write,
design, or advise anything.

*These are enduring principles, not implementation. Each is meant to remain true
decades from now, even if technology, business models, and the market have
changed completely. This document changes only by deliberate amendment.*

---

## Purpose

This Constitution exists to preserve VYNE's identity, principles, and
decision-making philosophy as the company grows. Every strategy, product, hire,
and partnership should be evaluated against these principles before it is pursued.

## Preamble — Our Worldview

VYNE exists because the most important decisions deserve more than incentives,
assumptions, or incomplete information.

We believe that **better understanding leads to better decisions, and better
decisions create better long-term outcomes** — for advisors and for the people
they serve. Every principle that follows exists to support that belief.

## Principle 1 — Mission

Help financial advisors make better-informed decisions about the future of their
**businesses, clients, teams, and careers** — with greater clarity, confidence,
and trust.

We accomplish this by **institutionalizing great consulting**, so that
understanding, judgment, and market intelligence *compound within VYNE over time*
rather than disappearing with any individual. Institutionalizing consulting is
our method; the advisor's decision is our purpose.

## Principle 2 — Advisor First

Every significant product, research, technology, marketing, and business decision
must improve the **advisor's** ability to make a better-informed decision.

If a choice primarily benefits VYNE while adding little value to the advisor, it
must be reconsidered. Every principle below is downstream of this one.

## Principle 3 — Decisions Over Transactions

VYNE is measured by whether it helps advisors make **better decisions** — never
by placements, volume, or any single outcome. Sometimes the right decision is to
move; sometimes to stay; sometimes to wait; sometimes something else entirely.

This is the difference between a recruiting business and a consulting business,
and it is permanent.

## Principle 4 — What VYNE Is

VYNE's identity is that of an **independent consulting firm.** Our proprietary
technology and market intelligence exist to strengthen that identity — to make
our consultants more informed, more current, and more valuable to advisors —
never to replace it.

Three layers make VYNE work, and they must never be confused:

- **The company** is a consulting firm — who we are, and what we say.
- **The method** is how we deliver value consistently.
- **The internal platform** — our technology and intelligence — is our edge.

**The platform is our advantage. It is never our identity.**

## Principle 5 — The Hierarchy

Value at VYNE flows in this order, and technology sits last for a reason:

1. The consulting relationship
2. Understanding the advisor
3. Marketplace intelligence
4. Professional judgment
5. Decision support
6. Readiness support, where appropriate
7. Technology that strengthens all of the above

**Technology exists to strengthen consulting. Consulting never exists to
demonstrate technology.**

## Principle 6 — Trust

**Technology is not what makes VYNE trustworthy. It is what helps VYNE earn trust
more consistently.** Trust is earned in the relationship; everything else serves
that.

## Principle 7 — Evidence Before Opinion

Every recommendation, comparison, and conclusion rests on the best available
evidence, current market intelligence, and professional judgment.

When the evidence is incomplete, we **acknowledge the uncertainty rather than
hide it.** VYNE is neutral and intellectually honest before it is persuasive.

## Principle 8 — How We Reason

The enduring discipline of our advice, always in service of the advisor's
decision:

- **Understanding before recommendation** — a view is earned, never a form to
  complete.
- **We measure our own confidence, never the advisor.** The burden of proof is on
  us.
- **Judgment, not automation** — technology makes the consultant better; it never
  replaces the consultant's accountability.
- **Our view matures as we learn** — it never lurches from silence to certainty.
- **No preferred destination** — every recommendation must be willing to conclude
  stay, move, wait, or something else entirely.
- **What we deliver is a milestone in the relationship** — remembered for its
  insight, not its formatting.
- **Understanding deepens; it does not merely accumulate** — every addition must
  improve our understanding of the advisor, or it does not belong.

## Principle 9 — The Decision Standard

When this Constitution does not explicitly answer a question, choose the path
that:

- Deepens understanding;
- Strengthens trust;
- Preserves consultant judgment;
- Protects advisor confidentiality;
- Improves long-term decision quality over short-term efficiency;
- Remains intellectually honest about what is known and unknown.

## Principle 10 — What VYNE Will Not Become

To protect its independence and keep advice from becoming a transaction, VYNE
does not adopt structures that would compromise either:

- It is **not a broker-dealer, custodian, product vendor, or marketplace**, and
  it does not turn its consulting into a self-serve product.
- **Advisor-provided information is governed and verified**; it never
  automatically becomes firm-wide truth.
- VYNE's **internal intelligence is a consulting capability, not a public
  product.**
- Advisors receive meaningful value **whether they share very little or engage in
  a deep, confidential review.**
- Adjacent practices are **deepenings of the consulting practice, not separate
  businesses.**

## Governance — Supremacy, the Gate, and Amendment

- This Constitution is the **highest authority** at VYNE. In any conflict, it
  prevails over the Founder Alignment Memo, which prevails over the VYNE Story.
- **No roadmap item, feature request, marketing initiative, or website copy may
  contradict this Constitution.**
- **The gate.** Before building anything, we ask two questions, in order:
  1. *Should VYNE build this at all?*
  2. *If we build it, does it strengthen or weaken the consulting relationship?*

  Only what strengthens the consulting relationship proceeds.
- This Constitution is intended to **change rarely.** Amendments occur only when
  VYNE's enduring philosophy genuinely evolves — never because products, markets,
  competitors, or technology have changed. Every amendment must be **dated and
  accompanied by a written explanation of why the change was necessary**, recorded
  in the Decision Log and the Foundation Change Log. Present-day interpretation
  belongs in the Founder Alignment Memo; the narrative belongs in the VYNE Story.
  Neither may alter what is written here.

*If a future decision conflicts with these principles, the decision should
