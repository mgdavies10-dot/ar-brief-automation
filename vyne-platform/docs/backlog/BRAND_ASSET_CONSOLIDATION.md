## 3 · Prior founder decisions recovered — recorded, not re-decided

*Made in earlier sessions; never written into the controlled brand records.*

1. The full **VE + VYNE + STRATEGIES** lockup is the **formal primary identity**.
2. **VE alone** is preferred for hats, shirts, icons and compact applications.
3. **Bronze is restrained functional brand language** and should generally sit **outside the core mark**.
4. The **heavier V stroke** may represent **foundation and experience**.
5. The **thinner directional element** may represent **opportunity and movement**.
6. The **horizontal bars should be elevated approximately 10–15%**.
7. The mark should feel **vine-like conceptually** — through growth, branching, connection, direction and
   convergence.
8. **No literal leaves, vines, foliage or botanical decoration.**
9. **Logo Option 1 remains the primary-mark direction** unless the founder expressly changes it.

> Decision 6 is a **change to the mark's geometry that the current asset does not reflect.** It cannot be
> applied inside this repository — see §5.

## 6 · Current implementation position

The local website uses **`packages/ui/assets/brand/vyne-logo-primary.png`** — the existing approved
repository asset — strictly within `LOGO_USAGE.md`: on white or ivory only · no recolouring · no cropping
· no tracing · no redrawing · **no extracting the monogram from the image.**

**This is the current approved implementation asset. It is not evidence that the production logo package
is complete.**

**Consequence, stated plainly:** because the asset has no transparency and may not be reversed, **the logo
cannot appear on any navy surface.** Navy sections therefore carry no mark. That is an asset limitation
showing through the design, and it resolves when the reversed variant exists.
