## Governing principles (new — must govern all future research)

1. **Open research universe.** *Named organizations are examples, research sources, competitors,
   platforms, or potential destinations — **not** a closed recommendation set.*
2. **Interpret destination content through the provider's commercial model.** *A provider's
   educational content may be valuable while still commercially shaped; it reveals **questions**,
   not neutral or complete **answers**, and never substitutes for independent, advisor-specific
   comparison.*
3. **The outcome set is open, with no default.** Stay · wait · renegotiate/restructure in place ·
   move (employee) · independent BD · supported independence · join an RIA · launch an RIA ·
   affiliate with an enterprise/platform · merge · sell · acquire · succession · retire · or another
   evidence-supported structure. **No destination, channel, firm, custodian, buyer, or model is the
   default.**

## The three groups (relationship classified *by journey stage*, not one global label)

- **Group A — Direct / near-direct VYNE competitors:** help advisors evaluate *multiple* firms,
  channels, structures, offers, or career decisions.
- **Group B — Advisor-question / content / AEO reference sources:** included for what their public
  content reveals about advisor questions and answer formats — *not* automatically competitors.
- **(Destinations / platforms / implementation providers)** live in the *open* universe (principle
  3); researched selectively when they reveal questions or become relevant to a specific engagement.

*An organization can be a competitor at one stage and complementary at another* (e.g., a legal
provider competes at Curiosity/Evaluation but is a referral partner at Implementation).

- **Claims:** "unbiased advice," a "curated list," a "side-by-side proforma… removing guesswork and
  emotion." Proof: **1,200+ advisors, $36B+ AUM, $365M+ GDC placed.**
- **Compensation / conflict (neutral disclosure classification):** *Clearly disclosed* — free to

  VYNE's *intended* firm-paid model. Both claim to be advisor-first and unbiased. **VYNE cannot
  differentiate on the claim.** Its real differentiators must be structural and demonstrated:
  current-firm-as-a-real-alternative, *open* (non-contracted) analysis, evidence provenance,

## The three-layer question model (apply to every provider-sourced question)

**Provider-specific → market-wide → advisor-specific interpretive.** Examples:
- *"Does Dynasty act as my employer?"* → *"In supported independence, who employs the advisor/team and
  who owns the business?"* → *"Do I want the ownership and operating responsibilities of this model?"*
- *"What payout does this firm offer?"* → *"How do payout, expenses, platform fees, transition
  assistance, and ownership differ across models?"* → *"Which economic structure produces the best
  long-term result for MY business after costs, risk, and ownership?"*

This keeps the Library from becoming a collection of firm-marketing questions.

## Findings that challenge VYNE (preserved)

1. **"Unbiased" is category language — and the moat is the *method*, not the money** (founder ruling
   2026-07-25). "Unbiased / advisor-centric / firm-agnostic" is universal; Successful OnBoarding shows
   a *free-to-advisor, firm-paid* model markets itself exactly as VYNE intends. The correct conclusion
   is **not** that the compensation model is the differentiator (or the gate) — it is that **the claim
   differentiates nobody.** VYNE's moat is **how it reaches conclusions** — the *combination* no one
   else fields: Constitution + Practice Transition Assessment + The VYNE Standard + Advisor Journey +
   Messaging + Decision Question Library + evidence classification + confidence levels + unknowns +
   alternatives + provenance + a genuine willingness to recommend *stay / wait / any* legitimate
   destination. **The business model is ONE dimension of trust that *supports* the method — not the
   differentiator, and not gating.** (A CPA, banker, attorney, and broker each have a compensation
   model; reputation rests on trusted judgment, not on who pays.) **Update (2026-07-25):** VYNE's core
   model is now founder-established — advisor-free, **hiring-firm-paid, success-based** (~8%–16% of
   verified T12/TTMP). VYNE therefore **must not** claim to be unbiased/neutral/conflict-free; it
   *manages and discloses* real incentives via demonstrated method (see
   `../commercial/BUSINESS_MODEL_AND_COMMERCIAL_PRINCIPLES.md`).
2. **Question-led is not unique** — Winthrop already ships assessments + a 100-question bank. VYNE
   must win on advisor-**specific** depth, not the format.
3. **Competitors show real strengths** — placement proof (Successful OnBoarding), market data
   (Diamond), M&A/valuation rigor and research (DeVoe/MarshBerry), AEO craft (Winthrop). Neutral, and
   worth respecting.
