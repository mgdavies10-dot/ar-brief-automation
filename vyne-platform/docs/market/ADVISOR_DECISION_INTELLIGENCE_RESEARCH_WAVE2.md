## 2. The finding, now strongly evidenced

**"Advisor-centric / unbiased / firm-agnostic" is universal category language — not
differentiation.** Four independent competitors all lead with it: Diamond (advisor-side
consulting), **Bridgemark** ("unbiased research; Feel, Fit, Financials"), **Elite** ("unbiased
advice"), **Spartan** ("we work for the advisor, not the firm; firm agnostic"). `[E — established
pattern]`
([Bridgemark](https://www.bridgemarkstrategies.com/about/the-bridgemark-strategies-advantage/) ·
[Elite](https://eliteconsultingpartners.com/about-us/) ·
[Spartan](https://spartan-advisory.com/))

**Implication:** VYNE cannot differentiate by *claiming* independence. It must differentiate on
**demonstrated decision quality** — evidence + provenance (fact / assumption / firm-claim / VYNE
judgment), compensation/conflict disclosure, shown reasoning, and *calibrated* recommendations. As
Wave 1 put it: *stop selling "independent"; prove the method.* This sharpens — does not contradict —
the frozen Messaging/Brand work.

## 3. Primary-decision taxonomy (new — the `primary decision supported` tag)

The advisor decision is not one question. Tag every library question with the **primary decision**
it serves:

- **Stay** — as-is · **stay & renegotiate** · **restructure within current firm**
- **Move (employee)** — wire↔wire · to **regional/boutique** · to **independent BD**
- **Independence** — **start own RIA** · **supported independence / join a platform** (Dynasty,
  Sanctuary, Equitable)
- **Join** an existing RIA or **team**; **form a partnership / merge teams**
- **Sell** the practice (external) · **internal succession** · **acquire** a practice
- **Retire / wind down** · **wait / revisit after milestones**

Each carries its own question cluster (below), and the *same book* should be evaluated across
several — which is precisely the integrated analysis the Practice Transition Assessment provides.

## 4. Expanded question set (new, evidenced — fold into the Library)

**Sell / valuation** `[E]`: *What is my practice actually worth? · Revenue multiple or EBITDA
multiple? (1.9–3.0x revenue; ~9.98x EBITDA, 2026) · Do favorable terms beat a higher headline
multiple? · What makes my book more valuable (recurring revenue, transferability, client age)?*
([SmartAsset](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/how-to-value-a-financial-advisors-book-of-business) ·
[Advisor Legacy](https://advisorlegacy.com/blog/understanding-valuation-multiples-for-a-financial-advisor-practice))

**Succession** `[E]`: *Internal successor or external sale? · Does my internal candidate have the
capital/skill/trust? · How long a runway do I need (5–10 yrs)? · Minority-stake/hybrid to keep
control?*
([RightCapital](https://www.rightcapital.com/blog/succession-planning-financial-advisors/) ·
[buyAUM](https://buyaum.com/resources/internal-vs-external-succession-planning-for-financial-advisors/))

**Acquire** `[E]`: *Can I finance this (SBA/conventional/seller note, 80–100%)? · Do I have cash
flow for payments? · How do I value the target and structure contingencies (holdback, client-stay
period, escrow)?*
([Capital Bank](https://capitalbankmd.com/resources/articles/buying-book-of-business-financial-advisors/) ·
[Succession Resource](https://www.successionresource.com/how-to-acquire-a-financial-practice/))

**Independence / supported** `[E]`: *Do I want to run a business or serve clients? · What's my real
net after infrastructure? · Fully independent vs. a platform (Dynasty/Sanctuary)? · Does the model
preserve ownership and enterprise value?*
([Dynasty](https://www.dynastyfinancialpartners.com/advisors/join-ria/))

**Changing broker-dealer** `[E]`: *Payout · technology · portfolio-management flexibility ·
compliance support · transition resources.*
([Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/long-list-considerations-when-changing-broker-dealers))

## 7. AI-sufficiency × personalization matrix

| **AI alone suffices** | valuation-multiple ranges, deal mechanics, Reg S-P basics, succession structures *(concede)* | — | — |

## 9. Which questions are best answered by…

- **Public factual content:** valuation ranges, deal mechanics, Reg S-P basics, succession models,
  BD-change considerations *(AEO/education; concede where AI already wins; add interpretation)*.
- **Interpretive content:** "does independence fit me?", "counteroffer?", "what does this trend mean
  for me?" *(articles/guides that reason, don't just list)*.
- **Tools / frameworks:** self-assessment of objectives, a comparison scaffold *(later; no design
  now)*.
- **Personalized assessment:** portability, economics, enterprise value, whole-business impact *(the
  Practice Transition Assessment)*.
- **Human consultation:** the calibrated recommendation, confidential nuance, emotional weight.

## 10. Implications for downstream Phase-1 work (no changes to frozen foundation)

- **Messaging:** lead with *demonstrated method*, not "independent" (now strongly evidenced).
- **Content Architecture:** organize by primary decision × question; concede commoditized facts,
  own interpretation and integration.
- **AEO:** target interpretation/integration queries ("what happens to **my** business if I…"),
  not "best recruiter" or factual queries AI already wins.
- **Lead-Capture:** value-first (education → self-assessment → assessment → consultation), mirroring
  graduated disclosure.
- **Consultation:** embody the independent wisdom (objectives over economics; never move for a
  bonus / stay for a counteroffer) as trust-building.

## 11. First-party intelligence plan (method only — no software/DB)

Once VYNE operates, the most valuable evidence is its own consultations. A privacy-conscious method:
a **consultation-note taxonomy** and **inbound-question tagging** using the library's fields;
**anonymized question extraction** (themes/decision patterns only — **never client PII in the
research library**); explicit **advisor consent** for any reuse; **periodic library reviews**;
tracking **question frequency, journey progression, and outcome** (stay / pause / move / no-decision).
Define governance and consent first; build nothing yet.

## 12. Recommendation — sufficient to begin Content Architecture?

**Yes, with two known, non-blocking gaps.** The library is now validated across the core transition
decision *and* the adjacent decisions (stay, sell, succession, acquire, independence), with a
primary-decision taxonomy and a strongly-evidenced positioning correction. The two remaining gaps —
**measured search/community demand** and **first-party data** — are **additive and ongoing**, not
prerequisites for structuring content. **Recommendation:** freeze the expanded library and proceed to
**Content Architecture**, *unless* the founder wants demand *quantification* first — in which case a
**narrow Wave 3** (autocomplete/PAA/Trends + direct community threads) is the right, bounded next step.
Either is defensible; I'd proceed and add the demand layer in parallel.
