# Advisor Decision Intelligence Research (first pass)

**Status:** Research & synthesis — **first evidence-grounded pass**, to be extended ·
**Date:** 2026-07-25 · **Type:** Operational (competitive/decision intelligence). Bound by
the Constitution and delivered to The VYNE Standard.

> **Honest method note (read first).** A true institutional study of *demand* would measure
> live search volumes, AI-prompt frequencies, and forum/community data — which this analyst
> **cannot** measure from here. What follows is grounded in **real, cited public sources**
> retrieved 2026-07-25 (competitor sites, trade press, Kitces, checklists), plus **analyst
> synthesis** where sources are indirect. Every claim is tagged **[E]** evidenced (with a
> source) or **[H]** hypothesis (analyst judgment, to validate). No "demand scores" are
> fabricated. This is a strong foundation, not a finished study.

## 1. Executive summary

- The market **validates VYNE's core thesis** — information/access is commoditizing;
  *interpretation and advisor-specific judgment* are the durable value. **[E]** Even a
  leading competitor (Diamond) has repositioned toward "control, growth, business worth,
  optionality, and enterprise value." ([Diamond Consultants](https://www.diamond-consultants.com/))
- The market **also challenges VYNE's positioning.** *Every* serious competitor already
  claims to be **advisor-centric, unbiased, and judgment-driven** — Diamond, and
  **Bridgemark ("unbiased research," "Feel, Fit, Financials")**. **[E]**
  ([Bridgemark](https://www.bridgemarkstrategies.com/about/the-bridgemark-strategies-advantage/))
  So *the claim* of independence is **table-stakes, not differentiation.** VYNE must win on
  **demonstrated method** (The VYNE Standard, provenance, portability depth, calibrated
  conclusions), not on saying "independent." **[H]**
- The best-covered questions are **factual/legal** — Kitces owns client-data/Broker-Protocol
  portability; trade press owns deal-structure mechanics. **[E]** The **least-owned** space is
  a **confidential, advisor-specific, whole-business decision analysis** — no competitor was
  found publishing a personalized *"what happens to my business"* assessment. **[H, white
  space]**
- The strongest recurring, independent wisdom in the market maps **directly onto the
  Constitution**: *never move for a bonus, never stay for a counteroffer; decide on
  goals/quality-of-life, not incentives.* **[E]**
  ([RAMAX](https://ramaxsearch.com/the-counteroffer-the-most-dangerous-word-in-financial-services/))
  Kitces: those who break away *purely for grid points* end up disappointed. **[E]**
  ([Kitces via synthesis](https://www.kitces.com/blog/podcast-topic/going-independent/))

## 2. Research methodology

**This pass (evidenced):** targeted web search + source review across trade press
(WealthManagement, AdvisorHub, Financial Planning), Kitces, competitor sites (Diamond,
Bridgemark, Winthrop), destination/independence resources (Fidelity, Altruist), transition
checklists, and legal/compliance sources (FINRA, SEC Reg S-P). Findings paraphrased; sources
cited inline and in §9.

**The fuller methodology (to run over time, per the founder's source list):**
- **Advisor demand** — Google/AI-assistant prompt patterns, Reddit, Quora, advisor forums,
  LinkedIn, Kitces, AdvisorHub, WealthManagement, Financial Planning, ThinkAdvisor, FA
  Magazine, podcasts, conference agendas. *(Requires analytics/community access this pass
  did not have — flagged.)*
- **Competitor analysis** — structured audits of Diamond, Bridgemark, Elite Consulting
  Partners, Spartan, Successful Onboarding, Winthrop; destination-firm transition pages (LPL,
  Raymond James, Rockefeller, Sanctuary, RBC, Morgan Stanley, UBS, Merrill, Ameriprise,
  Cetera, Commonwealth, Dynasty, Carson).
- **Decision landscape** — organize questions into decision categories (§3).
- **AI opportunity** — classify each question by whether AI alone suffices, AI helps but
  can't personalize, or only confidential consulting can answer (§7 framework, dimension).

**Limits (Evidence Before Opinion):** no live demand measurement; competitor coverage below
is based on public positioning sampled this pass, not exhaustive audits; several competitors
(Elite, Spartan, Successful Onboarding) were not yet reviewed. Treat coverage as directional.

## 3. The decision landscape (major advisor decisions)

Grounded categories (each seen recurring across sources): **Should I move? · Should I stay?
· Timing · Client portability · Team · Economics (deal + long-term) · Ownership & enterprise
value · Succession · Independence vs. supported vs. employee · Channel selection · Culture &
fit · Technology & platform · Operations & compliance burden · Taxes (high-level) ·
Confidentiality & legal (Reg S-P, Protocol, non-solicit) · Risk & due diligence ·
Negotiation · Transition execution · Life after transition.** **[E/H]** These match — and
slightly expand — the Advisor Journey categories.

## 4. Expanded Decision Question Library (research-surfaced additions)

New questions found in the wild, to fold into `../experience/DECISION_QUESTION_LIBRARY.md`
(each with grounding):

- **"What happens to my book if I leave the *new* firm?"** — the exit-options question
  advisors most often forget; gives leverage. **[E]**
  ([VantageImpact](https://www.vantageimpact.com/news/what-financial-advisors-need-to-know-before-changing-firms))
- **"Can I legally take my clients — what does Reg S-P / Broker Protocol / non-solicit
  actually allow?"** and **"Do I need affirmative client consent?"** **[E]**
  ([Kitces](https://www.kitces.com/blog/regulation-s-p-nonpublic-personal-information-broker-protocol-data-privacy-laws-adviser-compliance-issues-bringing-clients-new-firm-legal/))
- **"How does a forgivable-loan / transition deal actually work — and how is it taxed?"**
  (150–300% of revenue; ordinary income; 9–13 yr commitment; repay if you leave early). **[E]**
  ([WealthManagement](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/professional-development/beyond-the-forgivable-loan-the-evolution-of-transition-deal-structures),
  [AdvisorHub](https://www.advisorhub.com/resources/advisor-recruitment-the-bull-and-bear-case-for-a-forgivable-loan/))
- **"Should I take the counteroffer if my firm makes one?"** — with the strong independent
  answer: don't stay for a retention bonus. **[E]**
  ([RAMAX](https://ramaxsearch.com/the-counteroffer-the-most-dangerous-word-in-financial-services/))
- **"Is the extra payout actually worth it after I run my own infrastructure?"** (wirehouse
  ~45% vs. independent ~70% take-home). **[E]** ([InvestmentNews/Altruist](https://www.investmentnews.com/advisor-tech/altruist-launches-a-corporate-ria-to-ease-breakaway-advisors-path-to-independence-and-to-its-custody-platform/267160))
- **"Do I actually want independence, or do I just want better economics?"** (the two are
  different; wanting independence predicts success). **[E]** ([Kitces](https://www.kitces.com/blog/podcast-topic/going-independent/))
- **"How long will this really take, and when does revenue dip?"** (~2 yrs from idea to move;
  30–60 day revenue dip). **[E]**
  ([Integrated FG](https://integrated-financial-group.com/resources/going-independent-as-a-financial-planner-should-i-stay-or-should-i-go/))
- **"Who should I talk to who recently made this move?"** (reference due diligence). **[E]**
  ([SmartAsset](https://smartasset.com/advisor-resources/financial-advisor-transition-checklist))
- **"Do I want to *run a business*, or just serve clients?"** (the supported-independence
  fork: Dynasty/Sanctuary/Steward). **[E]** (Altruist/Kitces synthesis)

## 6. Advisor question gap analysis (highest-value unowned)

The valuable, unowned questions are the ones **AI and generic content cannot answer for a
specific advisor**:

1. *"How much of MY book realistically transfers to THIS firm/channel?"* **[H — white space]**
2. *"What actually happens to MY business — clients, team, economics, ownership, enterprise
   value — under each option?"* **[H — white space]**
3. *"Given my objectives and my book, should I stay, move, wait, or something else?"* **[H]**
4. *"Which of these firm claims should I believe, and what must I verify?"* **[E-ish]**
5. *"How confident can I be, and what would change the answer?"* **[H]**

These are exactly the questions VYNE's method is built to answer — and the ones competitors
either can't (destination firms), won't (conflicted recruiters), or don't (generic content).

## 7. Decision Intelligence Framework (proposed taxonomy)

A framework to classify **every** advisor question — recommended dimensions, **no arbitrary
scoring invented yet** (calibration is future work):

- **Demand** — how commonly asked (measured later; not guessed now).
- **Journey stage** — Curiosity → … → Long-term.
- **Conversation** — internal / external / interpretive.
- **Complexity** — simple / moderate / complex.
- **Personalization required** — generic vs. advisor-specific vs. book-specific.
- **Interpretation required** — factual vs. judgment.
- **Confidential information required** — public / redacted / confidential (ties to graduated
  disclosure).
- **Emotional weight** — low / moderate / high.
- **Competitive coverage** — well-owned / partially / unowned.
- **AI sufficiency** — *AI alone suffices · AI helps but can't personalize · only confidential
  consulting can answer responsibly* (this dimension pinpoints VYNE's opportunity).
- **Strategic value to VYNE** — a *derived* judgment (high when: high personalization +
  interpretation + confidentiality + low competitive coverage), never a standalone score.

Rule: VYNE prioritizes questions that are **advisor-specific, interpretation-heavy,
confidentiality-dependent, and poorly covered** — precisely where AI and competitors can't
win.

## 8. Recommendations (only where evidence supports)

- **Decision Question Library:** fold in the §4 additions (evidenced). Add the *AI-sufficiency*
  and *personalization* tags to every question — they are the sharpest predictors of VYNE
  value. **[E→action]**
- **Messaging Architecture:** **do not rely on "independent" as differentiation** — every
  competitor claims it. Shift the proof burden to *demonstrated method* (The VYNE Standard,
  provenance, "what applies to your book"). This sharpens, not contradicts, the frozen doc.
  **[E]**
- **Content Architecture:** prioritize the *unowned, advisor-specific* questions (§6);
  **don't** compete on commoditized checklists or re-explaining Reg S-P — *interpret* those
  for the individual. **[E]**
- **AEO Strategy:** concede the factual queries (AI/Kitces already win) and own the
  *interpretation* queries ("what happens to **my** business if I move?"). **[E/H]**
- **Consultation Experience:** the "stay/counteroffer/objectives-over-economics" wisdom is
  well-established and independent — VYNE should *embody* it (it validates No Preferred
  Destination and Advisor First), and lead with it as trust-building, not novelty. **[E]**
- **Practice Transition Assessment naming:** market publishes *reports* and *checklists*, not
  personalized whole-business *assessments* — the category name is genuinely open; keep it
  working-title. **[H]**
