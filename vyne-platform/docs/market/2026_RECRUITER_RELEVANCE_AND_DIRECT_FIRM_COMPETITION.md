## 6. Recruiting capabilities becoming commoditized

Increasingly *less defensible* as standalone value:

- Providing lists of firms.
- Collecting publicly available deal information.
- Making introductions.
- Repeating firm marketing claims.
- Producing generic comparisons.
- Matching advisors to firms primarily because those firms pay a fee.
- Claiming that information *access* alone is proprietary value.

## 7. Consulting capabilities that remain durable

VYNE does not need to own the raw information advantage. VYNE must own the:

- **Interpretation advantage** — what the information *means*.
- **Independent comparison advantage** — consistent standards across options.
- **Verification advantage** — testing firm claims.
- **Advisor-specific analysis advantage** — applied to *this* book.
- **Portability advantage** — how much realistically transfers.
- **Professional-judgment advantage** — measured, disclosed, accountable.
- **Decision-quality advantage** — better decisions, not more content.

## 8. Category expectations competitors already claim

Destination firms (e.g. LPL Financial, Raymond James, and others) increasingly offer
strong websites, AEO strategies, detailed capability content, affiliation-model
explanations, payout information, recruiting calculators / illustrative economics,
and direct online advisor-intake. Advisors can research firms, understand basic
capabilities, submit their business, communicate with recruiting teams, and get
preliminary economics **without an outside recruiter.** VYNE must assume the advisor
arrives *already informed* and often *already leaning.*

## 9. VYNE's defensible differentiation

VYNE does not out-explain LPL about LPL, or Raymond James about Raymond James. VYNE
**independently determines what those platforms, economics, restrictions, and
capabilities mean for a particular advisor and their actual business** — which claims
matter, which require verification, how alternatives compare under consistent
standards, how much of the book is realistically portable, and whether the advisor
should move at all (including *stay*).

## 10. Direct-firm digital recruiting & AEO as a major competitive force

This is treated as a first-order force, not a footnote. The advisor's research —
through AI/search, firm sites, direct recruiter conversations, calculators, and
preliminary proposals — is real and often complete on the basics. **VYNE's posture is
not to dismiss it but to make it rigorous:** help the advisor *test* what they've
gathered against consistent, independent standards and against their own book.

## 11. Why Book Portability is central

A proposal built on current T12, revenue, or assets is incomplete without a realistic
view of **how much of the business could actually move** to a specific firm/channel.
Portability is precisely where the destination firm cannot be objective and the
advisor most needs independent judgment. It is defined as a core VYNE Method
capability across six dimensions (client, asset, product/program, revenue,
operational, capability), under graduated disclosure, with confidence ranges — see
[`../method/BOOK_PORTABILITY.md`](../method/BOOK_PORTABILITY.md).

## 12. What should change in Phase 1 — and what should not

**Change (Phase 1):**
- The Digital Experience Strategy now assumes an *already-informed, already-leaning*
  advisor; adds a **Perspective** stage (understand what applies *before* deciding),
  a **direct-firm digital path** (test the advisor's research), and a **self-directed
  path** (education/frameworks that show when consulting adds value).
- **Book Portability** becomes a central, defined method capability.
- Positioning emphasizes **interpretation, verification, comparison, portability, and
  judgment** over information access.

**Do not change (frozen):**
- The foundation — Constitution, Founder Alignment Memo, VYNE Story — is untouched.
- VYNE remains a **high-touch, independent advisor-transition consultancy** (not a
  broker-dealer, custodian, vendor, marketplace, or self-serve platform).
- No preferred destination; Evidence Before Opinion; Advisor First.

## Governing conclusion (preserve)

*VYNE remains on the correct path as a high-touch advisor-transition consultancy. The
competitive challenge is no longer simply access to firms or information. VYNE must
help advisors determine what information applies to their actual businesses, compare
firms and channels under consistent standards, test firm claims, evaluate how much of
the book is realistically portable, understand the financial and operational
consequences, and make a decision that is supported by evidence and professional
judgment.*
