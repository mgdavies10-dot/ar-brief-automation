# Founder Decisions Required Before Production

Claude should track these explicitly and never guess silently.

## Business and product
- Final public legal entity and disclosures
- Whether recruiters are employees, contractors, or both
- Exact role hierarchy and which roles may approve publication
- Whether advisors may invite team members or only VYNE may provision access
- Whether advisors can comment, upload, approve, or only view
- Final commercial opportunity stages
- Final decision-readiness states
- Retention period for advisor and prospect records

## Technology
- Preferred hosting and database vendors
- Domain names and subdomains
- Authentication method and MFA policy
- Email provider
- File storage provider
- Whether e-signature is needed
- Whether calendar and email synchronization are in initial scope
- Whether the current single-file CRM contains data that must be migrated

## Modeling
- Canonical formulas and source priority
- Tax treatment assumptions and disclaimer language
- Allowed model users and approval rights
- Whether advisors may edit assumptions directly
- Required hand-check cases

## Brand and content
- Final logo asset and vector file
- Final typography licenses or approved web fonts
- Final public service names and copy
- Final privacy policy, terms, and regulatory disclosures

## Security and compliance
- Data classification policy
- Incident-response owner
- Backup and recovery objectives
- External cybersecurity review requirements
- Legal and privacy review requirements
