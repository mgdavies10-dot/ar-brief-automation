01 Executive Build Decision
Recommendation: build a focused authority and conversion MVP - not the full platform.
The first website must prove that advisors will discover VYNE, trust the methodology, complete an assessment, subscribe, and request a confidential conversation. It should not attempt to launch the full portability engine, public marketplace, custom CRM, and verified firm database simultaneously.
The website has two jobs: first, become a credible research destination for financial advisors who prefer private due diligence; second, convert the right visitors into identifiable, permissioned relationships without making the site feel like a recruiter qualification funnel.
Neutral constraints
AEO and organic search can compound, but they are unlikely to produce predictable qualified cases immediately.
Requiring CRD, T12 and AUM too early will improve verification but reduce conversion and trust.
Publishing deep firm intelligence before VYNE has reliable, dated and legally reviewable data creates credibility and liability risk.
A founder-light launch may be workable; a permanently anonymous advisory brand is likely to face a trust ceiling.
The public site should never imply that an assessment can determine whether an advisor should move or guarantee portability.

02 Product Principles and Success Criteria
Product principles
Advisor-first, not firm-first. The site begins with the advisor’s clients, objectives, economics and future - not a roster of recruiting firms.
Public intelligence, private conclusion. General education remains crawlable; personalized analysis requires a relationship and permission.
Progressive disclosure. Ask only for the information needed for the next level of value.
Confidence through specificity. Pages should explain the process, questions, evidence and limitations rather than rely on broad claims.
No manufactured urgency. Calls to action emphasize confidential research and decision clarity.
Every high-intent action creates a structured CRM record, next action and consent trail.
Every data point has a source date, confidence level and permitted use.

Gating rules
Do not require CRD to read public research or subscribe to the newsletter.
Do not reveal proprietary or advisor-supplied firm intelligence merely because a user enters a CRD.
Do not accept client names or personally identifiable book data through ordinary web forms.

A verified identity does not equal consent to contact a firm or submit an advisor.
Each deeper-access request should state what will happen next and whether a human review is required.

06 Global User Experience and Brand Requirements
Experience requirements
Premium, restrained design: institutional rather than flashy, with generous whitespace and limited motion.
Mobile-first assessment and forms; most interactive actions must be completable on a phone.
Every page should answer the advisor’s question above the fold before asking for information.
Persistent but quiet CTA: Start a confidential conversation.
No pop-up that blocks reading on the first visit. Newsletter prompts appear after meaningful engagement or at article end.
Plain-language confidentiality cues near every high-intent form.
Firm pages display last-reviewed dates and distinguish public facts from VYNE analysis.
Accessibility: keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic headings, sufficient contrast, form labels and error messaging.
