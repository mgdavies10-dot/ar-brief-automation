V Y N E S T R A T E G I E S
VOLUME V
ADVISOR DELIVERABLES TOOLKIT
Branded templates that every VYNE advisor engagement produces.
Purpose
This toolkit defines the advisor-facing documents that represent the VYNE experience. Every deliverable should carry consistent VYNE branding, version control, and a concise executive presentation style.

Presentation Standards
Every report begins with a one-page executive summary.
Every recommendation identifies assumptions and uncertainties.
Every model separates guaranteed, contingent and estimated values.
Every document contains version number and preparation date.
No internal CRM notes or recruiter compensation appear in advisor reports.
Future Digital Experience
Each document should ultimately be generated automatically from VYNE OS and published into Advisor Studio. The branded PDF becomes both the advisor deliverable and the permanent engagement record.
