V Y N E S T R A T E G I E S
Canonical Architecture & Language Guide
The control layer for a coherent institution
GOVERNING PRINCIPLE
VYNE exists to improve the quality of consequential career and business decisions made by financial advisors.
VERSION 0.1 | JULY 2026 | CONFIDENTIAL — INTERNAL USE ONLY
1. Why This Guide Exists
VYNE has enough ideas, frameworks, policies, technology specifications and brand work to create institutional strength—or institutional confusion. This guide establishes the control layer. It determines which concepts sit above others, which language VYNE uses, which language it avoids, and how future work is evaluated before it becomes part of the company.
THE DISCIPLINE
No new framework, product, page, agent, policy or campaign becomes canonical merely because it is compelling. It must fit the architecture, improve decision quality and strengthen trust.
2. Permanent Definition of VYNE
2.1 Purpose
VYNE exists to improve the quality of consequential career and business decisions made by financial advisors.
2.2 Institutional Positioning
VYNE is an independent strategic advisory firm that helps financial advisors understand where they are, define what they want, evaluate available paths and act with greater clarity, evidence and conviction.
2.3 Brand Promise
A better decision than the advisor would have made alone.
2.4 Commercial Reality
Recruiting and placement can be important services and revenue outcomes. They do not define the institution. VYNE may recommend staying, strengthening, preparing or moving. The firm earns trust because the answer is not predetermined.
2.5 Technology Reality
Technology improves rigor, responsiveness, institutional memory and scale. It remains infrastructure. VYNE should be known for how it thinks—not for which AI tools it uses.

4. The Role of VYNE's 9
VYNE's 9 is not a cosmetic rename of the Eight Questions. It is the primary public intellectual property of the firm and must become a complete decision system.
Nine questions should cover the advisor's current reality, desired future, client needs, platform fit, economics, ownership, risk, readiness and conviction.
The questions must support a stay, strengthen, prepare or move outcome without bias.
The assessment may score readiness and tension, but it must not manufacture false precision.
Results should lead to education and reflection before requesting identifying information.
The CRM handoff must preserve consent, source, answers, confidence and the advisor's chosen next step.
MIGRATION RULE
Preserve valuable scoring, branching and CRM logic from the Eight Questions specification. Retire the old name and any language that makes the product feel like lead qualification disguised as advice.
5. AEO and the Trust Acquisition System
AEO is central to VYNE's growth model because advisors increasingly begin consequential decisions in private research. VYNE should be present when the question begins—not only when a recruiter starts calling.
5.1 Strategic Formula
GROWTH DOCTRINE
AEO is the discovery engine. Trust is the strategy. Qualified confidential conversations are the conversion objective.
5.2 The Trust Flywheel
01. Advisor asks a high-intent question.
02. VYNE publishes the strongest neutral answer, framework, evidence or original research.
03. Search and AI systems surface or cite VYNE.
04. The advisor enters the website through an answer—not a sales pitch.
05. VYNE's 9, a calculator, a brief or the newsletter earns a deeper permission.
06. Repeated value builds familiarity and confidence.
07. A confidential conversation begins when the advisor is ready.
08. The advisor's outcome creates anonymized learning that improves future research.
5.3 Channel Balance
AEO should not be forced to carry Year 1 revenue alone. The launch system should combine:
Founder relationships and discreet direct outreach
Professional and advisor referrals
AEO, original research and newsletter nurture
5.4 What VYNE Measures
Priority-question visibility and citations
Qualified organic sessions rather than raw traffic
Newsletter engagement and subscriber quality
Assessment completion and next-step selection
Confidential conversations influenced by content
Time from first touch to relationship
Revenue and non-move outcomes influenced by authority

7. Canonical Decisions From the Source Audit
Company identity
VYNE is the institution; recruiting is a service capability and monetization path.
Prevents the placement model from narrowing the brand.
Primary public methodology
Public hierarchy is VYNE → VYNE Framework → VYNE's 9. ROOT/BRANCH/GROW remain guided internal/client-process tools.
Creates one memorable public architecture.
Assessment name
VYNE's 9 replaces the Eight Questions. Existing scoring and branching logic must be migrated, not merely renamed.
Preserves good product logic while creating ownable IP.
AEO role
AEO is a core compounding acquisition engine. Trust remains the strategy and qualified conversations remain the conversion objective.
Keeps traffic subordinate to authority and relationships.
Lead-generation mix
Launch with founder relationships, referrals and high-trust outreach while AEO compounds.
Protects near-term economics without weakening the long-term model.
Recruiter framework
Retain as an internal desk workflow only when useful. Do not market it as a competing firm-wide framework.
Avoids framework proliferation.
Post-transition promise
GROW must be a scoped service with clear eligibility, deliverables, duration and economics.
Avoids open-ended obligations and brand overreach.
CRM posture
Treat current CRM as a design prototype. Use no confidential advisor data until production authentication, encryption, backups, access controls and incident response exist.
Protects confidentiality and credibility.
Contractor control
Results expectations may remain; behavioral control, mandatory schedules and discipline language require counsel review.
Reduces classification exposure.
Relationship ownership
VYNE owns records, contractual rights and economics—not people. Stewardship rules govern allocation and protection.
Makes policy more defensible and respectful.
Volume numbering
Rename recruiter manuals into operating policies under People, Finance and Legal. Reserve permanent volume numbering for the institutional library.
Prevents future library confusion.
Brand role of technology
Technology is invisible infrastructure; 'agents prepare, humans decide.'
Makes the brand durable beyond tools.

9. The VYNE Test
Every future idea must pass the following test before it is approved:
Does it improve advisor decision quality?
Does it increase trust?
Does it simplify complexity for the advisor?
Does it belong at the correct altitude—brand, methodology, product, operation or technology?
Would a sophisticated advisor respect it?
Can the claim be supported?
Can VYNE deliver it consistently?
Does it remain right if VYNE grows to 100 employees?
Does it strengthen the institution rather than create another competing idea?
DEFAULT DECISION
When an idea is valuable but not ready, place it in the Incubator. Preservation does not require adoption.
10. Production Boundaries
No confidential advisor data in the single-file CRM prototype.
No public performance, market or platform claim without a source and review standard.
No automated external communication or consequential recommendation without human approval.
No recruiter compensation or contractor-control provision treated as final until counsel approves it.
No assessment scoring presented as objective truth when it is a directional decision aid.
No post-transition consulting promise without a defined scope, term, owner and economics.
