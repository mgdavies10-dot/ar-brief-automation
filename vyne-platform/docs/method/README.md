# The VYNE Method

**Type:** Operational — the repeatable consulting methodology that makes VYNE's
judgment consistent and institutional. **Not** a foundational document. Every method
capability is bound by the Constitution (especially Advisor First, Evidence Before
Opinion, Decisions Over Transactions, No Preferred Destination) and passes the
three-question review gate. This is how *institutionalizing great consulting*
becomes concrete.

## Professional Standards for VYNE Consultants *(registered — future work)*

A future operational document defining how VYNE consultants are expected to work, so
quality does not depend on which consultant an advisor meets. Expected to cover:
neutrality and no-preferred-destination discipline; confidentiality; how evidence,
firm claims, and professional judgment are distinguished and disclosed;
acknowledgment of uncertainty; the willingness to recommend staying; disclosure of
compensation and conflicts (with `docs/commercial/BUSINESS_MODEL_AND_COMMERCIAL_PRINCIPLES.md`);
graduated disclosure; and the boundaries of consulting judgment (no legal/suitability
advice; estimates not guarantees). *Not yet written — no code or UI.*
