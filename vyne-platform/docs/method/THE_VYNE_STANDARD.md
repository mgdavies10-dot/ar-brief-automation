# The VYNE Standard (preliminary, internal)

**Status:** Preliminary internal definition · **Date:** 2026-07-25
**Type:** Operational — the quality standard applied to *every material VYNE analysis
and recommendation.* Bound by the Constitution.

> **Internal working language, not approved external branding.** Whether "The VYNE
> Standard" ever becomes a public-facing term is a founder decision.

## What it is

The VYNE Standard is the bar every material analysis and recommendation must clear
before it reaches an advisor. It is how *Evidence Before Opinion* and *professional
judgment* become concrete and repeatable — so quality does not depend on which
consultant an advisor happens to work with.

## What every material recommendation must show

- **The advisor's objectives** it is serving.
- **Evidence**, with **source provenance** (verified fact · firm-provided claim ·
  public information · advisor-provided information · VYNE assumption · VYNE
  professional judgment · unresolved uncertainty).
- **Assumptions**, stated plainly.
- **Confidence level**, honestly calibrated.
- **Unknowns** — what we do not yet know.
- **Risks** — with likelihood, consequence, and mitigation.
- **Alternatives considered**, and the **tradeoffs** among them.
- **Conflicts and compensation disclosures.**
- **Why the conclusion follows** from the above.
- **What information could change the conclusion.**

## Calibration — the strongest conclusion the evidence supports

The Standard requires professional judgment *calibrated to the evidence.* VYNE is
willing to make an explicit recommendation, but **never expresses more certainty than
the evidence allows.** Governing principle: **VYNE gives the strongest conclusion the
evidence supports — no stronger and no weaker.** In practice this is expressed as one
of three levels — *decision-support conclusion · conditional recommendation · full
recommendation* — defined in the Practice Transition Assessment (Area 12). The advisor
always retains the final decision.

## What it reinforces

Advisor First · Evidence Before Opinion · No Preferred Destination · transparency ·
confidentiality · neutrality · professional judgment · the willingness to recommend
**staying** · intellectual honesty.

## What it does NOT claim

The VYNE Standard **does not claim to eliminate bias or uncertainty** — no honest
process can. It defines how VYNE **identifies, manages, discloses, and limits** them:
by naming assumptions, calibrating confidence, disclosing conflicts and compensation,
distinguishing evidence from judgment, and stating what would change the view. A
recommendation that hides uncertainty fails the Standard, however confident it sounds.

## Where it applies

Every material deliverable — most centrally the **VYNE Practice Transition
Assessment** (`PRACTICE_TRANSITION_ASSESSMENT.md`) and its Book Portability module —
is delivered *to* this Standard. It also governs the eventual **Professional Standards
for VYNE Consultants** (`README.md`), which operationalize it into how consultants
work.

## Not yet developed

The concrete mechanics — a confidence-calibration scale, a provenance-tagging
convention, review/QA checkpoints, and how the Standard is evidenced in a finished
deliverable — are **not yet defined** and are registered as future method work. They
should be built from real practice, not invented to look rigorous.
