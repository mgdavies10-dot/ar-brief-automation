# Recommendation & Decision Record — modular template

**Artifact:** 7A · Operating Artifact Sprint · **Version:** v0.1 · **Date:** 2026-07-25
**Status:** DRAFT for founder review

> ## Governing premise
> **The Recommendation & Decision Record states VYNE's professional judgment clearly, while preserving
> the distinction among VYNE's recommendation, the advisor's decision, and the advisor's separate
> authorization.**
>
> **The advisor-facing Record is concise and usable. The internal supporting record is rigorous and
> traceable. They are not the same document.**

## The three separations — the spine of this artifact

| | |
|---|---|
| **VYNE's recommendation** | what the evidence supports, in VYNE's professional judgment |
| **The advisor's decision** | what the advisor chose — recorded separately, and it does **not** retroactively change what VYNE recommended |
| **Authorization** | a **separate artifact**. This Record authorizes nothing. |

**Four modes of one modular template** — not four unrelated products:

1. **Decision-Support Record** — no directional recommendation
2. **Conditional Recommendation Record** — a direction, subject to stated conditions
3. **Full Recommendation Record** — an advisor-specific recommendation
4. **Decision Status and Closure Record** — the matter pauses or concludes

**Every advisor-facing page is designated `CONFIDENTIAL — ADVISOR-FACING`.**
**No advisor names in filenames** — engagement code only.

> **Never appears in the advisor-facing Record:** bare internal labels (Level 1/2/3, PTA module,
> materiality rating, evidence class, gate status) · gate fields · internal conflict analysis ·
> compensation status beside any option · fee percentages, amounts, step-ups, payment timing, clawbacks,
> firm-specific commercial terms · client PII · raw internal workpapers · any claim of being unbiased,
> neutral, conflict-free, or of having reviewed the whole market · "best firm" language · numeric
> confidence scores.

## §1 · Header and document identity — *required, all modes*

```
 CONFIDENTIAL — ADVISOR-FACING
 Engagement: E-__-___        Advisor: ______________________
 Decision being evaluated: _____________________________________________
 This Record is: ☐ Decision-Support  ☐ Conditional Recommendation
                 ☐ Full Recommendation  ☐ Decision Status and Closure
 Date: __________     Information current through: __________
 Version: ____        Supersedes: ____ (if applicable)
 Prepared by: ______________  Consultation completed with: ______________
 Approved by: ______________
 Level of detail we worked from: ☐ high-level  ☐ structured  ☐ detailed

 Our perspective as of [Month Year], based on the information identified
 in this document.
```

> **The advisor's participation in consultation confirms that the matter was discussed and tested. It
> does not mean the advisor agreed with VYNE's conclusion.**

*Disclosure level is stated in plain language — never as "L1 / L2 / L3."*

## §2 · Page one — the whole decision — *required, all modes*

**Page one answers all nine questions.** Everything after it is depth for those who want it.

1. **What decision are we evaluating?**
2. **What can we conclude today?**
3. **What can we not yet conclude?**
4. **How confident are we?** *(in language — never a number, percentage, or score)*
5. **What are the principal reasons?** *(few, and substantive)*
6. **What gives us pause?** *(material drawbacks — same voice, same type hierarchy as the conclusion)*
7. **What conditions apply?** *(Conditional mode — **on page one**, never in an appendix)*
8. **What would change this conclusion?**
9. **What is the immediate next step?**

> **A conditional conclusion whose conditions are not on page one has become a full recommendation by
> presentation.** That is the single most common way a Record overstates its evidence.

## §3 · Our recommendation, or what we can support today — *required, mode-specific*

**Decision-Support mode**
- **No directional recommendation.** State plainly that we are not in a position to recommend a
  direction, and that implying one would not be responsible.
- **What prevents one:** the specific information or dependencies missing, and what each would enable.

**Conditional mode**
- The **directional conclusion**.
- The **explicit conditions** that must be confirmed.
- **The consequence if each condition fails** — stated per condition, not generally.

**Full mode**
- The **advisor-specific recommendation**.
- Rationale · **material drawbacks** · alternatives · unknowns · **changed-facts triggers**.

**Closure mode**
- What was evaluated.
- What we could and could not conclude.
- Why the matter is pausing or concluding.
- **What should cause it to be revisited.**

## §4 · Your decision — *conditional (include once a decision exists or is forming)*

*Recorded separately from our recommendation. Use one:*

- ☐ No decision made.
- ☐ You are considering our recommendation.
- ☐ You selected the direction we recommended.
- ☐ You selected a different legitimate direction: ____________________
- ☐ You decided to stay.
- ☐ You decided to wait.
- ☐ You paused or concluded the engagement.

> **Your decision does not retroactively change what we recommended. Where the two differ, both are
> stated separately** — our recommendation as we made it, and your decision as you made it.

*Where they differ, our reservations are recorded once, in §12 — not repeated as pressure.*

## §5 · Authorization status — *required, all modes*

*Use one:*

- ☐ **No firm-specific authorization has been issued.**
- ☐ Authorization remains under consideration.
- ☐ A separate authorization was issued under artifact reference: ____________
- ☐ A prior authorization was revoked.

> **This Record authorizes no firm contact, submission, identity disclosure, confidential-information
> sharing, or implementation. Any authorization exists only through a separate firm-specific
> authorization artifact.**

*Authorization terms are not reproduced here.*

## §6 · The options and paths we considered — *required, mode-scaled*

**Decision-Support mode:** show the legitimate paths **still in scope**. **Do not rank them.**

**Conditional and Full modes:** compare the relevant alternatives on **consistent dimensions applied
identically to every option** — capabilities · affiliation and operating model · economics ·
portability · client impact · team impact · ownership · enterprise value · risk · implementation
implications · timing · unresolved issues.

**Include stay and wait wherever they are legitimate.**

**Missing information is shown as missing** — one of: *not established · not verified · not disclosed ·
not yet assessed.* Never as a blank, and never as a neutral or favourable value.

**Specific firms appear only when the engagement has legitimately narrowed to identified firms.** Before
that, the comparison is at the model or path level.

> **We do not imply that the entire market was reviewed.** State which options were evaluated, and that
> others exist that were not, with the reason.

> **No option is labelled by compensation status.** Not *compensating*, *non-compensating*, *potentially
> compensating*, or *compensation unknown*. That notation is internal only, and it never appears here,
> in any form or by any synonym.

## §7 · The major trade-offs — *required in Conditional and Full; optional otherwise*

Material advantages **and** disadvantages, in both directions.

**Never:** a transition package presented without long-term economics · a deal treated as the decision ·
attrition, repricing, expenses, risk, or ownership consequences omitted · "best firm" language · bare
scores or rankings.

## §8 · Decision basis and supporting evidence — *required, all modes*

*Plain language. The advisor should be able to see what our conclusion rests on without learning our
vocabulary.*

- **What is verified**, and how.
- **What you told us** — and where that is the basis rather than an independent check.
- **What a firm publicly claims** — attributed and dated, not adopted as fact.
- **What we assumed.**
- **What remains uncertain.**
- **What we interpreted or judged** — identified as our judgment.

*Evidence-class taxonomy is never exposed as apparatus. "Internal Supporting Record" refers exclusively
to VYNE's internal workpapers and is not this section.*

## §14 · How we're paid — *required, all modes*

> VYNE is generally compensated by the hiring firm when an advisor or team successfully joins that
> organization. Advisors do not pay VYNE for traditional recruiting and transition-consulting services.
> Compensation arrangements may vary by firm and engagement, and we may not be compensated for every
> alternative we evaluate — including staying, waiting, or building your own firm.

*Nothing beyond this paragraph. No percentages, amounts, step-ups, payment timing, clawbacks,
firm-specific commercial terms, gate status, internal conflict analysis, or compensation status beside
any particular firm.*

**●** required · **○** when applicable

**Mode prohibitions**

| Mode | Must not contain |
|---|---|
| **Decision-Support** | any directional recommendation · any "lean" · ranked options · a portability figure or range · a best-fit model |
| **Conditional** | the conclusion stated without its conditions · conditions relegated below page one · consequences of failure omitted |
| **Full** | implied certainty · omitted drawbacks · "best firm" language · a conclusion that outruns the disclosure level |
| **Closure** | **an invented recommendation.** Closure records what was and was not concluded — it does not manufacture a direction to end on. |

## Page-one skeleton

```
════════ CONFIDENTIAL — ADVISOR-FACING ════════════════════════════════════
 [Advisor] · [Decision being evaluated]
 This Record is: [mode, in plain language]
 Our perspective as of [Month Year], based on the information identified
 in this document.
───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 1. WHAT WE CONCLUDE
    — or: what we cannot yet conclude, and why we won't imply otherwise

 2. HOW CONFIDENT WE ARE
    in language, tied to the evidence — never a number

 3. WHY
    few, substantive reasons

 4. WHAT GIVES US PAUSE
    material drawbacks — same voice, same type size as the conclusion

 5. CONDITIONS                                   [Conditional mode only]
    each with the consequence if it fails — never in an appendix

 6. WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS

 7. THE IMMEDIATE NEXT STEP
    (never an authorization)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 Everything after page one is depth for those who want it.
```

## Page targets — launch operating targets, not permanent doctrine

| Mode | Target |
|---|---|
| Decision-Support Record | **1–2 pages** |
| Conditional Recommendation Record | **3–5 pages** |
| Full Recommendation Record | **5–8 pages**, excluding a necessary comparison appendix |
| Decision Status and Closure Record | **1–2 pages** |

**A Record exceeding its target requires a stated reason.**

**Length is not solved by:** smaller type · dense formatting · unnecessary tables · repeating methodology
· copying internal workpapers · including immaterial history.

### Review after five simulated cases

page length · advisor usability · preparation time · correction rate · **whether page one carried the
full decision** · whether the document felt **premium or bureaucratic**.

*Any change is an operating-version decision, not a methodology change unless a substantive defect is
found.*
