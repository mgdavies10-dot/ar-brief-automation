## How to classify — read before completing

### The six evidence classes *(frozen — do not add a seventh)*

**verified fact · public firm claim · market observation · assumption · VYNE interpretation ·
advisor-specific conclusion**

**Source** and **verification status** are recorded **separately**, and are not evidence classes:

| **Source** | advisor · document · firm · public source · specialist · other |
|---|---|
| **Verification status** | verified · advisor-confirmed · unverified · disputed · not applicable |

### Materiality — *does it change the decision?*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Low** | unlikely to change the decision, timing, viability, or material trade-offs based on current information |
| **Moderate** | could affect comparison, scope, timing, or next steps |
| **High** | likely to materially affect direction, viability, risk, economics, client impact, or timing |
| **Unknown** | information is insufficient and potential materiality cannot yet be bounded |
| **Out of scope** | outside VYNE's role, or requires a specialist |

### Confidence — *how well grounded is our current view?*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Limited** | the current view depends materially on assumptions or missing information |
| **Developing** | the main drivers are understood, but meaningful confirmation remains |
| **Supported** | the current L1 understanding is well grounded **for issue-spotting and scope decisions** |

> **Materiality and confidence are different things and are never combined.** A high-materiality area may
> carry limited confidence — that combination is precisely what justifies deepening.
>
> **"Supported" at L1 does not permit a directional recommendation.** It means the scan is sound enough to
> decide *scope*, not direction.
>
> **Materiality is not a score of the advisor, the firm, or the opportunity.**
> **No weighted total or aggregate numerical score is permitted.**
> **Do not use red/yellow/green ratings.**
