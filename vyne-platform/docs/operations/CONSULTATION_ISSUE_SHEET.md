# Consultation Issue Sheet *(optional — material matters only)*

**Artifact:** 5B · Operating Artifact Sprint · **Version:** v0.1 · **Date:** 2026-07-25
**Status:** DRAFT for founder review

**When to prepare one.** Only for a matter that is **material and needs depth** — typically one of the
three-to-five items on the Consultation Brief §5, or a High/Unknown module from the L1 worksheet.
**Most sessions need none, or one or two.** An issue sheet for every item defeats the one-page brief.

**This sheet is internal and is never shown to the advisor.** It is preparation and working space, not a
deliverable.

## Notes

- **§3 is the gate on preparing the sheet at all.** If the decision consequence cannot be stated in a
  sentence, the matter does not warrant an issue sheet.
- **§5–§7 are three separate axes.** Evidence class is the frozen taxonomy; source and verification
  status are recorded separately and are not evidence classes.
- **§11 is where preparation earns its keep.** Deciding in advance *how* an item will be stated and
  tested is what keeps a hard conversation from becoming either defensive or vague.
- **§13 preserves contradictions rather than resolving them.** Resolution happens with the advisor.
- **§17 is temporary working space.** It is not the record.
