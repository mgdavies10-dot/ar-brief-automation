# Pilot Time Log

**Artifact:** 5D · Operating Artifact Sprint · **Version:** v0.1 · **Date:** 2026-07-25
**Status:** **APPROVED as a controlled operating draft** (founder, 2026-07-25) · **Deliberately lean — one row per engagement.**

**Why this exists.** Cumulative time from Orientation review through the Recommendation Record — not any
single artifact — is the binding constraint on launch capacity. It can only be measured, not estimated.

> **Time targets and capacity assumptions will be evaluated after five simulated cases and are not
> permanent doctrine.**

## Review after five cases

Compare against the launch targets currently in the L1 Assessment Guide §1a *(advisor-facing scan
45–60 min; worksheet and handoff 20–30 min; total L1 benchmark 75–90 min)* and assess:

- **Where the time actually went**, versus where it was expected to go.
- **Which artifacts required rework**, and why.
- **Whether the short-form Brief was used** when it should have been.
- **Whether issue-sheet volume was proportionate** — many sheets per engagement suggests the Brief's
  three-to-five selection is not doing its work.
- **Whether the qualitative note trends toward *premium* or *procedural*** — the single most important
  signal, and the one no timing measure will capture.
- **Total founder time per engagement**, and therefore realistic concurrent capacity at launch.

**Any adjustment is recorded as an operating-version change** — not an amendment to frozen methodology,
unless a genuine methodological defect appears.
