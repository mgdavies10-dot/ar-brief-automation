> ### Binding rule
> **A Record cannot be approved, issued, or used to support a submission authorization while a required
> gate remains pending.**

**Honesty constraint.** At founder-led launch the founder is both the recommending consultant and the
gate administrator. **This is auditable discipline, not independent review, and nothing here describes
it as independent.** The second-person field reads *Not available (launch)* — which is itself the honest
disclosure.

*The separate Higher-Conflict Decision Gate form is **Artifact 9** and is not drafted yet. **This status
block operates until it exists**, and may later reference that artifact rather than duplicating it.*
