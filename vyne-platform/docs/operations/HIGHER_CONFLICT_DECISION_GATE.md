# Higher-Conflict Decision Gate

**Artifact:** 9A · Operating Artifact Sprint · **Version:** v0.2 · **Date:** 2026-07-25
**Status:** **APPROVED as a controlled operating draft** (founder, 2026-08-01; conforming revisions applied)

> ## Governing premise
> **The Higher-Conflict Decision Gate is a short, auditable internal issuance control. Its purpose is to
> test whether VYNE's compensation influenced the decision process or conclusion. It is not independent
> review, a substitute for evidence, or a certification that conflicts do not exist.**
>
> **The Gate tests both process and result:** the options considered · evidence requested · comparison
> structure · ordering · timing · rationale · recommendation · next step.

> **What completion does NOT mean.** Completion does not mean VYNE is neutral, unbiased, conflict-free,
> economically disinterested, or independently reviewed. It means **the documented conflict controls
> were completed and the conclusion passed the required tests.**
>
> **A consultant can reach the same conclusion while still having allowed compensation to distort the
> process.** That is why §4 and §5 test the process, not only the result.

**Rules — applying to the six triggers and the general screen alike**

- **Any Yes → Triggered.**

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | ☐ comp ☐ potentially ☐ non-comp ☐ unknown | ☐ | ☐ | | | ☐ no ☐ **yes → §11** |

**Confirm:**

```
 ☐ No legitimate option was omitted because it would not compensate VYNE
 ☐ No compensating option received more favourable evidentiary treatment
 ☐ No non-compensating option received a higher burden of proof
 ☐ Compensation status did not determine ordering
 ☐ Compensation status did not improve the recommendation rationale
 ☐ Unknown compensation status did not cause exclusion
```

> **Do not record exact fee percentages, amounts, step-ups, payment timing, or clawbacks here.**
> Where the **magnitude** of a compensation difference is materially relevant to the conflict analysis,
> **reference the founder-restricted commercial record** rather than copying terms into this Gate.

## §7 · Compensation-placement check

*Reviewed: Recommendation & Decision Record · Comparison Appendix · Advisor Consultation Summary ·
consultation rationale · internal comparison ordering · next-step recommendation.*

```
 ☐ VYNE compensation appears advisor-facing only through the approved general disclosure
 ☐ No fee terms appear advisor-facing
 ☐ Compensation status appears nowhere in advisor-facing comparison treatment
 ☐ Compensation does not appear in the fit rationale
 ☐ Compensation does not determine emphasis, ordering, timing, or urgency
 ☐ Advisor-relevant firm economics remain present and are not confused with
   VYNE compensation
```

> **This is not a test that the word "compensation" appears nowhere.** It is a test that **VYNE's**
> compensation appears only where authorized and **never as fit reasoning** — while **advisor-relevant
> firm economics remain permitted**, which is the whole function of the Appendix.

## §10 · Cooling period

> **Launch operating rule: for a triggered Gate, at least one full business day must pass between Gate
> completion and requesting a new firm-specific authorization from the advisor.**

```
 Gate completion (date/time): ____________________
 Earliest authorization request permitted: ____________________
 Actual authorization request (date/time), if any: ____________________
 Cooling requirement satisfied: ☐ Yes ☐ No ☐ Not yet applicable
```

- **No same-day pressure for authorization.**
- **The Gate must already be completed before the cooling period begins.**
- **If the substantive conclusion changes, the cooling period restarts** after the revised conclusion is
  tested and the new Gate is completed.
- The Record may be discussed or delivered once the Gate is completed — **VYNE simply does not turn a
  conflict-sensitive recommendation straight into an authorization request.**

> **The Gate itself authorizes nothing.**

> **The Gate may identify a defect. It may not conceal the defect by silently rewriting the


> **A direction change caused by process correction must not be mischaracterized as newly discovered

## §12 · Binding issuance effect

> **A Recommendation & Decision Record cannot be approved, issued, or used to support a firm-specific
> authorization while a required Gate remains Pending or Not cleared.**

- **The Comparison Appendix cannot be issued** as support for a gated Record while the Gate is
  unresolved.
- **No submission or implementation action may rely on an uncleared gated conclusion.**
- **A separate authorization artifact remains required** after the Gate and cooling controls are
  satisfied — clearing the Gate is not authorization.

## §14 · Gate rerun, versioning, and archive

> **Every Gate is tied to a specific Recommendation & Decision Record version. A completed Gate is never
> overwritten.**

| Situation | Required action |
|---|---|
| **Substantive change** to options, weighting, rationale, conditions, recommendation, timing, or next step | **New Record version and a new Gate.** |
| **New material information**, conclusion unchanged | Evaluate through the Record lifecycle. **Prior Gate clearance does not automatically carry forward.** If the Record is updated, a new Gate is completed where required. |
| **Non-substantive documentation correction** | Prior Gate **preserved**. Issue a **new Gate version or completion addendum tied to the same Record version**, identifying the correction. |
| **Any superseded Gate** | **Archived with the Record version it governed.** Never deleted, never overwritten. |

```
 This Gate governs Record version: ____
 Supersedes Gate: ____   Archived at: ____________
 Current Record references current Gate: ☐ confirmed
```

- **The current Record must reference the current Gate.**
- **Prior Gates remain preserved and traceable.**
- **If a new triggered Gate is completed, the one-full-business-day authorization cooling period
  restarts** (§10).

> **A Gate establishes the control result for the identified Record version at the stated time. It is not
> permanent clearance for the engagement.**

## §15 · Control-effectiveness review

**Conduct an internal review after the earlier of: ten triggered Gates, or twelve months of production
use.**

**Review:** number triggered · number Pending · number **Not cleared** · number returned upstream ·
number requiring a new Record version · **whether written counterfactual responses show meaningful
variation and substance** · whether the control is identifying genuine process defects or operating as
attestation-only.

> **A Gate that never identifies a defect is not automatically evidence that every engagement was
> unaffected by conflict. It may indicate that the trigger set, questions, administration, or review
> process requires strengthening.**

## §16 · Relationship to Artifact 7B

**The completed Gate is the authoritative Gate artifact.** The interim status block in the Internal
Record Assembly and Issuance Record (7B §4) **remains only as a summary and reference — it does not
replace this artifact.**

**Update 7B to reference:** Gate artifact ID · Gate version · trigger result · Gate result · completion
date · cooling status.

## Format

**The active Gate ordinarily fits on one page** through concise fields and references to the underlying
artifacts.

**A second page may be used only when** written counterfactual explanations · a return-path record · or
a material conflict analysis cannot be responsibly contained on one page.

> **Do not use small type or dense formatting to force one page.** The counterfactual explanations are
> the substance of this control; compressing them defeats it.
