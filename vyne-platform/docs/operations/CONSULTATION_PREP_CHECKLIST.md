# Consultation Preparation — Completion Checklist, Pre-Read Control & Documentation Boundary

**Artifact:** 5C · Operating Artifact Sprint · **Version:** v0.2 · **Date:** 2026-07-25
**Status:** **APPROVED as a controlled operating draft** (founder, 2026-07-25; revisions applied)

> **No consultation proceeds without preparation.** An unprepared consultation is a quality failure, not
> a scheduling inconvenience.

## 2 · Pre-read control

```
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
 Pre-read sent?   ☐ Yes — date sent: __________   ☐ No — why: _________________

 What it contained:
   ☐ decisions in scope        ☐ objectives as understood
   ☐ facts to confirm          ☐ assumptions to test
   ☐ unknowns                  ☐ alternatives under consideration
   ☐ questions the consultation should resolve
   ☐ other: __________________________________________________________________

 Deliberately reserved for discussion:
   ____________________________________________________________________________

 Working direction included?   ☐ No   ☐ Yes — and clearly labelled provisional
   If yes, how labelled: _____________________________________________________
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
```

**Rules**

- A pre-read **may** include decisions, objectives, facts to confirm, assumptions, unknowns,
  alternatives, and questions.
- It **must not** contain a polished final recommendation.
- Any working direction **must be clearly labelled provisional.**
- **The advisor must not first encounter an untested final conclusion in writing.**

*The purpose of a pre-read is to let the advisor arrive prepared to challenge — not to deliver a
conclusion ahead of the conversation that would test it.*

## 3 · During-session documentation boundary

> **During-session capture is temporary working space. Only decision-relevant corrections,
> disagreements, changes, dependencies, authorization decisions, and next actions are transferred into
> the Internal Consultation Record and Advisor Consultation Summary.**

**Transfer onward:**

| | |
|---|---|
| corrections | to facts, assumptions, or interpretations |
| disagreements | both positions, and the practical consequence |
| changes | to objectives, scope, or confidence |
| dependencies | newly surfaced, including specialists |
| authorization decisions | including "none" |
| next actions | with owner and timing |

**Do not record — in working notes or anywhere else:**

- personality judgments
- demeanor notes
- sales readiness
- likelihood to move
- closing probability
- irrelevant personal or family details
- speculation about motivation

**Working notes are discarded once the decision-relevant material has been transferred.** They are not
the record, are not retained as one, and are not shared.

*Test before writing anything down: does this bear on the decision, the scope, confidence, a dependency,
an authorization, or VYNE's accountability? If not, it does not belong in the notes.*
