# Firm / Model Comparison Appendix — template

**Artifact:** 8A · Operating Artifact Sprint · **Version:** v0.1 · **Date:** 2026-07-25
**Status:** DRAFT for founder review

> ## Governing premise
> **The Comparison Appendix supports the Recommendation & Decision Record by showing material
> differences among options on a consistent basis. It does not carry the recommendation, rank the
> options, or authorize contact with any firm.**
>
> **The advisor-facing Appendix is concise and understandable. Detailed sourcing, normalization,
> staleness, and governance remain in a separate internal record.**

## The primary protection

> **The most important protection in this Appendix is not "no ranking." It is *no false
> apples-to-apples comparison.***
>
> **A clean table can be more misleading than a bad paragraph** when cash, deferred compensation,
> equity, ownership, taxes, time horizons, and contingencies are mixed together. Two correct numbers
> that measure different things are more dangerous than one wrong number, because the error is invisible.

**Every page is designated `CONFIDENTIAL — ADVISOR-FACING`. No advisor names in filenames.**

> **Never appears:** VYNE's compensation, recruiting fee, fee percentage, or payment terms · whether an
> option compensates VYNE · internal commercial status · ranking, scores, weighted totals, or
> red/yellow/green · "best firm" language · client PII · internal evidence taxonomy or source hierarchy ·
> raw internal workpapers · any claim of having reviewed the whole market · any implication of
> authorization.

> **This Appendix supports the Recommendation & Decision Record identified above. It does not
> independently state or change VYNE's recommendation.**
>
> **This Appendix authorizes no firm contact, identity disclosure, information sharing, submission,
> negotiation, or implementation.**

## §2 · When this Appendix is used

**Absent by default.** Used only when **all three** hold:

- the live decision has **narrowed sufficiently**;
- the main Record **cannot present the necessary comparison clearly**;
- additional detail would **materially help the advisor understand the trade-offs**.

**It is never:** a marketplace directory · a public firm database · an exhaustive survey · a lead list ·
a ranking engine · a substitute for assessment or consultation.

## §3 · Appendix types

**Model-level** — comparing affiliation or operating models without narrowing to specific firms.
*Examples:* remain in the current employee model · another employee firm · supported independence ·
independent broker-dealer · corporate RIA · independent RIA · join an existing team · merge, sell, or
succeed.

> **A model-level comparison does not establish how any specific firm will perform.** Say so where the
> distinction could be missed.

**Firm-level** — only after the decision has narrowed to identified firms or platforms. **Use
advisor-specific terms where documented**, and **do not substitute generic public marketing terms when
more authoritative information is available.**

**Hybrid** — permitted when the live alternatives legitimately mix named firms and broader paths *(e.g.
stay · a named employee firm · a named supported-independence platform · establish an independent RIA)*.
**Label the basis of each option clearly**, so different levels of specificity are not disguised as
equivalent.

## §4 · Options included — *required*

- **Ordinarily two to four live options.**
- **Five where genuinely necessary.**
- **More than five ordinarily means the decision has not narrowed enough for a detailed comparison** —
  and that is itself a finding for the Record, not a reason for a wider table.

**Include stay and wait wherever they remain legitimate alternatives.**

> **The options shown are the options evaluated. They do not represent the entire market**, and this
> Appendix does not imply otherwise.

## §5 · Comparison dimensions — *required*

**Only the dimensions that materially differentiate the live options — typically four to seven.**

> **Do not add a dimension merely because it exists in the Advisor Decision Ontology.** Additional detail
> belongs **within** a material dimension, not in a wider comparison.

*Drawn from:* capabilities · affiliation and operating model · economics · portability · client impact ·
team impact · ownership · enterprise value · risk · transition requirements · implementation · timing ·
specialist dependencies.

**The Record carries the conclusion. This Appendix carries supporting comparison detail.**

   (or: "no material difference established" / "insufficient information
    to determine a difference")

**Permitted advisor-facing source descriptions:** *documented · firm-provided · publicly stated ·
advisor-reported · not yet confirmed · not established.*

*The internal evidence taxonomy and source hierarchy are never exposed here.*

## §7 · Advisor-relevant economics — *required where economics are compared*

> **VYNE's compensation, recruiting fee, fee percentage, payment terms, and internal commercial status
> never appear in this Appendix.**
>
> **Advisor-relevant economics may appear when properly sourced, normalized, and placed in context.**

**May appear:** payout or grid · transition package · forgivable-loan terms · deferred compensation ·
expenses · employer-provided benefits · equity · ownership · retention conditions · vesting ·
forfeiture · sunset benefits · operating costs · long-term enterprise implications.

**Never label an option** by whether it compensates VYNE, may compensate VYNE, does not compensate VYNE,
or has unknown compensation status — **in any form, or by any synonym.** That notation is internal only.

## §8 · Apples-to-apples normalization — *the load-bearing control*

**Before presenting any economic or operational comparison, establish whether the items share a common
basis.**

**Check every one of these:**

| | |
|---|---|
| annual vs. multiyear | gross vs. net |
| cash vs. deferred | guaranteed vs. contingent |
| recurring vs. one-time | pre-tax vs. after-tax |
| nominal vs. present value | employer-paid vs. advisor-paid |
| current income vs. ownership or terminal value | stated payout vs. actual payable production |
| retention, vesting, forfeiture, clawback, or performance conditions | identical time horizon |
| identical growth or attrition assumptions | |

**Where normalization is possible:** state the common basis explicitly, in the Appendix, where the
advisor will see it.

**Where normalization is not possible:**

> **"These figures are not directly comparable on the information currently available."**

Then **show the difference rather than pretending the terms are comparable** — state what each figure
actually measures, and what would be required to compare them.

> **Do not create false comparability.** A forced normalization is worse than an acknowledged
> incomparability, because it hides the judgment inside a number that looks objective.

## §9 · Firm claims — *required where any appear*

**Attribute plainly:**

- *"The firm states…"*
- *"The written proposal provides…"*
- *"The firm's current public materials describe…"*
- *"The advisor reports being told…"*

> **Inclusion in this Appendix does not convert marketing language, verbal representations, public
> claims, or market observations into verified fact.**

## §10 · Missing and unverified information — *required*

**Use:** *not established · not verified · not disclosed · not yet assessed · no current information
available.*

> **Missing or unverified information must not be treated as a disadvantage merely because another
> option has more complete information.** It remains an unknown with a stated decision consequence.

For each material unknown:

| The unknown | Why it matters | Can it be resolved? | How | Does it limit the comparison or recommendation? |

| | | | | |

## §11 · Material differences — *required*

**This Appendix may state:**
- a **material difference**;
- **no material difference established**;
- **insufficient information to determine a difference**.

**It may not state or imply:** a winner · best option · preferred firm · highest-ranked option · fit
score · readiness score · weighted total · red/yellow/green result.

> ### No implicit winner through design
> **Do not create a winner through formatting**: colour · icons · ordering · bolding · check marks ·
> totals · badges. Column order is not a ranking, and must not be allowed to read as one — where order
> could be misread, say what determines it.

## §12 · Matters requiring due diligence — *when applicable*

**Only material questions still requiring:** written confirmation · firm documentation · specialist
review · advisor disclosure · further consultation · **authorized firm inquiry**.

*An authorized inquiry is tied to a separate firm-specific authorization artifact — referenced, never
assumed.*

## §13 · Firm-contact boundary — *required*

> **Comparing a firm does not authorize VYNE to contact it, disclose the advisor's identity, request
> terms, submit information, or share confidential information.**

**Information here may come only from:** current public materials · advisor-provided information ·
information already lawfully held by VYNE · written firm information received through an authorized
process.

## §15 · Relationship to the Record — *required*

**This Appendix must be read with the identified Record.** It must not:

- carry a separate conclusion;
- conflict with the Record;
- silently introduce a new option;
- silently change an assumption;
- **be delivered without a valid Record reference.**

> **If information in this Appendix materially changes the conclusion, the Record is updated through its
> version lifecycle** — the Appendix does not absorb the change quietly.

## Appendix-type matrix

| | **Model-level** | **Firm-level** | **Hybrid** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Use when** | comparing models; decision not narrowed to firms | decision narrowed to identified firms/platforms | live alternatives legitimately mix both |
| **Options** | affiliation/operating models | named firms, plus stay/wait | mixed — **basis labelled per option** |
| **Terms used** | general model characteristics | **advisor-specific where documented** | per option, labelled |
| **Economics** | typical structures, plainly framed as typical | advisor-specific where documented; otherwise attributed | per option, labelled |
| **Primary risk** | implying a model result predicts a firm result | substituting public marketing for documented terms | **disguising different specificity as equivalence** |
| **Must state** | "does not establish how a specific firm will perform" | source and date per material term | the basis of each option |
| **Target length** | 2–4 pages | 3–6 pages | 3–6 pages |
