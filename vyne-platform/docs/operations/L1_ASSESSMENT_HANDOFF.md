## Completion notes

- **Section 5 is deliberately short.** If every module lands in "top material areas," the materiality
  classification has not done its work. Two to four is normal.
- **Section 6 is the most useful line on the page.** An unknown that would change the answer is worth more
  than three facts that would not.
- **Section 8 names contradictions without resolving them.** Resolution happens with the advisor, in
  consultation — that is what consultation is for.
- **Section 11 is a proposal, not a decision.** Disclosure advances only when a specific question requires
  it *and* the advisor chooses it.
- **Nothing in this handoff states a direction.** If a reader could infer VYNE's preferred outcome from
  this page, it has drifted past L1 and must be rewritten.
