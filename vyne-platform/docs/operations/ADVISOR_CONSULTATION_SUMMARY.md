# Advisor Consultation Summary — template

**Artifact:** 6A · Operating Artifact Sprint · **Version:** v0.1 · **Date:** 2026-07-25
**Status:** DRAFT for founder review

> ## Governing premise
> **The Advisor Consultation Summary is a concise record of what materially changed, where the decision
> stands, what remains unresolved, and what happens next. It is not meeting minutes, and it is not the
> Recommendation & Decision Record.**

**One page ordinarily.** A second page is permitted only when material disagreement, specialist
dependencies, or substantial next actions require it.

**Modular — omit what does not apply.** Do not show empty or irrelevant sections merely because they
exist in the template. A Summary with six live sections is better than one with twelve, half of them
saying "none."

> **Never appears in the advisor-facing Summary:** internal labels (Level 1/2/3, materiality ratings, PTA
> module numbers, gate status) · evidence-class taxonomy · internal reasoning · conflict-gate fields ·
> fee percentages, amounts, step-ups, payment timing, clawbacks, or firm-specific commercial terms ·
> client PII · any claim of being unbiased, neutral, conflict-free, or of having reviewed the whole
> market · "best firm" language.

## §3 · Where things stand today — *required*

*Use one, in plain language:*

- ☐ We're not yet in a position to support a direction.
- ☐ The decision remains open while information is gathered.

## §12 · How we're paid — *conditional*

*Include **only** when: this is the first substantive consultation Summary · a new specific firm entered

**Do not repeat automatically in every Summary.**

> VYNE is generally compensated by the hiring firm when an advisor or team successfully joins that
> organization. Advisors do not pay VYNE for traditional recruiting and transition-consulting services.
> Compensation arrangements may vary by firm and engagement, and we may not be compensated for every
> alternative we evaluate — including staying, waiting, or building your own firm.

- This Summary does not replace advice from your own legal, tax, or other professional advisers.
- **This Summary authorizes nothing.**

## Delivery standard

**Launch target: issue within two business days of a meaningful consultation.**

- **Factual corrections are made visibly**, under the Engagement Record Standards — never silently.
- **A material change in direction** returns to assessment/consultation and, where applicable, to the
  Recommendation & Decision Record lifecycle. A Summary does not carry a direction change on its own.

**Required in every Summary:** §1 header · §2 decision discussed · §3 where things stand · §4 what
changed or was clarified · §8 unresolved matters · §10 next actions · §11 authorization status.

**Included only when applicable:** §5 settled matters · §6 trade-offs · §7 differences in view ·
§9 specialist dependencies · §12 compensation disclosure · changed-facts trigger.
