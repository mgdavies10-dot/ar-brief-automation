## 1 · What changed

The first clean-room run of VYNE's entry stage happened.

A Consultant with no knowledge of the case design took the Orientation intake, the Entry Decision record,
the Record Standards, the Constitution, the Ontology, and the Operating Principles, and produced a
complete entry record in **60 minutes**. It produced no direction, named no firm, estimated nothing, and
stopped where instructed.

Three things came back that did not exist before the run:

1. **A decision framing that contradicts the advisor's own framing** — reached independently, from
   released facts alone.
2. **Twelve artifact and governance defects that only surface under use** — since extended to sixteen by
   founder ruling.
3. **A confidently wrong answer on the Gate-trigger field**, which the Consultant had no way to get right
   because the trigger list was never supplied to it.

**1 · It treated *"I've been referring both out"* as evidence of a capability gap.** *(the significant one)*

un-accessed?"*

**→ Ruling B addresses this directly.**

The advisor's first-checked scope item is literally ★'s question — *should I change anything at all
(act · wait · stay)*. The Ontology it received names ★ explicitly. The mapping table has no row for it.

*Mitigating:* the Ontology defines ★ as *"not a family — the synthesis, produced last,"* and the
Consultant's narrative addresses stay-versus-change substantively. **Minor.**

**3 · It flagged the missing acknowledgment but not what the acknowledgment carries.**

It correctly refused to advance the Orientation past DRAFT with §8 unsigned. It did not connect that to
the consequence: **no evidence exists that the advisor received the compensation disclosure.**

*Refinement on review:* the **Engagement Understanding §19 does carry** the compensation acknowledgment.

first made (§7), so the acknowledgment that follows it should cover it. *(Finding 2.)*
