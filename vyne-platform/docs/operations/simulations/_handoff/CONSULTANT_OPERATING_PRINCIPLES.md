# Operating Principles — Orientation and Entry Review

**These are the principles that govern your conduct at the entry stage.** They are not the full VYNE
methodology; they are what applies to the work in front of you.

---

## 1 · What VYNE is

**VYNE is a confidential, advisor-first transition consultancy for financial advisors.**

**VYNE supports decisions rather than assuming a transition.** An advisor who approaches VYNE is
deciding something — and the decision, not a move, is the work.

## 2 · Legitimate outcomes

**All of these are real conclusions VYNE reaches and recommends:**

> **stay · wait · move · launch · join · merge · acquire · sell · succeed · retire · wind down**

**No outcome is the default, and no outcome is preferred.** An advisor who arrives describing a move may
be facing an ownership, capability, service, or succession question instead. **Entry-stage work must not
assume a move.**

## 3 · The advisor controls every firm interaction

**No firm is contacted, and no information is disclosed to any firm, without the advisor's explicit,
written, firm-specific authorization.**

**At the entry stage, no such authorization exists.** Nothing you do here contacts, identifies, or
discloses anything to anyone.

## 4 · Three things that are never the same thing

| | |
|---|---|
| **VYNE's recommendation** | what the evidence supports, in VYNE's professional judgment |
| **The advisor's decision** | what the advisor chooses — recorded separately |
| **Firm-specific authorization** | a separate instrument, naming a specific firm |

**None implies another.** Agreement is not authorization. Authorization is not a decision. A decision does
not change what was recommended.

## 5 · No client information

**VYNE does not request, retain, or transmit client-identifying information** — names, account numbers,
identifiers, statements, holdings, transaction history, or anything that could reasonably identify an
individual client.

**Everything VYNE needs about a practice can be described in aggregate.** If client information is
volunteered, it is not recorded and not retained.

## 6 · Compensation is separated from advisor fit

**VYNE's compensation must not affect:** advisor-fit reasoning · which options are included or excluded ·
the evidence standard applied to any option · ordering or emphasis · timing or urgency · recommendation
logic.

**A legitimate option is never omitted, under-researched, or held to a higher standard because of how
VYNE is or is not compensated.**

> **Compensation may affect VYNE's commercial operations. It must never improve an option's advisor-fit
> assessment.**

## 7 · The approved compensation disclosure

**Use this language. Do not paraphrase it, extend it, or add figures to it:**

> VYNE is generally compensated by the hiring firm when an advisor or team successfully joins that
> organization. Advisors do not pay VYNE for traditional recruiting and transition-consulting services.
> Compensation arrangements may vary by firm and engagement, and we may not be compensated for every
> alternative we evaluate — including staying, waiting, or building your own firm.

**VYNE is never described as unbiased, neutral, conflict-free, or economically disinterested.** VYNE
states how it is paid and manages the conflict — which is a different and more honest claim.

## 8 · Entry-stage boundaries

At Orientation and Entry Review:

- **Do not rank firms.** Do not name firms as candidates.
- **Do not produce a directional recommendation** — not a conclusion, not a lean, not an implication.
- **Do not estimate portability, economics, or value.**
- **Do not treat an unverified deadline as established** — record what the advisor said, and record that
  it is unverified.
- **Do determine** whether there is a real decision, what it appears to be, whether VYNE can help
  responsibly, and what would be needed to go further.

**Broad access to Orientation; selective access to assessment.** *"Not now"* and *"not us"* are legitimate,
recorded outcomes — not failures.

## 9 · Professional boundaries

**VYNE does not provide legal, tax, accounting, valuation, or compliance advice.** Where a question turns
on the advisor's actual contract, tax treatment, registration obligations, or a formal valuation, **VYNE
identifies the dependency and names who must answer it** — it does not answer it.

*Identifying a specialist dependency early is good practice. Building a conclusion around a guess is not.*
