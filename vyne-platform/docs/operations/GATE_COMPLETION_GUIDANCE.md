## 1 · Trigger-source mapping — **⚠ conflict flagged**

**The three authoritative sources do not agree.** Per founder direction, this is flagged rather than
silently resolved.

| # | **Consultation Architecture §14** *(FROZEN)* | **Assessment Architecture §7.5** *(approved)* | **Commercial Principles §8.5** *(operational)* |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | the recommended **option** pays materially more than a close alternative | the recommended **firm** pays materially more | the recommended **firm** pays materially more |
| T2 | a **non-compensating** alternative appears substantively stronger | a **non-paying** alternative looks substantively stronger | a **non-paying** alternative looks substantively stronger |
| T3 | the advisor is considering **staying or waiting** | the advisor is considering **staying or waiting** | the advisor is considering **staying** *(waiting omitted)* |

### The three discrepancies

**D-1 — material. "Or waiting" is missing from Commercial Principles §8.5.** Consultation Architecture
(frozen) and Assessment Architecture both include **waiting**; Commercial Principles lists **staying**
only. **This matters:** *wait* is one of the most common no-fee outcomes, and a Gate that does not fire
on it would miss a large share of genuine conflict situations.

**D-2 — scope. "Option" vs. "firm" in T1.** Consultation Architecture says *option*; the other two say
*firm*. **"Option" is broader and correct** — under the Ontology, models and paths are legitimate
options, and a conflict can arise between a compensating *model* and a non-compensating one before any
firm is named.

**D-3 — wording. "Non-compensating" vs. "non-paying" in T2.** Same meaning. **"Non-compensating"** is
preferred because it matches the four-category internal notation used in every other artifact
*(compensating / potentially compensating / non-compensating / compensation unknown)*.

### Provisional resolution — **requires founder confirmation**

**Adopt Consultation Architecture §14** as authoritative, because it is:

1. **frozen** *(Consultation Architecture is APPROVED & FROZEN)*;
2. the **most recent** formulation;
3. the **broadest in scope** *("option," and "staying or waiting")*;
4. **already implemented** in the built operating artifacts — Consultation Brief §10, L1 Handoff §13, and
   Internal Consultation Record all use *staying · waiting · launching an RIA · non-compensating
   alternative may be stronger · other*;
5. consistent with Commercial Principles' own status as an **operational document subordinate to the
   frozen architecture**.

**T7** is not a new trigger — it records **Assessment Architecture §7.5's framing clause**, which
presents the six as *"including"* examples of a general test. Without T7, a novel conflict fitting the
governing test but not the six examples would pass unflagged.

> **Founder action required:** confirm this resolution and authorize a **conforming amendment to
> Commercial Principles §8.5** — adding *"or waiting"*, and aligning *option* and *non-compensating*.
> Until then the Gate operates on the frozen formulation, and the discrepancy stands recorded.

## 4 · Worked examples

### 4.1 · Not triggered

**Engagement E-26-031, Decision-Support Record.** Advisor asked about moving; scope broadened to
succession and ownership; no direction supported; counsel required on the covenant.

**Trigger screen:** T1 **No** *(no option recommended)* · T2 **No** *(no alternative assessed as
stronger — none assessed at all)* · **T3 No** · T4 **No** · T5 **No** · T6 **No** · T7 **No**.

> **Note on T3.** The advisor has *not yet* raised staying or waiting as a live consideration — the
> Record concludes no direction is supported, which is a different thing. **If the advisor subsequently
> indicates they are considering waiting, T3 fires and a Gate is required for the next Record.** The
> earlier Consultation Brief §10 flagged this as *foreseeable*; **foreseeable is not triggered.**

**Result: Not triggered.** No cooling requirement. Record may be issued under normal controls.

**§4, §5, §6, §8 still completed?** **No** — a Not-triggered Gate records the screen, the result, and
the attestation. *Requiring the full counterfactual apparatus on every untriggered Gate would be the
checkbox theatre the critique warns about.*

### 4.2 · Triggered and cleared

**Full Recommendation Record.** VYNE recommends a supported-independence platform. A legitimate
non-compensating alternative — the advisor building an independent RIA directly — remains in scope. The
recommended platform **may** compensate VYNE.

**Trigger screen:** **T1 Yes** *(recommended option may pay materially more than the non-compensating
alternative)* · **T2 No** *(the non-compensating alternative is not assessed as substantively stronger —
it is assessed as viable but slower and more operationally demanding, which is stated in the Record)* ·
T3 No · **T4 Yes** *(building an RIA is under consideration)* · T5 No · T6 No · T7 No. → **Triggered.**

**§4 no-compensation counterfactual — Yes.** Written: *"With no compensation from any outcome, the same
four options remain in scope and the same evidence would have been requested. The capability
requirements and the advisor's stated preference for reduced operational responsibility drive the
conclusion; both would be unchanged. The recommendation and next step would be the same."*

**§5 equal-compensation counterfactual — Yes.** Written: *"With identical compensation across outcomes,
ordering would be unchanged — the Appendix orders current path first, then alphabetically. Comparison
dimensions were selected on what differentiates the options, not on economics alone. Timing follows the
advisor's vesting date, which is documented. The direct-RIA path is presented with the same evidentiary
burden as the platform path."*

**§6:** four options, notations recorded internally, all six confirmations ticked. **§7:** compensation
appears advisor-facing only in the approved disclosure; **advisor-relevant firm economics appear
throughout the Appendix, which is correct and not a finding.** **§8:** substantive basis stated in the
administrator's own words. **§9:** no unresolved prerequisite dependency.

**Result: Triggered — Cleared; conclusion unchanged.**
**§10 cooling:** Gate completed Tue 14:20 → **earliest authorization request Thu 14:20** *(one full
business day)*. Record may be delivered and discussed meanwhile.

### 4.3 · Triggered and not cleared — the case that justifies the artifact

**Full Recommendation Record**, draft. Recommends Firm A. Firm B — non-compensating — was set aside
early.

**Trigger screen:** **T1 Yes** · **T2 Yes** · T3 No · T4 No · T5 No · T6 No. → **Triggered.**

**§5 equal-compensation counterfactual — No.** Written honestly: *"Under equal compensation, Firm B would
not have been set aside at the point it was. It was set aside on a single unverified statement about
platform capability, while Firm A's equivalent claim was accepted and carried into the comparison. The
two were not held to the same evidentiary standard."*

**§6 alternative-integrity check fails** on two confirmations: *no non-compensating option received a
higher burden of proof* and *compensation did not affect inclusion or treatment.*

> **What must NOT happen:** editing the draft to reinstate Firm B, re-running the comparison, and
> approving the same Record. **That would conceal the defect.**

**§11 return path executed:**
- **Original draft preserved internally** *(location recorded)*.
- **Cause classified: `compensation-influenced reasoning` and `inconsistent option treatment`** — **not**
  `newly discovered evidence`. Firm B's capability was always assessable; it simply was not assessed to
  the same standard.
- **Upstream artifact requiring revision: the Assessment** — Firm B's capability question returns to
  module 4 for equivalent treatment.
- **Returned to assessment**, then **consultation** to test the revised comparison with the advisor.
- **New Record version (v2.0)** issued, with **§15 what-changed** identifying the process correction.
- **New Gate completed against v2.0.**

**Result on the original: Triggered — Not cleared; return to assessment or consultation.**
**No authorization occurs.** The Appendix supporting the original Record is not issued. **Cooling does
not begin** — there is nothing cleared to cool.

> **The Gate did its job by failing.** A control that never blocks anything is not a control.

## 5 · Critique

- **Can founder self-administration be meaningful?** **Partly, and the artifact should not overclaim.**
  It cannot deliver independence. What it *can* deliver is **a forced written record** — §4, §5, and §8
  require prose, and writing *"under equal compensation Firm B would not have been set aside"* is
  materially harder than ticking a box. **The value is the sentence the administrator has to write**,
  and its auditability later. That is real but limited, and §1 and §13 say so plainly.
- **Checkbox theatre?** **The genuine risk.** Mitigations: three-state triggers *(so uncertainty cannot
  be resolved as "No")* · required written explanations · §8's own-words basis · and the Not-triggered
  path deliberately **not** requiring the full apparatus, so the heavy fields signal a real event.
  **Residual risk:** an administrator who writes *"Yes — compensation played no part"* in both
  counterfactuals every time has technically complied. **Nothing in a self-administered control can
  prevent that.** The only real check is the second-person review that does not yet exist.
- **Excessive defensiveness?** This artifact is internal, so advisor-facing tone is not at stake. It does
  carry four separate disclaimers of independence *(§1 banner, §1 note, §13 attestation, plus the
  premise)*. **That is deliberate** — the failure mode being guarded against is precisely the drift from
  *"documented control"* to *"we reviewed it."*
- **Are the counterfactuals answerable honestly?** **This is the sharpest question.** They ask the
  administrator to introspect about their own bias, which is exactly what bias resists. **§5 is more
  answerable than §4** because it asks about *observable artifacts* — ordering, dimensions selected,
  evidentiary standards applied — rather than about motive. **Recommendation: weight §5 in practice**,
  because process asymmetries are visible in the record while motive is not.
- **Does it duplicate 7B?** **No, and §14 resolves it** — 7B's block becomes a summary and reference.
  **Residual risk:** two places recording gate status can diverge. 7B should carry the Gate artifact ID
  and result, not a parallel set of fields.
- **Is one page realistic?** **For Not triggered and simple Cleared, yes.** For a **Not cleared** Gate
  with two written counterfactuals and a full return-path record, **no — and the artifact says so**,
  permitting a second page rather than forcing compression. **That is the right trade:** the
  counterfactual explanations are the control, and compressing them defeats it.
- **Does cooling become procedural?** **Real risk.** One business day is short enough to be absorbed as
  a scheduling step rather than a pause for reflection. **Its actual protective work is narrow but
  genuine:** it prevents converting a conflict-sensitive recommendation into an authorization request in
  the same conversation. **It should be measured in the pilots** — if authorization requests cluster at
  exactly the earliest permitted moment, the period is being administered rather than observed.

## 6 · Recommendation

**Approve with revisions.** The Gate does what the premise requires: it tests **process and result**,
not merely whether the conclusion would have been the same; it cannot silently repair a defect; it
blocks issuance and authorization while pending or not cleared; it keeps exact VYNE economics out while
preserving advisor-relevant firm economics; and it consistently refuses to describe founder
administration as independent review.

**Three revisions I would want:**

1. **Confirm the trigger-source resolution and authorize the Commercial Principles §8.5 conforming
   amendment** — adding *"or waiting"*, and aligning *option* and *non-compensating*. **This is the item
   I would not proceed past without a founder decision**, because the Gate's trigger set is currently
   inconsistent across three controlled documents and the missing *waiting* is material.
2. **Decide whether T7 stays.** It faithfully records Assessment Architecture §7.5's governing test and
   closes a real gap — but it is the one element not drawn verbatim from the frozen six, and you
   directed that no new trigger be invented. *My view: keep it, labelled as the governing test rather
   than a seventh trigger.* Your call.
3. **State what happens when the same Gate is completed twice with different results.** §11 covers a
   defect found *during* a Gate, but not a Gate re-run after new information where the earlier Gate had
   already cleared. A one-line rule — *a superseded Gate is archived with its Record version and never
   overwritten* — would close it.

**One item for founder attention:** worked example **4.3** is the one to read. It is the only place in
the whole operating system where a control **blocks VYNE's own recommendation**, and the value of the
Gate rests entirely on whether that path would actually be taken in practice rather than quietly routed
around. **A control that has never once produced a "not cleared" result after a year of engagements
should be treated as evidence about the control, not about the engagements.**
