# Internal Consultation Record

**Artifact:** 6B · Operating Artifact Sprint · **Version:** v0.2 · **Date:** 2026-07-25
**Status:** **APPROVED as a controlled operating draft** (founder, 2026-07-25; revisions applied)

**Distinct from the advisor-facing Summary (6A).** This record holds VYNE's internal reasoning,
governance trail, and evidence discipline. **Raw internal notes are never sent to the advisor**; where an
advisor question can only be answered from here, the answer is rewritten into an approved advisor-facing
artifact.

**Two tiers** (Consultation Architecture §11): Tier 1 every session; Tier 2 whenever its trigger occurs.

## Do not record

Personality judgments · demeanor scoring · sales readiness · likelihood to move · closing probability ·
irrelevant family details · speculation about motivation · **client PII**.

*Working test: does this bear on the decision, the scope, confidence, a dependency, an authorization, or
VYNE's accountability? If not, it does not belong here.* And: **notes must be shareable with the advisor
without embarrassment** — if a line would be awkward for them to read, it is either wrongly phrased or
should not exist.
