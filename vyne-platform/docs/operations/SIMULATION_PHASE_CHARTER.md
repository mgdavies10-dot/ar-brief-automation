# Simulation Phase Charter — proposed

**Date:** 2026-08-01 · **Status:** PROPOSAL for founder authorization · **Not begun.**

> ## Purpose
> **Run the same ten artifacts through five deliberately different advisor cases and document where the
> process becomes repetitive, slow, unclear, overly defensive — or genuinely useful.**
>
> **This phase does not create templates.** No new artifact, no revision to a frozen document, and no
> design or software work occurs during simulation. Findings are recorded; changes are proposed
> afterward, in one consolidated pass.

---

## 1 · What a simulation is

A **complete synthetic engagement** run end to end using the real artifacts, at real length, producing
real documents. **Not a walkthrough, not a checklist review, not a discussion of what would happen.**

**Each case produces:** an Orientation and Entry Decision · an Engagement Understanding acceptance · an
L1 worksheet and Handoff · a Consultation Brief and any Issue Sheets · a Consultation Summary and
Internal Record · a Recommendation & Decision Record · a Gate where triggered · an Appendix where
warranted · an Authorization where reached · and a Pilot Time Log entry.

**Synthetic advisors only.** No real advisor, no real firm terms, **no client PII** — the standing rules
apply to simulated engagements exactly as they would to real ones, because the point is to test the
system as it will actually run.

## 2 · The five cases — deliberately different

*Chosen so that each stresses a different control, and so that no two produce the same artifact set.*

| # | Case | What it stresses | Expected end state |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Stay** — advisor arrives wanting to move; evidence supports staying | no-fee outcome · Gate T3 · whether VYNE will actually conclude *stay* | Full or Conditional Record · Gate triggered and cleared · **no authorization** |
| **2** | **Wait** — timing dominates; a vesting date and an unresolved covenant | conditions on page one · specialist prerequisite · Gate T3 | **Conditional Record** · counsel dependency · no authorization |
| **3** | **Employee-firm move** — decision narrows to two named firms | Appendix · asymmetric source strength · normalization · authorization chain | Full Record + **firm-level Appendix** · Gate · Authorization D/E/F |
| **4** | **Supported independence** — capability-driven; a non-compensating alternative is live | Gate T1/T2 · compensation separation · **due-diligence authorization before any Record** | DD Authorization (A/B/C) **first** · then Record · Appendix |
| **5** | **Advisor chooses against the recommendation** | the three separations · §12 tone · §4 advisor decision · disagreement recorded without grievance | Full Record · advisor decision differs · **both positions recorded** |

**Sequence:** run **1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5.** Cases 1 and 2 are the least artifact-heavy and surface pacing
problems early; case 3 first exercises the Appendix; case 4 is the only one that begins with
authorization *before* a Record; case 5 is the tone test and benefits from four prior cases of practice.

**Do not run them in parallel.** Each case should inform how the next is observed.

## 3 · What is measured

**Quantitative — per case, into the Pilot Time Log**

Orientation review · L1 conversation · L1 worksheet and handoff · consultation preparation ·
consultation(s) · Summary · Record · **total founder time** · issue sheets prepared · Brief form (full or
short) · number of consultations · material comparison points · Source Bundles · Appendix pages ·
Record pages by mode · rework minutes.

**Qualitative — per case, three or four sentences**

> *Did the process feel focused, repetitive, premium, or procedural? Where did time go that did not earn
> it?*

**Specific questions the artifacts themselves asked to have tested**

| Artifact | Question raised |
|---|---|
| L1 Guide §1a | Are 45–60 min / 75–90 min realistic? How often is the shortened path used? |
| Consultation Brief | Is one page realistic? Is the short form used when it should be? |
| Consultation Summary | Is two-business-day delivery achievable? Correction rate? Overproduced? |
| Record 7A | Did **page one carry the full decision**? Premium or bureaucratic? |
| Appendix 8B | Do material comparison points stay in the 8–20 norm? |
| Gate 9A | **Do the written counterfactuals show meaningful variation** — or the same sentence each time? |
| Authorization 10A | Advisor friction. Does 60 days create stale permission or unnecessary re-signing? |

## 4 · What is deliberately *not* done during simulation

- **No new artifacts or templates.**
- **No revisions to frozen documents** — findings are recorded, not applied.
- **No design, UI, CRM, software, or automation.**
- **No calculator work** *(Phase-3 backlog, unaffected)*.
- **No counsel-dependent language finalized** — the Engagement Understanding remains a counsel-review
  draft throughout, and simulations are run on it as drafted.

> **The discipline that makes this phase useful is not fixing things as you notice them.** A defect
> noticed in case 2 and fixed immediately means cases 3–5 test a different system, and the pattern across
> five cases is lost.

## 5 · Output

**One consolidated findings report** at the end, containing:

1. **What worked** — specifically, with the case that demonstrated it.
2. **What was repetitive** — duplication between artifacts, re-asking, transcription rather than
   selection.
3. **What was slow** — with measured time, not impression.
4. **What was unclear** — where a consultant had to guess.
5. **What was overly defensive** — where protective language outweighed usefulness.
6. **What was genuinely useful** — the artifact or field that changed a decision or caught something.
7. **Proposed changes**, each classified: *operating-version change* · *conforming amendment* · **or a
   documented pilot finding requiring the frozen-doctrine amendment path** *(finding · affected doctrine ·
   proposed amendment · why necessary · founder approval · new version + DL entry)*.

**Nothing is changed until that report is reviewed.**

## 6 · Honest limits of this phase

- **Simulated advisors do not push back the way real ones do.** The tone test in case 5 and the friction
  test in case 3 are the weakest simulations, because the founder plays both sides.
- **Time measured under simulation will understate real time** — no scheduling delays, no waiting on
  counsel, no advisor who goes quiet for three weeks.
- **The Gate cannot be honestly simulated.** A self-administered conflict control tested by the person
  who designed it will clear. **Case 4 should be run with a deliberate attempt to reach the answer VYNE
  would prefer, to see whether the counterfactuals catch it** — and if they do not, that is a finding
  about the Gate.
- **Five cases is enough to find friction and duplication. It is not enough to validate judgment
  quality.** That requires real advisors.

## 7 · Founder authorization required

**This charter is a proposal.** Simulations do not begin until the founder separately approves — and may
approve a different case set, sequence, or measurement scope.
