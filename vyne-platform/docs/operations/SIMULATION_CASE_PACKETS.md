# Case 1 · STAY

**SIM-01** · *Advisor arrives wanting to move; the evidence supports staying.*

**What this case tests.** Whether VYNE will **actually conclude "stay"** when that conclusion earns
nothing — and whether the Gate treats a no-fee outcome with the same rigour as a compensating one.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Synthetic** | advisor, practice, team, book, current-firm relationship, frustrations |
| **Real public intelligence** | the general affiliation models the advisor is weighing; publicly documented characteristics of one or two comparator firm *types* |
| **Researched during the case** | what a comparable independent or regional model would actually offer on the two capabilities the advisor says they lack |
| **Synthetic terms** | none — no proposal exists in this case |

**Initially available facts.** ~$240M AUM, ~$1.6M production, employee model, 14 years at the firm, small
team (one junior advisor, one CSA). Stated reason for exploring: *"the service model has deteriorated and
I'm tired of apologising to clients."* Stated objective: better service support and modestly better
economics.

**Hidden facts, released on schedule.**

| Release point | Fact |
|---|---|
| **At L1 (Module 5)** | a firm-wide service reorganisation was announced two months ago and is not yet implemented |
| **At L1 (Module 4)** | the two capability gaps the advisor cites are **available at the current firm** but require an internal desk the advisor has never been introduced to |
| **At consultation** | the advisor has not raised the service issue with anyone at the firm |
| **At consultation** | a peer at the same firm moved last year and reports the grass was not greener |

**Founder decisions required.** *(A)* is this a genuine change decision or an internal-escalation problem
· *(B)* does capability materiality change once the internal desk is known · *(C)* which three matters to
test · *(D)* whether "stay" is the supported conclusion and whether internal renegotiation is in scope ·
*(E)* whether the advisor should receive a Record at all, or something lighter.

**Gate.** **T3 TRIGGERED** — the advisor is considering staying. Gate **required**. Expected: *Triggered —
Cleared*, with both counterfactuals written. **No authorization is created merely to keep the engagement
moving.**

**Expected artifacts.** Orientation · Entry Decision · Engagement Understanding · L1 (light scan, several
shortened-path modules) · Brief · Summary · **Record (Conditional or Full)** · **Gate** · *no Appendix* ·
*no Authorization*.

**Proposed advisor-package options.** *(a)* Consultation Summary only · *(b)* Summary + Conditional
Record · *(c)* Record + a short next-steps roadmap for the internal conversation. **Founder selects at E.**

> **What would invalidate this case:** if the "stay" conclusion is reached because the case was
> constructed to produce it rather than because the evidence drove it. **The test is whether the process
> *finds* it** — so the founder should be willing to conclude "move" if the tested evidence points there.

# Case 2 · WAIT

**SIM-02** · *A vesting date and an unresolved covenant dominate the decision.*

**What this case tests.** **Conditions on page one** · a specialist dependency that is a prerequisite to a
dependent conclusion but does not stop other work · whether **waiting receives the same analytical
treatment as a compensating move**.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Synthetic** | advisor, book, deferred-comp schedule, the covenant document's existence |
| **Real public intelligence** | how deferred compensation and forgivable-note structures generally work; Protocol status and what changed when firms withdrew — **researched, dated, and qualified** |
| **Researched during the case** | general mechanics only — **no firm-specific covenant interpretation** |
| **Synthetic terms** | the advisor's own unvested schedule (labelled synthetic) |

**Initially available facts.** ~$410M AUM, ~$2.8M production, employee model, 9 years at current firm
following an acquisition. Considering a move. Believes the non-solicit is "about a year."

**Hidden facts, released on schedule.**

| Release point | Fact |
|---|---|
| **At L1 (Module 10)** | the firm was acquired 18 months ago; the advisor does not know whether the covenant travelled or was replaced, and has no copy |
| **At L1 (Module 5)** | material unvested deferred compensation vests in ~14 months |
| **At consultation** | the advisor's stated timeline ("this year") is driven by a recruiter's call, not by anything in their own situation |
| **After counsel referral** | *(founder choice)* counsel confirms a **narrower** restriction than assumed — testing whether the conclusion updates cleanly |

**Founder decisions required.** *(A)* scope and disclosure level · *(B)* whether the covenant is a
prerequisite dependency and which areas continue in parallel · *(C)* how to test an unverified deadline
without dismissing the advisor's urgency · *(D)* the conditions and their failure consequences · *(E)*
whether a Conditional Record is issued now or after counsel responds.

**Gate.** **T3 TRIGGERED** — the advisor is considering waiting. Gate **required**, and it must test
**whether waiting received the same evidentiary and analytical treatment as a compensating move.**
Specialist dependencies respected. **No firm-specific authorization** unless a distinct due-diligence
need arises and is separately approved.

**Expected artifacts.** Orientation · Entry · Understanding · L1 · Brief · Summary · **Conditional
Record with conditions on page one** · **Gate** · counsel dependency recorded · *no Appendix* · *no
Authorization*.

**Proposed advisor-package options.** *(a)* Summary + Conditional Record · *(b)* + due-diligence question
list for counsel · *(c)* + next-steps roadmap keyed to the vesting date.

> **What would invalidate this case:** conditions drifting off page one, or the unverified deadline being
> either accepted as real or dismissed rather than tested.

# Case 3 · EMPLOYEE-FIRM MOVE

**SIM-03** · *The decision narrows to two named firms with very different documentation.*

**What this case tests.** The **Comparison Appendix** · **asymmetric source strength** · **normalization
and the incomparability pattern** · the **full authorization chain**. **Heaviest case.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Synthetic** | advisor, book, team, the "written proposal" from Firm A |
| **REAL public intelligence** | **two actual named wealth-management firms**, researched live: affiliation model · platform and capability structure · custody and technology · publicly documented transition or recruiting programs · public statements about advisor support |
| **Researched during the case** | everything above, **with source, retrieval date, effective date where known, currency, and type recorded per item** |
| **Synthetic terms** | Firm A's advisor-specific proposal only — **labelled synthetic**, created solely to test source asymmetry and normalization |

**Firm selection for research.** Two **publicly documented** firms in different channels — one large
employee-model firm and one regional or hybrid — **selected at the founder's direction at Checkpoint B**,
so the research targets are the founder's choice rather than mine.

**Initially available facts.** ~$680M AUM, ~$4.2M production, employee model, team of four. Decision has
already narrowed to two firms. Firm A has provided a written proposal; Firm B has had one informal
conversation and published materials only.

**Hidden facts, released on schedule.**

| Release point | Fact |
|---|---|
| **At L1 (Module 3)** | ~35% of revenue sits in a firm-proprietary program that will not transfer |
| **At consultation** | Firm A's proposal contains a retention condition the advisor had not registered |
| **At Appendix preparation** | Firm B's public materials describe a capability in terms that **cannot be normalized** against Firm A's documented figure — **the incomparability trigger** |
| **At authorization** | the firm asks for detailed revenue mix and a team document **that were not authorized** — the §14 scope-change test |

**Founder decisions required.** *(A)* scope · *(B)* **which two real firms to research**, and what
intelligence is needed · *(C)* the 3–5 matters · *(D)* comparison dimensions (4–7) and whether the
incomparability pattern applies · *(E)* whether the Appendix is issued and at what length.

**Gate.** Likely **T1** — the recommended option may pay materially more than a close alternative. Screen
all six plus the general screen.

**Expected artifacts.** Full set including **firm-level Appendix**, **Gate**, and **Authorization
(D/E/F)** — plus a **refused scope expansion** and an amended authorization version.

**Proposed advisor-package options.** *(a)* Record only · *(b)* Record + Appendix · *(c)* Record +
Appendix + due-diligence questions + authorization. **This is the case most likely to over-deliver** —
Checkpoint E should test that.

> **What would invalidate this case:** forcing a normalization that the evidence does not support, or
> letting Firm B look worse because less is documented about it.

# Case 4 · SUPPORTED INDEPENDENCE

**SIM-04** · *Capability-driven; a non-compensating alternative stays live; due diligence precedes any Record.*

**What this case tests.** **Due-diligence authorization before any Recommendation Record** — the direct
test of the founder's correction · **compensation separation** where a non-compensating option is
genuinely competitive · the Gate at the recommendation stage.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Synthetic** | advisor, book, capability requirements, the direct-RIA cost estimate |
| **REAL public intelligence** | **actual supported-independence platforms and custodians**, researched live: service model · custody · technology · transition support · ownership and economics structure · publicly stated capabilities |
| **Researched during the case** | the above, **plus** what a direct independent-RIA path publicly requires — registration, compliance, vendor structure |
| **Synthetic terms** | none from any platform — **this case runs on public information only**, which is itself part of the test |

**Platform selection for research.** Founder directs at Checkpoint B. At least one **supported-independence
platform** and the **direct-RIA path** as the non-compensating comparator.

**Initially available facts.** ~$520M AUM, ~$3.1M production, currently employee model. Wants ownership
and specific capabilities (lending, alternatives). Open to building independently but unsure about
operational load.

**Hidden facts, released on schedule.**

| Release point | Fact |
|---|---|
| **At L1 (Module 9)** | the advisor's tolerance for operational responsibility is **lower** than their ownership language suggests |
| **At DD inquiry** | the platform's public materials do not establish two of the advisor's must-have capabilities — **the reason DD authorization is needed** |
| **At consultation** | the advisor's spouse is a decision participant not previously named |
| **At recommendation** | the direct-RIA path is **stronger on ownership** but weaker on the advisor's stated operational tolerance |

**Founder decisions required.** *(A)* scope and whether ownership or capability is the real driver ·
*(B)* **which platforms to research**, and **authorizing anonymous or identified DD contact** · *(C)* the
3–5 matters · *(D)* whether the non-compensating direct path stays in scope and how it is treated ·
*(E)* the package.

**Gate.** **The DD authorization does not require the recommendation-stage Gate.** Once a directional
recommendation is prepared, **apply the full trigger screen** — likely **T1 and/or T2**. Where triggered,
**Gate and cooling apply before any later recruiting introduction, submission, or transition-focused
authorization.**

> **This is the case where the Gate should be stress-tested deliberately.** Run the recommendation with a
> genuine attempt to reach the answer VYNE would prefer, and see whether the counterfactuals catch it. **If
> they do not, that is a finding about the Gate, not about the case.**

**Expected artifacts.** Orientation · Entry · Understanding · **DD Authorization (A/B/C) — issued BEFORE
any Record** · L1 · Brief · Summary · Record · Appendix · **Gate at recommendation stage** · possibly a
second authorization.

**Proposed advisor-package options.** *(a)* Record + firm-intelligence brief · *(b)* + Appendix · *(c)*
Record + brief + due-diligence questions, Appendix withheld as excessive.

> **What would invalidate this case:** if the DD authorization is skipped and the platform intelligence is
> simply assumed, the case tests nothing that Case 3 does not already test.

# Case 5 · ADVISOR CHOOSES AGAINST THE RECOMMENDATION

**SIM-05** · *VYNE recommends one direction; the advisor legitimately chooses another.*

**What this case tests.** **The three separations** — recommendation, decision, authorization · **§12 tone
without grievance** · whether disagreement is recorded honestly rather than absorbed.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Synthetic** | advisor, book, both options, the advisor's weighting |
| **Real public intelligence** | the two models being weighed, at model level; one named firm if the founder directs |
| **Researched during the case** | model-level economics and ownership characteristics |
| **Synthetic terms** | the employee-firm offer's headline package — labelled synthetic |

**Initially available facts.** ~$450M AUM, ~$2.9M production. VYNE's analysis will support a
supported-independence path on capability and enterprise-value grounds. The advisor is drawn to an
employee-firm offer with a larger near-term package.

**Hidden facts, released on schedule.**

| Release point | Fact |
|---|---|
| **At consultation** | the advisor's spouse recently had a health event; near-term liquidity now weighs far more heavily than it did at Orientation |
| **At consultation** | the advisor **does not dispute** VYNE's capability analysis — they weight it differently |
| **At Record preparation** | the advisor decides for the employee firm |

> **The health event is deliberate.** It makes the advisor's weighting **entirely reasonable**, which is
> the point: the tone test only works if the advisor is obviously not making a mistake.

**Founder decisions required.** *(A)* scope · *(B)* materiality · *(C)* how to test a weighting difference
without arguing · *(D)* whether VYNE's recommendation changes given the new personal context — **or
whether it stands with the advisor deciding differently** · *(E)* the package, and specifically whether
§12 reads as neutral.

**Gate.** Per facts — screen all six. If the chosen option compensates and the recommended one does not,
**that differential alone is not a trigger** (9B), but T1/T2 should be assessed on their terms.

**Expected artifacts.** Full set · **Record with §3 recommendation, §4 advisor decision, §12 both
positions, §5 no authorization** · Gate as screened.

**Proposed advisor-package options.** *(a)* Record only · *(b)* Record + Summary. **Checkpoint E should
specifically ask: does §12 sound aggrieved?**

> **What would invalidate this case:** any language implying the advisor erred — *"despite our
> recommendation," "chose to disregard," "VYNE continues to believe."* If those appear, the case has
> failed and the finding is a **D4 tone defect**.
