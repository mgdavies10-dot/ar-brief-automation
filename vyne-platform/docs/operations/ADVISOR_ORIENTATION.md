## Welcome

Thank you for the time you're about to spend on this.

This Orientation is how a conversation with VYNE begins. Its purpose is narrow and specific: to
understand **what you're actually trying to decide**, and to determine whether we can do work that is
genuinely useful to you.

**A few things worth saying at the outset.**

**This is confidential.** What you share here is not shared with any firm, and your exploration does not
get back to your current firm through us.

**Please don't include any client information.** No client names, account numbers, statements, or
anything that identifies a specific client. We don't need it, and we won't keep it. Everything we need
about your business can be described in aggregate.

**Completing this does not authorize us to contact anyone.** No firm is ever contacted without your
explicit, written permission naming that specific firm. This document is not that permission, and
nothing here creates it.

**We may conclude that the best next step isn't a move.** Depending on what we learn, the right answer
may be to **stay**, to **wait**, to **gather more information first**, to **speak with a specialist**
(an attorney, for instance) — or to proceed with a full assessment. All of those are real answers, and
we'd rather give you the right one than the one that keeps a process moving.

**This is a starting point, not an application.** Answer what you can. "I don't know yet" and "I'd
rather discuss that live" are both perfectly good answers, and we'll note them as they stand.

## 6 · How much you'd like to share

*You control this, and you can change it at any point.*

We work at three levels of detail. **Most engagements start at Level 1**, and many never need to go
further.

| | What you share | What we can responsibly conclude |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1** *(default)* | the high-level picture — roughly what's above | orient the decision, identify what matters, frame your real options, and tell you what we'd need to go further |
| **Level 2** | structured, aggregated detail — revenue composition, segment and account structure, team structure | compare options seriously; give portability and economics as **ranges** |
| **Level 3** | detailed, advisor-specific review *(still no client PII)* | full advisor-specific analysis and a specific recommendation, where the evidence supports one |

**Your preference to start:** ☐ Level 1 ☐ Level 2 ☐ Level 3 ☐ Not sure — let's discuss

**Three commitments about this:**

- **We won't pressure you to share more.** If you'd rather stay at Level 1, we'll do the best work Level
  1 supports and tell you plainly what it can and can't settle.
- **Our conclusions can never outrun the evidence.** We won't give you a confident-sounding
  recommendation built on information we don't have. If a question needs Level 2 or 3 to answer
  honestly, we'll say that instead of guessing.
- **Moving to a deeper level is always your decision**, made when a specific question requires it — not
  because the process reached a later stage.

## 7 · How we work

**We evaluate legitimate alternatives — including staying.** We don't have a preferred destination, and
"stay" and "wait" are real conclusions we reach and recommend.

**No firm is contacted without your explicit, written, firm-specific permission.** Not a general
authorization — permission naming that firm. You decide what is shared and when.

**How we're paid.** VYNE is generally compensated by the hiring firm when an advisor or team
successfully joins that organization. **Advisors do not pay VYNE for traditional recruiting and
transition-consulting services.** Compensation arrangements may vary by firm and engagement, and **we
may not be compensated for every alternative we evaluate** — including staying, waiting, or building
your own firm.

We think you should know that, because it's a real fact about our business and you're entitled to weigh
it. What we do about it: compensation plays no part in how we assess whether an option fits you. It
appears in no comparison, no ranking, and no reason we give you. If we ever recommend something that
earns us nothing, that's the process working as intended.

**What you'll get.** If we proceed, you'll receive a written record of our conclusions — what we
concluded, why, how confident we are, what we don't know, and what would change our view. It's yours to
keep, re-read, and discuss with your spouse, attorney, or CPA.

## 9 · What happens next

We'll review this and come back to you — usually within a few business days — with one of the
following, and our reasoning for it:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Proceed** | we'll send a Working Agreement and begin the assessment |
| **Proceed with limited scope** | there's useful work to do, but narrower than the full picture — we'll say exactly what and why |
| **Let's gather a bit more first** | one or two specific things would materially change what we can conclude |
| **Wait** | there's a timing reason to revisit this later; we'll tell you what to watch for |
| **Specialist first** | a legal, tax, or other professional question needs answering before our work would be sound |
| **We're not the right fit** | the matter is outside our scope or capacity — we'll say so plainly and, where we can, point you somewhere better |

Whichever it is, you'll get the reasoning, not just the outcome.
