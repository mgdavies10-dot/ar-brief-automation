# Firm-Specific Authorization

**Artifact:** 10A · Operating Artifact Sprint *(final artifact)* · **Version:** v0.1 · **Date:** 2026-08-01
**Status:** DRAFT for founder review

> ## Governing premise
> **Firm-specific authorization is the only instrument through which the advisor authorizes VYNE to
> contact a named firm, disclose specified information, request specified action, or facilitate a
> defined interaction.**
>
> **Authorization is firm-specific, purpose-specific, information-specific, recipient-limited,
> time-limited, version-specific, and revocable. Nothing is implied.**

> **A Recommendation & Decision Record is not required for every authorization.** VYNE may need limited
> authorization to obtain firm-specific information **during due diligence, before a directional
> recommendation can responsibly be completed.** *(§4 sets the basis for each type.)*

**Ordinarily one page.** No advisor names in filenames.

## §3 · Purpose — select every applicable purpose separately

- ☐ **A · Anonymous due-diligence inquiry.** VYNE may contact the named firm **without identifying you**
  and request general or program-level information. *This does not indicate that VYNE recommends the
  firm.*
- ☐ **B · Identified due-diligence inquiry.** VYNE may disclose your identity and request specified
  information. *This does not by itself indicate a final recommendation or a decision by you.*
- ☐ **C · Introduction or meeting request.** VYNE may identify you and request or arrange a meeting.
- ☐ **D · Submission or request for advisor-specific terms.** VYNE may provide the specifically
  authorized profile information and request terms specific to you.
- ☐ **E · Term clarification and advocacy.** VYNE may communicate your questions and preferences,
  request clarification, request modified or improved terms, and convey positions you have approved.
- ☐ **F · Other, narrowly described:** ______________________________________

> **Authorization to communicate, facilitate, or advocate does not permit VYNE to accept or reject
> terms, execute documents, bind you, or make any legal commitment on your behalf.**

**Nothing not selected is authorized.**

## §7 · Client information — never authorized

> ### **Client personally identifiable information is never authorized through this instrument.**

**Prohibited without exception:** client names · account numbers · Social Security numbers · dates of
birth · home addresses · tax identifiers · account-level holdings · account statements · transaction
history · identifiable client financial information · **any information that could reasonably identify
an individual client.**

> **This prohibition cannot be overridden by a write-in field, and it cannot be waived by the advisor.**

If client information reaches VYNE, it is handled under the Engagement Record Standards and is **not
retained or transmitted through this process.**

*Authorization permits disclosure. It does not require VYNE to disclose everything permitted.*

## §10 · What is not authorized

**Unless separately and expressly selected above, this instrument does not permit:**

contact with another firm · disclosure to an unnamed affiliate · disclosure beyond the selected
recipient scope · use for a new or unrelated purpose · reuse in a later approach · disclosure after
expiration or revocation · public disclosure · **client PII** · implementation or transition activity ·
account transfer activity.

**And in no case — regardless of what is selected — may VYNE:**

> **accept or reject firm terms · execute an agreement · bind you · or make any legal commitment on your
> behalf.**

*Note: where §3E and the corresponding §5 permissions are selected, VYNE **may** communicate your
preferences, ask questions, seek clarification, and request improved or revised terms. Advocacy within
scope is permitted; **agency is not.***

## §12 · How VYNE is paid

> VYNE is generally compensated by the hiring firm when an advisor or team successfully joins that
> organization. Advisors do not pay VYNE for traditional recruiting and transition-consulting services.
> Compensation arrangements may vary by firm and engagement, and we may not be compensated for every
> alternative we evaluate — including staying, waiting, or building your own firm.

> **Signing this authorization does not cause you to owe VYNE a fee.**

## §13 · Duration and expiration

```
 EXPIRES: ________________      ☐ 60 days (default)  ☐ other: ______
```

> **No indefinite or open-ended authorization.** Provisional launch default: **60 calendar days from
> acceptance.** A shorter or longer period may be selected based on purpose; a period materially longer
> than 60 days has its reason recorded internally.

**At expiration:** VYNE stops new contact, disclosure, requests, and follow-up. **Previously completed
contacts and disclosures cannot be undone.** Further action requires renewal or a new authorization.

*The 60-day default is an operating pilot standard, not permanent doctrine — reviewed after five
simulated cases.*

## §14 · Change in scope

**A new or amended authorization is required before:** adding another firm or affiliate · changing from
anonymous to identified contact · adding a new information category · sharing a new document · expanding
the recipient group · changing the purpose · **moving from due diligence to submission** · beginning term
advocacy not previously authorized · continuing after expiration · taking any action after revocation.

> **A material scope expansion may not be written informally into an email or internal note. You must
> acknowledge the specific new version.**

## §15 · Revocation

> **You may revoke this authorization at any time through any clear written or verbal instruction. VYNE
> will confirm the revocation in writing.**

- **Revocation is effective when received.** VYNE stops future contact, disclosure, requests, and
  follow-up; unused authority ends immediately.
- **Completed introductions and information already disclosed cannot be recalled or undone.** Where
  practical, VYNE may ask the recipient not to use or further distribute the information, **but cannot
  guarantee compliance.**
- **Withdrawal from the VYNE engagement automatically revokes every unused authorization** unless you
  expressly reconfirm it.
- **A revoked authorization cannot be revived informally** — a new version is required.

## §16 · No pressure

> **No authorization should result from pressure to act immediately.**

- VYNE will not press you for same-day authorization.
- **Where a Higher-Conflict Decision Gate was triggered, at least one full business day passes after
  Gate completion before VYNE requests authorization — and that requirement applies even if you express
  urgency.**
- Where no triggered-Gate cooling applies, you may independently request immediate action; **this
  instrument is still completed**, and the internal record states that the request was advisor-initiated.
- **VYNE will not suggest advisor initiation as a way around the cooling rule.**
- **This authorization does not create urgency or a deadline.**
