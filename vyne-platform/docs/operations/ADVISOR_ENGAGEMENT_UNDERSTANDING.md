## 1 · What this document is

This document explains how the VYNE engagement works and what each party should expect. It is written in
plain English and is subject to the formal terms governing the engagement. Counsel will determine
whether it operates as, accompanies, or is incorporated into the engagement agreement.

We wrote it because the most important things about working with us are not obvious from a website, and
you should not have to discover them as you go.

## 2 · The decision relationship

You are making a consequential decision about your business. Our role is to help you make it well —
not to move you toward a particular outcome.

That means the work is oriented around your decision, not around a transaction. We measure ourselves by
whether your decision improved, and a conclusion that you should stay where you are, or wait, is a
complete and successful piece of work.

## 3 · What you should expect from VYNE

- **A structured process, at a pace set by your decision.** Orientation, then an assessment of your
  practice, then a consultation where we test our thinking with you, then a written record of our
  conclusions.
- **Our reasoning, not just our conclusions.** You will always be able to see how we got there.
- **Honesty about what we don't know.** We will tell you where we are uncertain, what is missing, and
  what would change our view.
- **A written record you keep.** What we concluded, why, how confident we are, what we don't know, and
  what would change it.
- **No pressure.** Not toward a firm, not toward a decision, and not toward a timeline.

## 4 · What VYNE will do

- Work to understand your practice, your objectives, and your constraints.
- Evaluate the legitimate alternatives available to you — including staying where you are.
- Distinguish what is verified, what a firm claims, what we have assumed, and what is our judgment.
- State our confidence, the unknowns, and the alternatives we considered and set aside.
- Give you **the strongest conclusion the evidence supports** — no stronger, and no weaker.
- Identify where a question needs a lawyer, accountant, or other specialist, and say so early.

## 5 · What VYNE will not do

- We do not practise law, provide tax or accounting advice, issue formal valuations, act as your
  compliance officer, or prepare regulatory filings.
- We do not perform the mechanics of a transition — repapering, account transfers, systems setup, or
  office build-out.
- We do not contact any firm about you without your specific written permission.
- We do not claim to have reviewed every firm or option in the market.
- We do not describe ourselves as unbiased, neutral, or free of conflicts. We are compensated in a
  particular way, we tell you how, and we manage it — which is a different and more honest claim.
- We do not tell you that any firm is "the best." We may tell you which option best fits *your*
  practice on the evidence we have.

## 6 · What VYNE asks from you

Our conclusions are only as good as what we are working from, so we ask that you:

- Provide information that is accurate to the best of your knowledge.
- Correct us when we have misunderstood something material — this is genuinely useful, not an
  inconvenience.
- Tell us when facts, objectives, deadlines, or circumstances change.
- Let us know about prior contact with any firm or recruiter where it is relevant.
- **Do not provide client-identifying information.**
- Involve the other people who are part of this decision when the time is appropriate.
- Obtain specialist advice where a question requires it.
- **Make the final decision.** It is yours, and we will not make it for you.
- Provide separate written authorization before we contact any firm.

## 7 · Confidentiality and information handling

- Information you share in this engagement is restricted to **authorized VYNE personnel and approved
  providers or professional advisers with a legitimate need to know.**
- **Nothing is shared with a hiring firm without your explicit, firm-specific authorization.**
- Where disclosure is legally required, it is handled under the direction of counsel.
- **We do not request or retain client-identifying information.**
- Your identity and the information you provide are distinct from client-identifying information, and
  are handled under this engagement's confidentiality and privacy controls.

## 8 · No client PII

We do not want, ask for, or keep client names, account numbers, statements, addresses, dates of birth,
or anything else that identifies one of your clients. Everything we need about your business can be
described in aggregate — segments, structure, concentration, and revenue composition.

If client information reaches us inadvertently, we will tell you and we will not retain it.

## 9 · How recommendations are formed

Every conclusion we give you rests on an explicit basis:

- **What is verified**, and where it came from.
- **What a firm has claimed**, attributed to that firm and dated — never presented as established fact.
- **What we have assumed**, stated as an assumption, with what would confirm or refute it.
- **What is our judgment**, labelled as judgment.
- **What we don't know**, and how it bears on the decision.
- **The alternatives we considered**, including the ones we set aside and why.
- **How confident we are**, and what would change it.

We test all of this with you before it becomes a recommendation. **A conclusion is not final because we
wrote it down** — it becomes recommendation-ready only after we have worked through it with you.

## 10 · You control every introduction

No firm is contacted about you without your **explicit, written, firm-specific authorization** — a
separate document naming that firm and the information to be shared, signed each time.

A general willingness to explore is not authorization. Agreeing with a recommendation is not
authorization. Authorization for one firm does not extend to another. And we will not ask you to
authorize an introduction in the same conversation in which we first recommend it.

## 11 · Legitimate outcomes

All of these are real conclusions we reach and recommend:

**stay · wait · renegotiate where you are · move to another firm · launch your own firm · join an
existing firm · merge · acquire · sell · plan a succession · retire or wind down.**

**Several of these pay us nothing.** We say so plainly here because you should be able to hold us to it.

## 12 · How VYNE is compensated

VYNE is generally compensated by the hiring firm when an advisor or team successfully joins that
organization. **Advisors do not pay VYNE for traditional recruiting and transition-consulting services.**
Compensation arrangements may vary by firm and engagement, and **we may not be compensated for every
alternative we evaluate** — including staying, waiting, or building your own firm.

What we do about that: compensation plays no part in how we assess whether an option fits you. It
appears in no comparison, no ranking, and no reason we give you. Where the situation creates a heightened
risk of that being questioned, we document internally why our conclusion stands independent of how we
are paid, before the recommendation is issued.

## 13 · Graduated disclosure

You decide how much to share, and you can change that at any point.

| | What you share | What we can responsibly conclude |
|---|---|---|
| **Level 1** *(where we start)* | the high-level picture | orient the decision, identify what matters, frame your options, and tell you what we'd need to go further |
| **Level 2** | structured, aggregated detail | compare options seriously; portability and economics as **ranges** |
| **Level 3** | detailed, advisor-specific review *(still no client PII)* | full advisor-specific analysis and a specific recommendation, where the evidence supports one |

**We start at Level 1** and go deeper only when a specific question requires it **and you choose to.**
Our conclusions can never outrun the information available — we will tell you what a level can and
cannot settle rather than guessing past it.

## 14 · Conclusions, changes, and uncertainty

Our conclusions describe our judgment **at a point in time**, based on the information identified in the
record we give you. They remain subject to material new information or changed circumstances.

Recommendations should **evolve as evidence develops.** If our direction changes materially, we will
identify the new or newly verified information that caused it and explain it clearly. What we will not do
is quietly substitute a different conclusion.

## 15 · Professional boundaries and specialists

Some questions must be answered by a licensed professional: your actual contract and restrictive
covenants; the tax treatment of your transaction; formal valuation; registration and compliance
obligations; transaction documents; and the operational work of a transition.

We identify these dependencies, explain what they affect, and may help you find appropriate specialists.
**Those specialists remain responsible for their own advice and work.** We will not build a conclusion
on a guess about a question that belongs to them.

## 16 · Advisor-facing Records and permitted use

The written record of our conclusions is **yours to keep, re-read, and use in your decision.**

You may share it confidentially with your **spouse or partner, attorney, CPA or tax adviser, relevant
team members, and other named professional advisers.**

VYNE retains ownership of its methodology, frameworks, templates, analytical structure, and internal
records. The Record is not to be publicly posted, reproduced commercially, distributed broadly, or
**sent to a hiring firm** without our written approval. Where information genuinely needs to reach a
firm, we will prepare an approved excerpt or a separate firm-facing summary rather than sending the full
Record.

## 17 · Pausing, withdrawal, and conclusion

**You may pause or withdraw at any time**, for any reason, without explanation.

**We may pause, narrow, decline, or conclude an engagement** where: the evidence is insufficient to
support useful work; information proves unreliable; a professional boundary requires a specialist first;
we do not have the capacity to do the work properly; or the matter falls outside our scope.

If an engagement pauses or ends, we will **identify any firm authorization that is still outstanding**
and confirm its status — normally, no authorization remains active. Where useful work has been done, we
may issue a short **Decision Status and Closure Record** setting out what we could and could not
conclude, what remains unknown, and what circumstances should cause you to revisit the decision. Ending
without a decision is a legitimate outcome and does not erase the work completed.

Retention of records remains governed by our approved retention policy (§18).

## 18 · Records and retention

We keep a record of the engagement — what we were told, what we concluded, and how. Advisor-facing
Records and our internal working records are retained and destroyed under a **written retention policy**.
Legal holds, active disputes, contractual obligations, and applicable law take precedence over ordinary
destruction schedules.
