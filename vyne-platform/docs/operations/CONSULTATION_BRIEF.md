> **The consultant prepares with more structure than the advisor should ever feel.**

## Using the brief

- **It is a prompt sheet, not an agenda to walk through.** The advisor experiences **Listen · Reflect ·
  Test · Decide · Commit**; the brief sits beside the consultant, not between them.
- **Sections 5 and 6 are capped deliberately.** If more than five things must be tested, the session is
  overloaded — split it, or narrow the purpose in §2.
- **Section 8's three categories are honest by design.** "Provisionally set aside" with a return
  condition is very different from "rejected," and the difference matters if the advisor challenges it.
- **Section 13 is completed before the session, not after.** It is a preparation control, not a report.
- **Depth belongs in issue sheets (5B)**, prepared only for matters that genuinely need them.
