# M4 Product Plan — the VYNE Method, made real

**Status:** PROPOSED (founder product review) · **Date:** 2026-07-21
**Frame:** M4 is a *product* milestone. Every feature is justified by advisor
value first, engineering second, and each carries the six product sections
(Advisor Problem · Why It Matters · Alternatives Considered · Advisor Experience
· Business Value · Founder Acceptance Criteria) before implementation.

## What M4 is really building

Not "the OS core flow." M4 makes the **decision spine of the VYNE Method**
physical: a place where an advisor's situation is captured with more clarity than
they've ever seen it, where readiness for a consequential decision is built
deliberately instead of rushed, and where that clarity becomes a premium
deliverable. Founder-first (M4-A), then correctly scoped for recruiters (M4-B).

The through-line an advisor eventually feels: *"They understood my practice
better than I did, and they didn't push me — they made the decision clearer."*

## Feature sequence (authorized M4 spine)

Each is proposed with the full six sections before it's built; the founder
reviews the proposal and the built result at the gate.

1. **F1 — Advisor Workspace + Current Reality authoring** *(proposal: this
   milestone; see `M4-F1_current_reality.md`).* The center of the product: one
   permissioned picture of a relationship, and the structured "Reality" surface
   where an advisor's practice is captured once and assembled later.
2. **F2 — Decision Workspace + readiness.** The Method's cockpit: the decision
   question in plain language, a readiness scorecard that *gates* the
   recommendation (premature action has no affordance, not a warning), an
   append-only decision log.
3. **F3 — Artifact Builder + lifecycle + cooling rule.** Turn captured reality
   into a premium Current Reality Record; carry it Draft → In review → Approved
   with the cooling rule as felt product behavior. (Publishing to the advisor is
   M5.)
4. **F4 — Synthetic seed + recruiter scoping + cooling-rule policy (M4-B).** A
   demonstration data set that tells one compelling advisor story end-to-end, the
   same surfaces correctly narrowed for a recruiter, and the configurable cooling
   rule.

The synthetic seed is designed *last-but-informed-first*: the personas exist to
make F1–F3 tell a real transition story, so their design follows the feature
proposals rather than preceding them.

## How M4 honors the product principles

- **Increase advisor confidence / reduce transition uncertainty:** Current
  Reality and readiness turn a vague, anxious decision into a structured, visible
  one.
- **Surface insights advisors can't easily get elsewhere:** the structured
  Reality + readiness scorecard is a lens no CRM or competitor gives them.
- **Create trust before asking for a consultation:** the deliverable does the
  persuading by being genuinely useful; VYNE never pushes.
- **Premium / differentiated:** the Method made physical — the interface enforces
  discipline (cooling rule, readiness-gated recommendation) that a spreadsheet or
  a generic CRM cannot.

## Quality bar (M4 onward)

Assume any surface could be demonstrated to a $100M–$1B advisory team:
enterprise-grade correctness and calm, so simple an advisor never sees the
machinery. When choosing between another feature and making an existing workflow
dramatically better — improve the workflow. Clarity over complexity; decision
support over information overload; trust over persuasion.
