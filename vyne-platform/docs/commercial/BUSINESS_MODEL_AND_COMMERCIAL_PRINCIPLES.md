## Why this is a separate document

The Constitution is **compensation-independent by design.** It must remain correct whether VYNE is
paid by hiring firms, advisor retainers, enterprise or succession consulting, M&A advisory,
subscription research, licensing, executive search, or family-office work. Putting a revenue model
into the enduring documents would date them. This is where the commercial mechanics belong.

## 1 · The core operating model (founder-established, 2026-07-25)

> **VYNE's core recruiting model is hiring-firm-paid and success-based:** advisors do not pay VYNE
> for traditional recruiting and transition-consulting services; the **hiring firm** pays a
> success-based fee (a percentage of the advisor's verified production) only upon a successful
> placement. *(The specific fee range is confidential — see §10, Founder-Restricted.)*

The advisor generally does **not** pay VYNE for the traditional recruiting engagement. The intended
process:

1. **Confidential discovery.** VYNE works confidentially with the advisor to understand the advisor,
   team, practice, objectives, clients, book, economics, concerns, and possible paths.
2. **Evaluation.** VYNE helps identify and evaluate firms, channels, structures, and other legitimate
   alternatives.
3. **No submission without authorization.** No advisor is submitted to a firm without the advisor's
   knowledge and explicit authorization.
4. **Authorized submission.** When the advisor authorizes an introduction, VYNE submits the advisor
   or team to one or more selected firms.
5. **Success-based fee.** If the advisor chooses a firm, joins it, and the fee conditions are
   satisfied, the **hiring firm** pays VYNE a recruiting / placement / transition-assistance (or
   similar) success-based fee.
6. **Fee.** A success-based fee — a percentage of the advisor's **verified trailing-twelve-month
   production / revenue** (T12 / TTMP). *(Specific range is confidential — see §10.)*
7. **Variability.** Fees may vary by firm, agreement, advisor profile, production level, placement
   structure, calendar-year threshold, or other contractual terms.
8. **No-fee outcomes.** If the advisor stays, waits, renegotiates internally, chooses a non-paying
   destination, or pursues a path that produces no compensable placement, VYNE may receive **no fee**
   under the core model.

## 2 · Compensation & incentive analysis (structural facts)

These incentives **do not prove biased advice or misconduct.** They are structural facts that
require governance, disclosure, and methodological controls.

1. **Outcome incentive.** VYNE generally earns a fee if the advisor completes a compensable move, and
   may earn nothing if the advisor stays, waits, restructures internally, or selects a non-paying
   path.
2. **Destination incentive.** VYNE's fee may differ among firms — a higher-paying firm can produce
   materially more revenue for VYNE than a lower-paying one for the same placement.
3. **Contracted-market incentive.** VYNE may have economic relationships with some firms and not
   others.
4. **Timing incentive.** A success-based fee may create pressure — actual or perceived — to
   accelerate a decision or move.
5. **Production incentive.** Larger-T12 advisors may generate larger fees, potentially creating
   different commercial attention across advisor segments.

**Governing principle:** **VYNE should *manage and disclose* conflicts, not claim they do not exist.**

## 3 · Prohibited claims

VYNE must **not** be described as: conflict-free · compensation-neutral · inherently unbiased · paid
regardless of the recommendation · representing the complete market under contract · or having
identical economics across all destinations. VYNE may **aspire to impartial judgment**, but its
credibility must come from **transparent conflict management and demonstrated method** — not from
denying that economic incentives exist.

- **Do not say:** "VYNE has no conflicts."
- **Strategic principle (record only — not public copy yet):** *"VYNE uses a transparent, documented
  process designed to manage real-world incentives and support the strongest conclusion the evidence
  allows."*

## 4 · The trust model (compensation is one component, not the differentiator)

Trust is built from the **whole set**, not any single claim:

transparent compensation · explicit conflict disclosure · **Advisor First** · **No Preferred
Destination** · open destination universe · **current firm a valid alternative** · **waiting a valid
alternative** · advisor-controlled submissions · evidence provenance · separation of fact /
assumption / firm claim / VYNE judgment · documented unknowns · alternatives · confidence levels ·
calibrated conclusions · recommendation rationale · **willingness to recommend a result that produces
no fee** · clear professional boundaries.

This aligns with the moat framing: the differentiator is **how VYNE reaches conclusions** — the
combination no one else fields — *supported by* transparent conflict management, not replaced by it.

## 5 · Incentive-Alignment principle (load-bearing, retained)

VYNE's commercial model must **reinforce, not undermine,** its constitutional principles. Regardless
of how VYNE is compensated, its incentives must encourage objective advice, intellectual honesty, and
recommendations made in the advisor's best interest. **When commercial incentives and advisor
outcomes conflict, the advisor's long-term interests take precedence.**

### Approved public / advisor-facing disclosure language (the ONLY compensation language that may leave this doc)

No percentages or dollar amounts appear in any advisor-facing or public use.

- **Standard sentence:** *"VYNE is compensated by the hiring firm when an advisor or team
  successfully joins that organization. Advisors do not pay VYNE for traditional recruiting and
  transition-consulting services."*
- **Fuller disclosure (e.g., a Compensation Disclosure page):** *"VYNE Strategies is generally
  compensated by hiring firms under private recruiting or placement agreements when an advisor or team
  successfully joins a participating firm. Compensation arrangements may vary by firm and engagement.
  Advisors are not charged for VYNE's traditional recruiting and transition-consulting services. VYNE
  manages potential conflicts through advisor-controlled introductions, disclosure of its role, and a
  documented process for evaluating relevant alternatives."*

**Meaningful conflict disclosure to an advisor consists of:** VYNE is paid by the hiring firm ·
compensation is **success-based** (contingent on a completed placement) · arrangements **may vary** by
firm or engagement · VYNE **may not be compensated for every alternative** · **the advisor controls
every submission and introduction.** *(Exact percentages, amounts, step-ups, payment timing, and
firm-specific terms are never disclosed — see §10.)*

## 7 · Advisor Submission Standard (working draft — legal/compliance review required)

- No submission without **express advisor authorization.**
- Authorization must identify the **specific firm(s).**
- **No mass distribution** of advisor identities.
- Advisor **controls disclosure level**; initial conversations may use redacted/high-level info where
  practical; **no client PII.**
- **Submission date and scope documented.**
- Information provided must be **accurate and advisor-approved.**
- VYNE **does not imply exclusivity** unless it exists in writing.
- VYNE documents whether the advisor **or another recruiter** previously contacted the firm.
- VYNE does **not** claim lead ownership merely because a name exists in a database.
- Destination contact, information sharing, and next steps are **auditable.**

## 8 · Method controls (minimum credible for launch; mature state later)

*Controls that demonstrate compensation is not driving the conclusion. Do not over-engineer.*

1. **Compensation-blind initial assessment.** Complete objectives, current-practice scan, risks, and
   possible model categories **before** firm-specific compensation is presented to the consultant
   making the initial recommendation.
2. **Fit scoring separated from VYNE revenue.** VYNE's expected fee is **never** a fit criterion or
   assessment variable.
3. **Open-market notation.** For every relevant option, record whether it is compensating ·
   potentially compensating · non-compensating · compensation-unknown. **Do not auto-remove
   non-compensating options.**
4. **Recommendation rationale.** Every conclusion documents: why it fits · material drawbacks ·
   assumptions · unknowns · alternatives considered · what could change the conclusion.
5. **Higher-conflict review** (extra founder review) when: the recommended firm pays materially more
   than a close alternative · a non-paying alternative looks substantively stronger · the advisor is
   considering **staying** · the advisor is considering **launching an RIA** · VYNE establishes a fee
   agreement **after** analysis began · VYNE has another financial relationship with a provider.
6. **Compensation disclosure record.** Document what the advisor was told, and when.
7. **No unsupported "best firm" language.** VYNE may identify the strongest fit the evidence supports;
   it does **not** describe a firm as universally best.

**Confidentiality rule (governing):** *VYNE's fee percentages, fee amounts, payment schedules,

advisor profile, production level, placement structure, calendar-year threshold, and other contractual
terms.

**Retained internal uses** (why the range lives here): financial modeling · firm-contract strategy ·
recruiter compensation · revenue forecasting · incentive-governance analysis · founder and counsel
review.
